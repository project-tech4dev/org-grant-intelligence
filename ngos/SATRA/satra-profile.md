# SATRA (Social Action For Appropriate Transformation And Advancement in Rural Areas)

- Website: https://www.satra.ngo/
- Founded: 2002, Sipajhar, Darrang district, Assam, India
- Headquarters: Satgharia, Trinarayanpur, Sipajhar, Darrang, Mangaldai - 784145, Assam, India
- Motto: "People For Development, Development For People"

## Vision
To establish a progressive, peaceful, and developed society based on the values of equity,
justice, trust, love, honesty, dignity, and mutual help.

## Mission
To organize and empower the rural poor to promote development as a liberating force for
achieving social justice, economic growth, and self-reliance.

## Thematic / Focus Areas
Livelihood, Youth Development, Women Empowerment, Environment, Mental Health,
Child Protection, Disaster Management, Education, Access to Justice.
