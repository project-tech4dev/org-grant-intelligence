# SATRA (Social Action For Appropriate Transformation And Advancement in Rural Areas) — Organization Profile

> This document is updated continuously as new information is found. See `SATRA_Programs.md` for detailed program-area information. **SATRA's website hosts a genuinely deep, largely-functional document archive — Annual Reports spanning FY2008-09 through FY2024-25 (17 years) — several of which have real, extractable text**, making this (alongside PESCH) one of the best-documented organizations researched in this project.

## Organization Overview

- **Full name:** Social Action For Appropriate Transformation And Advancement in Rural Areas (SATRA)
- **Founded:** **27 February 2002** — Mrs. **Jyoti Prava Bora** invited social activists from across Assam to her residence in Sipajhar to form a new development organization. **Dr. Dinesh Baishya** (then-principal of B. Borooah College, Guwahati) is credited with naming the organization "SATRA."
- **Motto:** "People For Development, Development For People"
- **Headquarters:** Satgharia (Trinarayanpur), Sipajhar, Darrang District, Mangaldai – 784145, Assam, India
- **Leadership succession:**
  - **Mrs. Jyoti Prava Bora** — Founding Chief Functionary (2002); stepped down in 2003 to join a government job.
  - **Mr. Nani Kumar Saikia** — elected **Executive Director in January 2004**, and remains ED as of the most recent (FY2023-24) annual report — over 20 years of continuous leadership. Described as receiving remuneration as the only board member paid, "towards his full-time involvement as Director."
  - **Dr. Prasanna Kumar Nath** — current **Chairperson** (per FY2023-24 report).
- **Early volunteer team (pre-funding era, worked ~2 years unpaid):** Biplab Saharia, Sanjib Sarma, Debeswar Medhi, Jagadish Nath, Moni Bora, Sumitra Barman, and others.
- **First major institutional funder:** **RGVN (Rashtriya Grameen Vikas Nidhi)**, which enabled SATRA's move into microfinance/economic development — described as the organization's "breakthrough."
- **Board members (named, per website "Executive members" and FY2023-24 report):** Dr. Prasanna Nath (Chairperson), Mr. Kamal Bhatta, Santanu Saikia, Dr. Dinesh Baishya (the same person who named the organization in 2002, still on the board decades later), Dr. Bazrul Islam, and others.

- **Staffing (FY2023-24, from the Annual Report's own HR tables):**
  - **Total staff: 85** — 8 permanent (6 male, 2 female), 76 contractual (49 male, 27 female), 1 part-time (audit).
  - By salary band: 11 staff earn up to ₹10,000/month; 64 staff earn ₹10,001-25,000/month; 7 staff earn ₹25,001-50,000/month; 3 staff earn above ₹50,000/month.
  - **312 volunteers** across various projects.
  - No board member remuneration in FY2023-24 except Nani Kumar Saikia (ED).
  - Board met 6 times in FY2023-24 (29 Apr, 2 Jul, 10 Sep, 24 Dec 2023, 11 Feb, 24 Mar 2024), attendance ranging 8-11 members; Annual General Meeting held 23 Oct 2023.
  - **SATRA Raising (Founding) Day: 27 February** — celebrated in 2024 with a blood donation drive (42 volunteer donors, Mangaldai Civil Hospital blood bank).
- **Network memberships (FY2023-24):** VANI (Voluntary Action Network India), New Delhi; IAG (Inter-Agency Group), Assam; National RCRC and NE-RCRC; White Ribbon Alliance, Assam Chapter — confirming SATRA is part of the same overlapping Northeast India NGO ecosystem (RCRC, White Ribbon Alliance) already identified in this project's research on Diya Foundation and MOSONiE.

## Reach (note a discrepancy between two of SATRA's own sources)

- **Per the live website homepage (undated):** 6 Districts, 482 Villages, 916,848 Individuals, 96 Youth Clubs.
- **Per the FY2023-24 Annual Report's own "Our Reach" page:** **3** Districts, 482 Villages, 916,848 Individuals, 96 Youth Clubs — i.e., the villages/individuals/youth-club figures match exactly, but the **district count differs (3 vs. 6)**. This is likely because the website's team-page structure lists **6 district/regional teams** (Udalguri, Arunachal, Darrang, Sonitpur, West Karbi Anglong, Kokrajhar) reflecting the *full range* of places SATRA has ever worked, while the "3 Districts" figure in the FY2023-24 report may reflect only the districts with *active* programming in that specific year. Not yet confirmed with certainty — flagged as an open question.

## Vision, Mission, Objectives (unchanged across all sources reviewed)

- **Vision:** "To establish a progressive, peaceful, and developed society based on the values of equity, justice, trust, love, honesty, dignity, and mutual help."
- **Mission:** "To organize and empower the rural poor to promote development as a liberating force for achieving social justice, economic growth, and self-reliance."
- **Objective:** "To create a peaceful society for all, free from prejudice of caste, creed, color, and religion, where all people can live harmoniously."

**Government/regulatory bodies acknowledged:** Registrar of Societies, Assam; FCRA Division, Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India; Income Tax Department; NITI Aayog; Social Welfare Department of Assam; District Administration, Darrang; Circle Officer, Sipajhar Revenue Circle.

**Funding/technical partners acknowledged (a very long list, confirming SATRA has diversified funding across two decades):** RGVN, NYK (Nehru Yuva Kendra), NABARD, CAPART, Central Silk Board, KVK (Krishi Vigyan Kendra), NEDFi, SIDBI, VHAA, VHAI, **Oxfam India**, IGSSS, ICCO, **Tata Trust**, SDTT (Sir Dorabji Tata Trust), Sishu Sarothi, Ashadeep, **Terre des Hommes (TDH) Germany**, **TDH Switzerland**, Give Asia, ActionAid India, **Azim Premji Foundation / Azim Premji Philanthropic Initiatives**, **Bread for the World**, **KSCF (Korean Sunlight Charity Foundation, or similar — "KSCF US")**, **North East Nutrient Pvt Ltd**, **Dasra** (Rebuild India Fund), **Wipro**, Ashra Hastra Trust, and **EDGH Luxembourg** (women/youth livelihoods), and **Misereor** (Conflict Management & Peace Education, in association with TDH Germany). Government line departments in agriculture, veterinary science, sericulture, and handloom textiles are also credited as ongoing collaborators.

*(Cross-reference note: Azim Premji Foundation/Philanthropic Initiatives, Oxfam India, Tata Trust/SDTT, Dasra, and TDH/Misereor recur as funders across multiple Northeast India NGOs researched in this project — reinforcing the earlier finding of a small, overlapping pool of national/international funders supporting the region's grassroots NGO sector.)*

## Geographic Team Structure (per website navigation)

SATRA organizes its field staff into district/regional teams for: **Udalguri, Arunachal (Pradesh), Darrang, Sonitpur, West Karbi Anglong, and Kokrajhar** — six distinct geographic teams, plus thematic teams for **Conflict Management and Peace Education, Women Empowerment, and Youth Development**.
