## 2024-25 — "Explorer Eyes" (Expansion into Arunachal Pradesh)
Reach grew to **7 districts** (Darrang, Udalguri, Sonitpur, Kokrajhar, West Karbi
Anglong in Assam, plus Papum Pare in Arunachal Pradesh), **712 villages**,
**~924,673 individuals**, 96 youth clubs, 600+ handloom weavers, with a team of
72 full-time staff. The EDGH/tdh Germany women & youth livelihood project shows
strong outcomes: ~240 of 300 targeted women (80%) skilled in Eri silk rearing/weaving,
50 trained as Master Trainers, and ~60% reporting a 20–25% income increase; the area
is now recognized as an Eri silk rearing zone. Other sections cover Community Mental
Health, Child Rights, Peace Education, Environment & Climate Change, the Rebuild
India–Dasra capacity-building partnership, the Chimple digital-learning project, and
forward plans on climate adaptation and gender equality.

## 2023-24 — "Explorer Eyes" (3 Districts, 482 Villages, 916,848 Individuals)
Reach: 3 districts, 482 villages, ~917,000 individuals, 96 youth clubs, 74 full-time
staff. Community Mental Health Programme (Azim Premji Foundation) registered 2,879
patients (688 new, 414 recovered) via 24 camps, 15,120 follow-up visits, 674
counseling sessions, and introduced 16 trained "suicide gate-keeper" volunteers.
Women & Youth Empowerment (EDGH/tdh) engaged 250–300 women in Eri rearing/weaving
and 150 youth in vermicomposting/beekeeping. A Livelihood project (Bread for the
World) formed two people's organizations and trained 130 youth/farmers in organic
farming. Conflict Management & Peace Education (Misereor/tdh Germany) ran in the
Bodoland Territorial Council area with 10 village action plans and youth centers.
Includes an audit report section.

## 2022-23 — Expanding Reach (4 Districts, 370 Villages, ~20,000 Families)
Operating in Darrang, Udalguri, Sonitpur, and Baksa districts with 70 full-time
staff. Community Mental Health Programme registered 2,191 patients (457 new) via 24
screening camps, 18,432 follow-up visits, and 163 counseling sessions. Launched the
Women & Youth Empowerment project (EDGH Luxembourg/tdh Germany) in 8 Char-area
villages targeting 300 women and 150 youth in Eri silk and eco-livelihoods, plus an
Environmental Sustainability program on regenerative agriculture and seed
preservation. Marked SATRA's 20th anniversary (blood donation drive) and launched a
labor-registration/social-security initiative for unregistered workers post-COVID.

## 2021-22 — COVID Recovery & Community Response
Dominant theme was COVID-19 recovery: reached ~20,000 families in Darrang & Udalguri
with dry ration, cash transfers and livelihood support, and administered ~60,000
vaccine doses in remote Adivasi/Bodo villages (with Azim Premji Foundation/NHM). The
Community Mental Health & Disability Program covered 80 villages, 1,857 patients
enrolled, 1,100 caregivers trained, and 455 people assisted with disability
certificates. The Strengthening Community Action project (Bread for the World)
delivered livelihood training (banana cultivation, vermicompost, beekeeping) and
gender-mainstreaming workshops. Darrang CHILDLINE handled 210 cases, over half being
child-marriage interventions.

## 2020-21 — COVID-19 Pandemic Response
The year was dominated by COVID-19 emergency relief: pre-lockdown awareness
campaigns reached 20,000+ households in Darrang and 6,000+ in Udalguri; during
lockdown, dry ration went to ~7,000 families (with APPI, Tdh Germany, CASA, Aman
Biradari Trust), plus an Oxfam India Unconditional Cash Transfer of ₹5,000 to 232
poor families. Continuity of care was maintained for 1,100+ mental-illness patients
via medicine access and online psychiatric consultations. This report (78 pages)
combines the Annual Activity & Audit Report, listing board/staff details alongside
programme narratives.

## 2019-20 — Mental Health & Public Health Emphasis
The Community Mental Health Programme was relaunched with major new funding from
Azim Premji Philanthropic Initiatives (from Nov 2019), covering 80 villages in
Darrang district with screening camps and caregiver support (SATRA has run mental
health work since 2006). A new immunization-advocacy partnership began with the
Voluntary Health Association of India (VHAI). Continuing programmes included the
Brahmaputra River Basin Resilience Building Programme (Oxfam), Conflict Management &
Peace Education in Udalguri (Tdh-Germany), and new areas such as Childline 1098, the
Samvidhan Live/Jagrik Project, Labor Rights, and Environmental Rights.

## 2018-19 — Sanitation, Peacebuilding & Rights Expansion
Flagship new project: Faecal Sludge Management, a sanitation value-chain initiative
(with Tdh Foundation, Mangaldai Municipal Board, and district administration),
described as unique in eastern India and aligned with Swachh Bharat's ODF+ goals.
Conflict Management & Peace Education launched for children/adolescents in
ethnic-violence-affected Udalguri (Tdh-Germany), using music, dance and street
theatre. The Brahmaputra disaster-resilience programme continued into its 3rd phase
(15 villages, Oxfam India), alongside Respectful Maternity Care (with Diya
Foundation) and a sericulture/weaving livelihood programme that had grown from 20 to
10,000+ women since 2005.

## 2017-18 — Disaster Risk Reduction & Flood Response Focus
Centered on the Brahmaputra River Basin Resilience Building Programme (Oxfam
India): community-based disaster preparedness across 35 villages (Village Disaster
Management Committees, task forces, mock drills, WASH training), which reduced
livestock loss by over 80% since 2011. A post-flood humanitarian response (with Tdh
Foundation Germany) delivered relief — nutritional supplements, hygiene kits, and
WASH awareness — to 912 households (~5,704 people) across 5 flood-affected char
villages. Other programmes referenced include Swabalamban (livelihoods) and
advocacy work influencing government erosion-control measures.
