## 3. Program Areas (consistent across years, current 5 themes per 2024-25 report)

1. **Gender equity** — flagship: **AVAHAN** (Against Violence at Home – Act Now), running since 2017; now "AVAHAN 3.0". Domestic violence survivor support, community Response Groups, legal camps, village key-leader training.
2. **Sustainable livelihoods & climate** — SusFarm, sericulture/intercropping (with DWIMU & BARDWI), horticulture, handloom & weaving (Aagor Dagra Afad — women-led handloom enterprise).
3. **Health incl. nutrition, anaemia, SRHR, mental health** — **Project MITA** (Mental Illness Treatment Alliance, formerly "Mental Illness Treatment in Assam"), started 2007; low-cost model (~₹10-350/month); operates via 10+ partner NGOs across 14+ districts of Assam; ~5,500+ individuals reached annually; conducts psychiatrist-led camps in PHCs.
4. **Primary education** — **LEAP** (Learning Ecosystem Augmentation Project) — Forest Learning Centres (FLCs) for children in forest villages bordering Bhutan; ASER-style literacy/numeracy testing; Shiksha (learning centres), MESC (Middle Elementary Study Centres), CDCs (Child Development Centres).
5. **Youth life skills & empowerment** — "Colouring the Rainbow" (Ultimate Frisbee-based sports-for-development, gender-inclusive), Sifung Harimu Afad (Bodo cultural/music preservation collective, founded 2014), Community Media Unit (CMU, since 2021 — local-language documentaries/YouTube).

### Capacity-building arm: IDeA (Institute of Development Action)
- Founded 2007, based in Guwahati; three strategic pillars: **Cultivate & Incubate, Connect & Organise, Consult & Advise**.
- Has trained 1,000+ NGO professionals; supported 15+ partner NGOs across Assam, Meghalaya, Nagaland.
- **As of 2023-24, IDeA became an independently registered legal entity: "Roots to Branches Foundation."**
- Key institutional supporters of IDeA: Caring Friends, Child Aid Network (Germany), terre des hommes (TDH, Germany), BMZ (Germany), Dasra.
