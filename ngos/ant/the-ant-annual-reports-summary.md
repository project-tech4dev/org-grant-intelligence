# The ANT (The Action Northeast Trust) — Annual Reports Summary (FY2018-19 to FY2024-25)

Consolidated summary of all 7 available Annual Reports, produced by directly downloading and text-extracting each PDF from theant.org.

---

## FY 2018-19
**Overview:** Reflective note from founder Dr. Sunil Kaul as the ant approached its 20th anniversary (~150 staff), amid rising regulatory scrutiny of NGOs.

**Programs:** AVAHAN (255 DV survivors supported, judicial colloquium with Gauhati HC judge); Bodoland Girl Icon Programme (50-55 girls, 49 villages); LEAP (3,653 students); Project Shiksha (28 CDCs + 4 MSSCs); S4D (3,191 children/96 villages, stopped 4 child marriages); Project Dolphin (277 youth/45 villages); Caring 4 Rights (30 rights committees); MITA (6 districts, 600+ patients); IDeA 2.0 (new Guwahati office, 9 partner NGOs); Artisan Mapping (9,000 artisans mapped, UNDP-funded).

**Staff/Governance:** ~150 staff. Board: V.K. Madhavan, Debojit Brahma, Himalini Verma, Netaji Basumatary, Gita Rani Bhattacharya, Angela Rangad; Sunil Kaul (Founder/Managing Trustee).

**Funders:** Azim Premji Philanthropic Initiatives, kfb Austria, Caring Friends, UNICEF, UNDP, Milaan Foundation, TDH/BMZ Germany.

**Financials:** FY2019 Total Income ≈ ₹5.61 crore (via Give.do).

**Notable:** Report opens as tribute to "lowly NGO workers" amid rising public suspicion of civil society orgs.

---

## FY 2019-20
**Overview:** Leadership transition — Pranami Garg selected as ED-designate from 150+ applicants. Marks onset of COVID-19 and 19th anniversary (Sikkim trip).

**Programs:** S4D (1,562 children + 1,524 youth, first wheelchair-using staff hired); LEAP (learning gains for 1,794 middle + 2,444 primary students; ASER: illiteracy dropped from 3.22% to <1%); Project Dolphin (Aflatoun curriculum, 36 youth reviving traditional arts); AVAHAN (383 DV cases over 3 yrs, 139 given legal counselling); MITA (6,937 treated cumulatively since 2007, uninterrupted through early COVID); IDeA 2.0 (Faith Foundation graduated to FCRA-registered status; Project FLY expanded to 400 players); hosted JSIA/Jindal & IAS trainee visits.

**Staff/Governance:** Staff scaling to 170 (₹2M monthly salary bill). Same Board as FY2018-19.

**Funders:** Childaid Network, BMZ Germany, DKA Austria, Arpan Foundation, Azim Premji Philanthropic Initiatives, UNICEF, kfb Austria, Meljol, Youth Volunteers Organisation.

**Financials:** FY2020 Total Income ≈ ₹5.34 crore; Expenses ≈ ₹5.09 crore.

**Notable:** "Corona? No issues" — Mental Health team never missed a camp through early lockdown (featured in Civil Society magazine).

---

## FY 2020-21
**Overview:** Pranami Garg formally took over as ED (May 2020) amid COVID's first wave, through the devastating second wave (March 2021).

**Programs:** Field team fully deployed from 15 May 2020 in COVID-safe groups; sourced oxygen concentrators; reached 21,117 people directly, 58,759 families, 981 villages — no camp cancelled; AVAHAN tracked DV cases by type/referral source; LEAP supported 68 girls from low-income families; Mental Health tracked 1,200+ patients (Sept 2020 snapshot).

**Staff/Governance:** Full staff roster by project team. Same Board.

**Funders:** Arpan Foundation, Mahavir Trust, Smt. Bhagwanidevi Basudev Jhunjhunwala Trust, Optimum Stock Trading Co., Rachna Credit Capital + 30 individuals.

**Financials:** FY2021 Total Income ≈ ₹6.44 crore; Expenses ≈ ₹3.82 crore (income notably outpaced spend).

**Notable:** Report opens with apology for lateness; candid first-person account of COVID fear and fake news ("black tea before sunrise prevents CoViD-19").

---

## FY 2021-22
**Overview:** Second leadership transition — Pranami Garg resigned suddenly (Jan 2022); Rhondeni Kikon took over field operations. Staff grew to ~220. First-ever direct Government funding received (BTC's PMAGY scheme).

**Programs:** AVAHAN (MOU with ASRLM; 71 DV cases; One Billion Rising campaigns; featured survivor story "Pallabi"); Bring the Change (7Cs curriculum, 551 sessions, 164 girls reached Bronze); Shiksha (7 MESCs, 354 children; "Library in a Bag," 573 sessions); Colouring the Rainbow (1,854 Frisbee sessions, disability-inclusive); MOULD (new, Kadoorie-funded, Deosiri); FAB (65 children's clubs/58 villages); IDeA (Jodo Gyan math training, 48 schools; PMAGY survey of 2,896 households); COVID response (17,000 households reached).

**Staff/Governance:** ~220 staff. Board: Netaji Basumatary, Himalini Verma, Debojit Brahma, V.K. Madhavan, Angela Rangad, Gitarani Bhattacharya, Dhananjay Brahma, Sunil Kaul.

**Funders:** Anil Dua, Arpan Foundation, Azim Premji Philanthropic Initiatives (merging into APF), Ethical Tea Partnership, Global Worth Securities, Sir Horace Kadoorie International Foundation, DKA Austria, Dasra, Childaid Network, terre des hommes.

**Financials:** FY2022 Total Income ≈ ₹7.82 crore; Expenses ≈ ₹4.23 crore.

**Notable:** "This was the first time in the history of the ant in 21 years that we received funds from any Government agency directly, and wonder if and when the entire amount will get to us!"

## FY 2022-23
**Overview:** 23rd Foundation Day (Oct 2022). Netaji Basumatary became ED from 1 Jan 2023. Featured in CNES visual storyboards and The Wire.

**Programs:** LEAP (141 govt schools, 108 made "child-friendly"; ASER: 83% could read a story); AVAHAN (94 Response Groups, 321 DV cases, 1,000+ participant Walkathon); Bring the Change (24 villages, 2 child-marriages resolved); Livelihood Kuklung (283 farmers trained, 11,050 saplings produced); Community Media Unit (documentary "Biyad: Love with Nature"); MITA (13,232 registrations, 30,430 consultations, documentary "MITA Cares"); IDeA (15 partner orgs/3 states; Project ENRICH reached 3,500 children; curated Bodoland Knowledge Festival gender theme).

**Staff/Governance:** Board: Devojit Brahma, Dhananjay Brahma, Geeta Rani Bhattacharya, Dr. Sunil Kaul, Shannon Dona Massar, V.K. Madhavan, Netaji Basumatary.

**Funders:** Azim Premji Philanthropic Initiatives, SBI Foundation, Childaid Network, terre des hommes, Sir Horace Kadoorie International Foundation, American Friends of EdelGive Foundation.

**Financials:** Image-only pages (not text-extractable); Give.do doesn't cover FY2023.

**Notable:** First-ever Biodiversity Walks; internal sexual-harassment-prevention workshop for staff.

## FY 2023-24
**Overview:** Crisis year — FCRA fund-access suspension (~May 2023) forced downsizing and departure of 140 staff. IDeA spun off as independent entity "Roots to Branches Foundation." Announced Silver Jubilee plans and new 5-year strategy.

**Programs:** LEAP (18 FLCs + 2 MLCs, 838 children, 75% attendance); Colouring the Rainbow (1,086 youths, story of Anjuma Begum); AVAHAN (321 DV cases, Response Groups manage ~40% independently); Sifung cultural revival (317 youth + 63 children, 87 villages); MOULD/Sus-FARM/DWIMU livelihoods (500 households; 500 women weavers supported); MITA (16th year, 27 sites, ~5,000 patients/month); IDeA (15 partner orgs; State Child Rights Convention with UNICEF Assam).

**Staff/Governance:** Board: Gitarani Bhattacharya, Debojit Brahma, V.K. Madhavan, Dhananjay Brahma, Shannon Donna Massar, Suja Warriar, Dr. Sunil Kaul (resigned 23 April 2023).

**Funders:** Rohini Nilekani Philanthropies, EdelGive Foundation, Interglobe Foundation, SBI Foundation, Azim Premji Philanthropic Initiatives, Eicher Group Foundation.

**Financials:** Image-only pages (not text-extractable).

**Notable:** ED's letter: "...the unfortunate departure of 140 staff members during the year, due to inability to access FCRA funds" — frames it as the toughest year, sustained by donor/community solidarity.

## FY 2024-25 (Silver Jubilee Year)
**Overview:** 25th anniversary. Organized around five themes: gender equity, sustainable livelihoods/climate, health, primary education, youth life-skills.

**Programs:** AVAHAN 3.0 (151 villages, 175 new DV cases, 1,325 people reached); LEAP (18 FLCs, 736 children); Colouring the Rainbow (1,243 children/youth, 44 villages); Sustainable Livelihoods (2,400 farmers/50 villages); MITA (5,815 registered clients, 60 monthly PHC camps); Apraava Scholarship (124 students supported).

**Staff/Governance:** Board: Gitarani Bhattacharya (Chair), Debojit Brahma, V.K. Madhavan, Dhananjay Brahma, Shannon Donna Massar, Suja Warriar. ED: Netaji Basumatary; Programme Director: Rhondeni Kikon.

**Funders:** Rohini Nilekani Philanthropies, Eicher Group Foundation, Infosys Foundation, Azim Premji Philanthropic Initiatives, NABARD, SBI Foundation, TERI, Interglobe Foundation.

**Financials:** Financial statement pages (58-64) are image-only, not text-extractable.

**Notable:** ED's letter reflects on the 25-year arc: "What began as a humble initiative in the Bongaigaon district (now Chirang) in 2000 has grown into a trusted organization for empowerment and development across Northeast India."

## Cross-Year Trends Observed
1. **Leadership churn**: 4 ED transitions in 7 years (Sunil Kaul → Pranami Garg → Rhondeni Kikon interim → Netaji Basumatary).
2. **MITA growth**: ~600 patients (2018-19) → 5,800+ registered clients (2024-25).
3. **FCRA vulnerability**: explicit 2023-24 fund-access disruption (140 staff let go) despite valid FCRA registration.
4. **Institutional funder consolidation**: Azim Premji Foundation & SBI Foundation present nearly every year; Rohini Nilekani Philanthropies & EdelGive prominent from ~2021.
5. **Structural evolution**: IDeA → "IDeA 2.0" (Guwahati office, 2018) → independent "Roots to Branches Foundation" (2023-24).
6. **Financial pattern**: Income notably outpaced expenses in FY2021-22, consistent with the later-reported FCRA fund-utilization disruption.
