# Projonmo — Organization Profile

> This document is updated continuously as new information is found. See `Projonmo_Programs.md` for the program-area detail, annual-report status, and recognition/awards.

## Organization Overview

- **Full name:** Projonmo
- **Founded:** The work began informally in **2013**, "in a small room with just three passionate individuals offering extra tutorials to children who were identified as slow learners" (per the Founder's own "Message From Founder"). After realizing education alone was insufficient, this evolved into the formally-named organization **"Projonmo" in March 2014**. Both "2013" and "2014," found in different sources, are therefore correct — referring respectively to the informal starting point and the formal founding date.
- **Founder:** **Ashoka Sarma** — described as "a passionate social entrepreneur," and also Projonmo's **Secretary** per the current Governing Body. She also mentors/supports marginalized women through Self-Help Groups (SHGs) as a separate activity.
- **Other named leadership (confirmed via website, August 2024 Independence Day flag-hoisting mention):** **Simanta Borah** — Vice President; **Anumita Bora** — Headmistress.
- **Legal status:** Registered under the **Societies Registration Act of 1860**. Per one third-party source, Projonmo was established "under the umbrella of Bal Bharti's Educational Trust" (not yet confirmed on the official site itself — flagged for verification). Certified **80G and 12A** (donations eligible for tax savings under 80G).
- **PAN:** AACAP9434E (per Projonmo's own Bank Details document).
- **Bank details (for donations):** Bank of Baroda, Beltola Branch, Guwahati; Account No. 29650200000566 (Current Account); IFSC: BARB0BELTOL.
- **Headquarters:** Guwahati, Assam. A second centre operates in **Tamulpur, BTAD area** (Bodoland Territorial Area District), Assam.
- **Confirmed physical address:** Ankur Path, House Number 9, Survey, Guwahati, Assam, India, 781028. Office hours: 9:30 AM – 5:30 PM.
- **Core mission/focus:** Support and services for **Children with Special Needs (CWSN)** and **Adults with Special Needs (AWSN)** — intellectual and developmental disabilities. Services described include: Occupational Therapy, Speech Therapy, Special Education, Special Schooling, Daycare, and Vocational Training/Activities. The organization also trains social workers, teachers, and daycare helpers, and facilitates engagement with medical, welfare, and human rights organizations. Also works to create **livelihood options for mothers of children with special needs** (both rural and urban) so they can take informed financial decisions about their child's care.
- **8-point mission (per a third-party directory, likely sourced from official materials):**
  1. Deliver individualized, multidisciplinary therapy and education.
  2. Build local capacity and professional expertise in Northeast India.
  3. Foster life skills and long-term independence for children.
  4. Raise awareness and reduce stigma around disabilities.
  5. Ensure access to care for economically vulnerable families.
  6. Advocate for inclusive policies and systems-level change.
  7. Strengthen evidence-based practices through research and documentation.
  8. Empower parents and caregivers as active allies in every child's journey.
- **7 Core Values (per the official "About" page):** Dignity, Inclusive by Design, Integrity in Action, Empowerment Through Support, Collaboration, Adaptability with Purpose (a 7th value is referenced but was cut off in the page text retrieved — to be confirmed).
- **Geographic reach (as described):** Aims to "generate access across people of the state of Assam and beyond" / serves "children with special needs across Northeast India" — positioning itself as a Northeast India-wide resource, though physical centres are confirmed only in Guwahati and Tamulpur so far.
- **Named partners/funders (confirmed so far):**
  - **Azim Premji Foundation (APF)** — supports Projonmo in at least two ways: (1) funded a one-day training on "Basic Home Therapies for Special Needs Children"; (2) is the confirmed funder behind the 2024 launch of the **Community Divyangjan Centre (CDC), Tamulpur** — see `Projonmo_Programs.md`. (The website separately uses the phrase "Azim Premji Philanthropy" for what is confirmed to be the same funding relationship — Projonmo is simply inconsistent in naming, not describing two different funders.)
  - **AIIMS Guwahati** — a multi-faceted partner: (1) Projonmo's inaugural Parent Support Program, "Caring for Your Child with ASD"; (2) an AIIMS yoga therapist trained parents at Projonmo's premises; (3) AIIMS doctors have collected blood samples from children at Projonmo for a clinical research study on "oxidative stress hormones" — see `Projonmo_Programs.md`.
  - **Bal Bharti's Public School / Educational Trust, Guwahati** — Projonmo's Special School is affiliated with Bal Bharti's Public School, and one source states Projonmo itself was founded under the umbrella of Bal Bharti's Educational Trust.
  - **News18 Assam North East** and **Rotary Club of Guwahati ICONS** — jointly recognized Projonmo with a "Special Category Award" for its social contributions (year not stated) — see `Projonmo_Programs.md` for detail.
- **Internship Program:** Projonmo runs a structured, hands-on internship program for students/professionals in social work, psychology, education, therapy, and public health — interns join multidisciplinary teams across therapy sessions, educational planning, community outreach, parent support, and livelihood initiatives. Applicants submit an Internship Form + CV to admin@projonmo.org.in.
- **Recent activities mentioned on website (undated or partially dated):**
  - Observed India's 78th Independence Day (15 August 2024) with a flag-hoisting ceremony (Ashoka Sarma, Simanta Borah, Anumita Bora presiding).
  - A soap-making workshop held during an "Inclusive Summer Camp" as a sensory-rich activity for children.
  - A digital-literacy initiative teaching children with special needs basic computer operations ("bridging the digital divide").
  - A public statement of solidarity regarding the late Dr. Moumita Debnath (R G Kar Medical College, Kolkata), condemning actions that undermined her dignity — reflecting the org's broader stance on rights/dignity issues beyond its core disability-services mandate.
