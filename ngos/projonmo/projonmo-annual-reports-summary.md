## Annual Activity Report — Session 2022-23
*(Most detailed/expansive report of the three — 12 distinct projects)*

1. **Autism Awareness Month** (2 April 2022, 15th annual World Autism Awareness Day) — cake cutting, dance, coloring activities.
2. **Music Therapy** — new program launched with musician Nanda Banerjee; used to build cognitive ability & self-expression in special-needs children.
3. **Group Activities for CWSN** — regular sessions to build independence, communication, and social skills (targeting core Autism-related challenges).
4. **Vocational Training** — weaving, block printing, candle-making, incense stick packaging — aimed at future employability.
5. **Fund Raising** — stall at Royal Global School's Diwali Fest (20 Oct 2022) selling decorative candles, clay items, handwoven napkins/runners made by specially-abled persons.
6. **International Day of Persons with Disabilities** (3 Dec) — fun activities, dance performances by children and parents.
7. **Free Workshop for Women** (26–30 Dec 2022) — 4-day workshop on block printing & candle making for women from Economically Weaker Sections (EWS).
8. **Health Camps** — monthly camps at Tamulpur and Mirza, in association with Bal Bharti's Educational Trust.
9. **Annual Day Celebration** (22 Jan 2023) — dance performances by children, educators, and therapists.
10. **Free Disability Aids** — partnered with Mobility India (a national/international leader in Physical Rehabilitation & Assistive Technology, recognized by ISPO) to donate aids for children with physical disabilities. Projonmo serves 70+ children from its center on Survey, Beltola Road.
11. **Workshop on SRHR** (4 Feb 2023) — Sexual & Reproductive Health and Rights discussion for children with special needs reaching adolescence, led by Dr. Manidipa Baruah (Gauhati University).
12. **4th State Divyangjan Sports Meet** — children's participation in sports events (including Special Olympics-style meets) to build confidence and social cohesion.

**Core year-round services mentioned:** Therapy Centre (Occupational Therapy, Speech Therapy, Special Education, Physiotherapy, Music Therapy, Yoga, psychological assessment, early intervention) and a Day Care Centre for CWSN.

## Overall Trajectory (2020–2023)
Projonmo is a **Guwahati-based special-needs NGO** (est. under Bal Bharti's Educational Trust, registered 2013-14) that has steadily expanded its programming for Children/Adults with Special Needs:
- **2020-21:** Survival/adaptation mode — pivoted to virtual teaching during COVID lockdown, then carefully reopened with pandemic protocols; added crisis-response activities (ration distribution, mental health camp).
- **2021-22:** Resumed a fuller calendar of recreational, therapeutic, and awareness events (baking, gardening, sports, cultural celebrations).
- **2022-23:** Significant program expansion — added **Music Therapy** as a new offering, formalized **Vocational Training** (weaving, block printing, candle-making) for employability, launched **women's community workshops**, partnered with **Mobility India** for disability aids, and added **SRHR education** for adolescents. This is the most comprehensive of the three reports, suggesting organizational growth and diversification of services.
- **2023 (Financial Policy):** Formalized internal financial governance — a sign of growing institutional maturity and donor-compliance readiness.
