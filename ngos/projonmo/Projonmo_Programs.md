# Projonmo — Program Areas, Annual Reports & Recognition

> This document is updated continuously as new information is found. See `Projonmo_Organization_Profile.md` for organization identity, leadership, legal/bank details, and internal policy documents.
>
> **Important context:** Projonmo is a Guwahati-based NGO serving **children and adults with special needs/developmental disabilities** — a different sector from the livelihoods/health/education NGOs previously researched in this project (SNEHPAD, Diya Foundation, MOSONiE). The requested table format (Program / Status / Description / Sector / SDG Tags / Occupation Taxonomy / Geography / Named Funders / Metric Trend / Open Questions) is applied here as a **structural template**, not because Projonmo runs programs literally named "AVAHAN," "LEAP," etc. — those were illustrative examples from a different organization's reporting style, not Projonmo's actual programs.

## Annual Report Access Status (read this first)

Projonmo's website (projonmo.org.in/reports.php) lists **5 Annual Reports**: 2024-25, 2023-24, 2022-23, 2021-22, 2020-21. **All 5 are broken links (HTTP 404)** — the linked `/report/` directory either doesn't exist or doesn't contain these files (a direct directory-listing attempt returned HTTP 403 Forbidden). Multiple path/casing variations were tried and all failed. No working mirror has been found elsewhere.

This is the same "broken document repository" pattern found in this project's earlier research on SNEHPAD and Diya Foundation. **As a result, every "Metric Trend Across Years" field below is built from the live website's descriptive/marketing text (undated, qualitative) rather than from dated annual-report figures.** A dated "Events & Updates" blog feed on the site (see Programs 9 and 10 below) has partially filled this gap with some real, dated, named-funder activity — but it is not a substitute for full annual-report data.

### 1. Special Education & Schooling (Ankur Path)
- **Status:** Active — the organization's founding/core activity
- **Description:** A "world-class, evidence-based curriculum" delivered by therapists/special educators, identifying developmental needs early and fostering growth through personalized adaptations. Operates as a full Special School at "Ankur Path," Survey, Beltola, Guwahati — affiliated with Bal Bharti's Public School, Guwahati — using Individualized Education Plans (IEPs) and holistic, child-centered teaching. Aims to help children integrate into mainstream school programs.
- **Sector:** Disability Services / Special Education
- **SDG Tags** *(inferred)*: SDG 4, SDG 10
- **Occupation Taxonomy**: None (direct social-service delivery, not a livelihoods program)
- **Geography (as stated):** Ankur Path centre, Survey, Beltola, Guwahati, Assam
- **Named Funders/Partners:** Bal Bharti's Public School, Guwahati (educational affiliation/umbrella)
- **Metric Trend Across Years:** No enrollment figures found on the website; would likely be in the (currently broken) annual reports.
- **Open Questions:** How many children are currently enrolled at Ankur Path, and how has enrollment changed over time?

### 3. Livelihood Education and Skill Training
- **Status:** Active
- **Description:** Skill-building activities in economically viable trades — weaving, incense packing, candle making, block printing, mushroom cultivation, and oil pressing — for children/young people with disabilities, aimed at fostering independence, self-confidence, and self-reliance.
- **Sector:** Livelihoods / Vocational Training (Disability-Inclusive)
- **SDG Tags** *(inferred)*: SDG 1, SDG 8, SDG 10
- **Occupation Taxonomy** *(inferred)*: Retail, Commerce & Craft → Creative & Traditional Trades, 🔴 Informal (Persons with Disabilities / Youth)
- **Geography (as stated):** Guwahati (Ankur Path daycare centre) and Tamulpur (Community Development Centre / Community Divyangjan Centre — see Program 9)
- **Named Funders:** Not specified for the Guwahati version; the Tamulpur/CDC livelihood component is funded by Azim Premji Foundation (see Program 9)
- **Metric Trend Across Years:** Participants "create handmade products for public sale, promoting self-worth and economic participation" — no sales figures, participant counts, or income data given.
- **Open Questions:** How many participants per trade, and is there any income/sales data? What does "public sale" look like in practice?

### 9. Community Development Centre, Tamulpur / Community Divyangjan Centre (CDC)
- **Status:** Active; formalized/relaunched as the **"Community Divyangjan Centre (CDC)"** flagship program in **2024** — the organization's most significant recent expansion.
- **Description:** A rural satellite centre providing occupational therapy, speech therapy, and special education tailored to each child, plus home-based therapy for families unable to travel due to physical/financial/logistical barriers. Also runs livelihood programs for mothers/caregivers (mushroom cultivation, horticulture, weaving) and trains local community workers and government functionaries to build "sustainable solutions owned by the very communities we serve." Works to improve access to basic health services and reduce community stigma.
- **Sector:** Community-Based Rehabilitation (CBR) / Rural Outreach / Livelihoods
- **SDG Tags** *(inferred)*: SDG 1, SDG 3, SDG 4, SDG 10
- **Occupation Taxonomy** *(inferred)*: Agritech & Allied Ecosystem → Cultivation & Sourcing (mushroom/horticulture); Retail, Commerce & Craft → Creative & Traditional Trades (weaving) — both 🔴 Informal (Rural women/caregivers)
- **Geography (as stated):** Tamulpur, BTAD (Bodoland Territorial Area District), Assam
- **Named Funders — CONFIRMED via a dedicated event announcement:** *"We are thrilled to introduce the launch of our Community Divyangan Centre (CDC) in Tamulpur, Bodo Land Territorial Region, with the generous support of the **Azim Premji Foundation (APF)**."* This confirms "Azim Premji Foundation" and "Azim Premji Philanthropy" (used elsewhere on the site) refer to the same funding relationship — Projonmo is simply inconsistent in naming, not describing two different funders.
- **Metric Trend Across Years:**
  - **At launch (2024): 50+ children** already benefiting from regular therapy sessions.
  - **3-year target (from 2024): 200+ households** with children with special needs to be reached.
  - A separate, undated blog post confirms ongoing clinical activity: "Occupational Therapy assessment at CDC centre Tamulpur."
- **Open Questions:** Is "CDC" a rebrand/formalization of the pre-existing "Community Development Centre, Tamulpur," or a genuinely new, separate facility? Is there any interim progress data toward the 200-household/3-year target (would likely be in the broken annual reports)?

## Cross-Cutting Observations

1. **No program-level quantitative data found anywhere on the live website** except the CDC launch figures (Program 9) and the AIIMS blog snippets (Program 10) — every other program description is purely qualitative. This is a marked contrast to SNEHPAD, Diya Foundation, and MOSONiE, whose websites at least had some undated snapshot figures even before annual reports were located.
2. **Named funders/partners found so far:** Azim Premji Foundation (a.k.a. "Azim Premji Philanthropy" in some site text — same relationship), Bal Bharti's Public School/Educational Trust (institutional affiliation), AIIMS Guwahati (clinical/training partner), News18 Assam North East + Rotary Club of Guwahati ICONS (award recognition).
3. **The organization's language is notably more "rights-based" and internationally-framed** than the other NGOs researched in this project — explicit references to the UN CRPD, India's RPwD Act, and Universal Design for Learning (UDL) suggest a more recent/professionalized program-design process.
4. A dated **"Events & Updates" blog feed** (projonmo.org.in/blog.php, with individual posts at `events_details.php?ref=N`) is currently the *only* source of concrete, dated, named-funder program activity for Projonmo, given the broken annual reports. Posts found and used: ref=3 (AIIMS yoga training), ref=4 (OT assessment at CDC), ref=6 (CDC launch, full detail), ref=7 (News18 Award). IDs ref=1, 2, 5, 8, 9 did not return distinct content when tried. This feed should be checked periodically for new posts as the organization updates it.
