## Annual Report 2021-2022
**Partners:** SELCO Foundation, Envo Renewable Energy Services (ERES), Young Indians (Guwahati Chapter), Meghalaya Rural Bank, District Disaster Management Authority Ri-Bhoi, Azim Premji Foundation, National RCRC & NE-RCRC, Haiyya, Goonj.

- **Health Care & Awareness:** Menstrual hygiene awareness with SHUDDHI org (140 reusable pads distributed); COVID-19 awareness & sensitization across 16 villages with District Medical & Health Office; COVID-19 vaccination drive reaching 37 villages via Patharkhmah CHC (supported by Azim Premji Foundation); International Women's Day event (50 participants) on gender equality & menstrual hygiene.
- **Education:** MOSONiE NCPE (Non-Conventional Practical Education) alternative school program (since Oct 2016); Digital Education Program using solar-powered tools; Child Rights & Safety awareness reaching 150 students (with Young Indians & Childline Nongpoh).
- **Environment:** 700 fruit tree saplings (banana, pear, litchi, orange, lemon, coconut, jamun) distributed across 4 villages with Young Indian & Round Table orgs.
- **Clean Energy:** Ongoing project since 2017 with SELCO Foundation — 34 solar-powered livelihood installations (Eri-spinning machines, rice hullers, salon trimmers, petty shops, LSKs) + 138 basic home lighting systems installed to date.
- **Livelihoods:** Eri-silk spinning training for 43 women across 3 villages (with Central Silk Board & Resham Sutra Pvt. Ltd.).
- **Interns:** 10 student interns hosted during the year.
- **Relief Response:** COVID-19 relief activities.

## Annual Report 2022-2023
**Reach:** Umling (30 villages), Jirang (50 villages), Bhoiryngbong (4 villages), Umsning (2 villages).
**Focus areas:** Health Care & Hygiene, Sustainable Livelihood, Environment & Natural Resource Protection, Education (school libraries).

- **Health:** 800+ reusable sanitary pads distributed; COVID-19 vaccination drive (2nd wave) across 37 villages in Jirang Block (with Azim Premji Foundation & Patharkhmah CHC); Mental Health awareness session with District Mental Health Programme, Nongpoh.
- **Livelihood — Soh-Marit (Black Pepper) Project:** New incubation grant from IDeA–the ant Organization; 40 farmers/households across 2 villages (Nongspung-A, Timbokgre); baseline survey of 80 households; farmer exposure visits.
- **Livelihood — Poultry:** Backyard poultry training with Retd. Prof. Krishna Sharma (College of Veterinary Science); 250 free chicks distributed to farmers across 3-4 villages.
- **Environment:** Tree plantation drive (World Environment Day, 6 June 2022) with Goonj — fruit saplings (guava, lemon, gooseberry, orange, banana, plum, peach, mango, jackfruit) to 48 farmers across 4 villages; 2 schools involved.
- **Clean Energy:** Since 2017 — cumulative 138 home lighting systems + 56 livelihood solar solutions installed across Jirang, Umling, Bhoiryngbong blocks (with SELCO Foundation, ERES, Soltech Solar); new installs included water pumps (piggery), Eri-spinning, LSK, solar bakery, poultry farm lighting; end-users linked to Meghalaya Rural Bank for loans.
- **Interns:** Students from Azim Premji University, Assam Don Bosco University, USTM.
- **Cloth for Work (CFW):** Started Aug 2022 with Goonj — 850 family package kits distributed across 15 villages in Jirang & Umling Blocks for community infrastructure work (roads, wells, toilets, drainage, kitchen gardens).
- **Other:** School libraries stocked (2 schools, books donated by Goonj); Community Health Kits distributed to ASHAs (with Goonj); RCRC Household Survey — 300 households across 20 villages in Jirang Block (post-COVID livelihood impact study); walking sticks distributed.

## Annual Report 2023-2024
**Scope:** 1 District, 4 Blocks (Jirang, Umling, Bhoirymbong, Umsning).

- **Clean Energy:** Awareness in 10 new villages; 170 livelihood solar solutions + 30 home lighting systems installed (Eri-spinning machines, LSK/photocopy, petty shop & farm lighting, dryers, egg incubators). Inaugurated 3 Eri-spinning centers + a MOSONiE Resource Center (Aug-Sept 2023), benefiting 118 individuals. Training sessions for 60 women on solar-powered Eri-silk spinning (with Central Silk Board); 2 exposure visits for 65 end-users; financial literacy camp (40 participants, with Meghalaya Rural Bank & MSRLS); solar egg incubator training (37 participants).
- **Soh-Marit (Black Pepper):** Supported by Roots to Branches Foundation; 21 new participants in 3 new villages; 1,300 black pepper + 430 banana saplings distributed to 29 farmers; exposure visit to District Horticulture Farm, Byrnihat (17 participants); household-level holistic planning consultation (19 farmers, with Diya Foundation's Romen Rabha as resource person).
- **Enhancing Income in 10 Tribal Villages (with Azim Premji Foundation):** Piggery training — 140 participants trained, 103 availed ₹4,500 revolving-fund loans each; Sericulture/Eri-silk training for 37 women (4 batches, with Dept. of Textiles & Resham Sutra).
- **Health & Hygiene:** Menstrual hygiene awareness reaching 150 women/adolescent girls; Operation & Maintenance monitoring of 49 solar-powered health centers (with SELCO Foundation) across Ri-Bhoi District.
- **Environment:** Cloth for Work (CFW), School-to-School (S2S), and Community Library programs continued across 3 blocks with Goonj.

## Annual Report 2024-2025
**Letter from Executive Director:** Liinai Margaret — highlights the organization's first-ever staff retreat, a new 5-year strategic plan, and continued focus on livelihoods and women's health.

**Geographical outreach expanded to 2 states:** Meghalaya (Ri-Bhoi District: Umling, Bhoirymbong, Jirang, Umsning blocks) and Manipur (Senapati District: T. Khullen, Khabungkarong, Koide blocks) — a notable geographic expansion beyond Meghalaya.

**Key Impact Metrics (FY 2024-25):**
- **Piggery Livelihood:** 104 farmers adopted pig farming; 95 connected to piggery insurance; 16 community volunteers trained as vaccinators; ₹4.98 lakh disbursed as revolving fund across 9 Village Organizations; 119 farmers trained in scientific pig rearing.
- **Soh-Marit (Black Pepper):** 1,300 black pepper + 420 banana saplings planted; 50 farmers supported across 5 villages; 720 kg organic manure distributed to 41 farmers.
- **Clean Energy:** 534 total livelihood-based Decentralized Renewable Energy (DRE) solutions installed to date (388 livelihood solutions + 146 home lighting systems).
- **Cloth for Work & School-to-School:** 800 CFW kits distributed across 39 villages; 985 S2S kits distributed to 14 schools.
- **Menstrual Hygiene:** 36 awareness sessions across 29 villages; 2,000 hygiene kits distributed; 6 staff trained as in-house Menstrual Hygiene Management trainers.
- **Health Energy:** 47 health centers monitored across 4 blocks in Ri-Bhoi District.

**Organizational Development:** Participated in the "NoDE" (Northeast Organizational Development for Empowerment) Program — strategic planning with Bridgespan (5-year plan developed), digital communications training, and a proposal-writing workshop in Guwahati.

**Strategic Planning Retreats:**
- Aug 2024: 3-day "ViMiVASTRA" strategic planning workshop (with Roots to Branches) — revisited vision/mission/values, conducted SWOT analysis with 4 villages, built a 3-year action plan.
- Jan 2025: First-ever Annual Staff Retreat ("Reflection, Recreation & Visioning") at Chanako Eco Camp Resort, Guwahati.

**New Vision (2024-25):** "Regeneration of common wealth towards wellbeing of people and the planet."
**Purpose:** Empower underserved communities through collective action towards equitable access and resource regeneration.
**Core Values:** Integrity, Dignity, Inclusivity. **Guiding Principles:** Transparency, Solidarity, Teamwork.

## Overall Trajectory (2021–2025)
MOSONiE has evolved from a pandemic-response/multi-activity NGO (2021-22) into a more strategically focused organization built around **three flagship, multi-year programs**:
1. **Clean Energy / DRE livelihoods** (with SELCO Foundation) — scaled from 34 installs (2021-22) to 534 cumulative installs (2024-25).
2. **Soh-Marit (Black Pepper) sustainable livelihoods** — scaled from a 2-village pilot (2022-23, 40 farmers) to a multi-year program (2023-26) reaching 50+ farmers by 2024-25.
3. **Menstrual Health & Hygiene** — scaled from 140 pads (2021-22) to 2,000 kits/29 villages (2024-25).

The organization also expanded geographically into **Manipur (Senapati District)** by 2024-25, invested heavily in internal organizational development (strategic planning, staff retreats), and deepened partnerships with Azim Premji Foundation, SELCO Foundation, Goonj, Roots to Branches Foundation, and Central Silk Board.
