## Additional Notes on Website/Reporting Structure

- The website's **"Reports & Studies"** page also features **beneficiary testimonial stories** as distinct content pieces (not just the annual reports), including:
  - _"In a remote Meghalayan village, one solar husker is changing fortunes, freeing up time for women"_ (101Reporters, syndicated/linked)
  - _"Solar power a money-spinner for women spinning eri silk yarn"_ (VillageSquare/CEEW, syndicated/linked)
  - **Ritalish Syngkli** (Teacher) — menstrual hygiene program testimonial (quoted in `MOSONiE_Programs.md`, Program 3)
  - **Kong Iaishah Rymbai** (Weaver) — _"From a simple dream to a thriving movement-MOSONiE's support helped me turn Eri Weave into a hub where women learn, create, and earn. Today, we're not just weaving silk, we're weaving empowerment."_
  - **Rikynti Kharmawphlang** (Student, Nongpoh) — mentioned on the homepage as a testimonial-giver, full quote not yet captured
- The site also references a **"Mosonie Impact and Livelihoods"** donation/impact page (linked from the homepage) not yet separately fetched in this pass.
- **Tender Notices page** currently lists one open position: **"Project Coordinator"** (Tender & Recruitment Notice, submission deadline 28 Jul, stated year ambiguous — the page uses a "2026" date that may reflect the same site-wide future-date display quirk observed on other Northeast Indian NGO websites researched previously, e.g., SNEHPAD's Instagram).
- **The CSR Universe** profile lists a very broad set of "Thematic Areas" for MOSONiE (Agriculture, Education & Literacy, Environment & Forests, Food Processing, Health & Family Welfare, Housing, Micro Finance/SHGs, MSMEs, New & Renewable Energy, Nutrition, Tourism, Vocational Training, Women's Development & Empowerment, Children, Drinking Water, Aged/Elderly, Land Resources, Legal Awareness & Aid, Rural Development & Poverty Alleviation, Water Resources, Animal Husbandry, Dairying & Fisheries) — this appears to be a generic/maximal tag-set rather than a curated list specific to MOSONiE's actual programming, and should be treated with caution.
- The same CSR Universe profile separately lists eight more specific "Special Features":
  1. Installing solar home lighting systems in villages without electricity
  2. Bringing education to villages without schools
  3. Bringing healthcare to villages with inaccessible roads
  4. Helping villagers avail important government documents
  5. Bringing livelihood schemes to the poor
  6. Strengthening social institutions
  7. Solar skill training for village youths
  8. Alternate livelihood training for **stone quarry workers** — this last point is not mentioned anywhere on the current website and may represent either an older or a not-yet-rediscovered program.

### Questions Resolved

- ✅ **SELCO Foundation partnership start year:** Confirmed as **2017** (stated identically in 3 separate reports), resolving the 2017-vs-2018 discrepancy between VillageSquare and an Instagram/CSP post.
- ✅ **Founding story / history:** Confirmed incorporation date (8 September 2016, Section 8 Company), and that 5 of 6 founding members are from Manipur (Senapati/Churachandpur/Ukhrul districts), explaining the Manipur geographic footprint as founder-linked rather than a separate program expansion.
- ✅ **Whether "Community Health Camps" and COVID relief overlap:** Resolved — COVID-19 ration/hygiene-kit relief and vaccination-drive work are distinct, separately-reported activities (see Programs 8 and 11 in `MOSONiE_Programs.md`), not folded into a generic "Community Health Camps" line, which itself could not be found as a distinct, metric-bearing program in any of the 4 readable reports.
- ✅ **Year-by-year Clean Energy/DRE trend line:** Now fully built out — 3 end-users (2017) → 194 total systems (FY22-23) → 394 (FY23-24) → 534 (FY24-25).
- ✅ **Year-by-year Menstrual Hygiene trend line:** Now fully built out — 140 (FY21-22) → 800+ (FY22-23) → 2,000 (FY23-24) → 2,000 kits/29 villages (FY24-25).
- ✅ **Soh Marit funder identity and timeline:** Confirmed launched 2022; funded first by "IDeA-the ant" (Incubation Grant, FY22-23), then by "Roots to Branches Foundation" (FY23-24 onward) — independently corroborating this research project's separate finding (from the Diya Foundation files) that Diya's old "IDeA" program spun off into Roots to Branches Foundation.

### Questions Still Open

- **Financial/budget statements:** No income/expenditure/funder-wise financial tables were found as extractable text in any of the 4 readable reports — the FY2021-22 report has a "Financials" section header (page 21) but the actual content appears to be an image/table with no extractable text. **This remains a full gap** — MOSONiE's actual budget size, funder-wise income breakdown, and expenditure ratios are unknown from any source consulted.
- **"Stone quarry workers" alternate-livelihood program** (flagged by The CSR Universe): still not found in any of the 4 readable annual reports — may be in the 2 unreadable reports, may be outdated/discontinued, or may be a CSR Universe data error.
- **"15,000+ households / 50,000+ trees / 20,000+ reached" homepage claims:** Still not tied to a specific year or precise calculation, though the newly-confirmed program-level figures (e.g., 1,100 households in the piggery program alone, 534 DRE installations, thousands of menstrual-hygiene-kit recipients) make the "15,000+ households" figure plausible as a genuine multi-program, multi-year cumulative count rather than an inflated claim.
- **MOSONiE NCPE School** (the Timbokgre village school, running since October 2016): completely unmentioned in the FY2022-23, FY2023-24, and FY2024-25 reports, despite being a substantial, named institutional commitment in FY2021-22. Is it still running? This is arguably the single most important unresolved question in this research so far — a physical school disappearing from 3 consecutive annual reports without any mention of closure is unusual and worth a direct follow-up.
- **"Chartered Village" strategy:** Also only appears in the FY2021-22 report and nowhere else — likely superseded by later strategic-planning language (see FY2024-25's "ViMiVASTRA" process) but not explicitly confirmed as such.
- **The apparent net decrease in home-lighting-system count** between the implied FY2023-24 cumulative (168) and the stated FY2024-25 cumulative (146) — see Program 4 in `MOSONiE_Programs.md` for detail.
- **FY2019-20 and FY2020-21 remain completely unread** (image-only PDFs) — this is a 2-year blind spot in the trend lines, sitting right at the start of the COVID-19 pandemic, which is likely to have been an eventful period for the organization.
