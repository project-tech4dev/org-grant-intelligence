# MOSONiE Socio-Economic Foundation — Organization Profile

> Built from MOSONiE's official website (mosonie.org), independent third-party coverage (101Reporters, VillageSquare/CEEW, MNRE, RCRC, LinkedIn/Instagram), and the **full text of 4 of the 6 downloaded Annual Reports** (FY2021-22, FY2022-23, FY2023-24, FY2024-25). **FY2019-20 and FY2020-21 are scanned/image-only PDFs with no extractable text layer** — OCR would be required to read them, which is not available in this research setup; they are flagged as a permanent gap rather than guessed at. This document is updated continuously as new information is found.

This file covers the organization's identity, leadership, and governance. Programs are covered separately in `MOSONiE_Programs.md`.

---

## Organization Overview

- **Full name:** MOSONiE Socio-Economic Foundation (MOSONiE SEF)
- **Founded:** 2015; **registered** in 2016 as a **Section 8 Company** under the Companies Act, 2013
- **Leadership:** A **women-led** organization with **five/six founding members** (see roster below).
- **Incorporation date confirmed:** Officially incorporated **8th September 2016**, under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013 (stated identically across the FY2021-22, FY2022-23, FY2023-24 annual reports).
- **Registered office (confirmed evolution across years, per the annual reports themselves):**
  - FY2021-22 & FY2022-23 reports: **H.Q./H.O.: Rajali Area, Near Village Reserve, Pillangkata, Ri-Bhoi, Meghalaya**, with a **Branch Office at Latakata, Basistha, Guwahati, Assam-781029**.
  - FY2023-24 report: **Registered Address: Rajali Pillangkata, Ri-Bhoi District, Meghalaya-793101**, with an **Extension Office at Umtrynga Village, Umling Block, Ri-Bhoi District, Meghalaya**.
  - FY2024-25 report: MOSONiE is now based at **Umtrynga Village, Umling Block, Ri-Bhoi District, Meghalaya, 793101** — i.e., the former "extension office" village has become the primary address.
  - Current website (mosonie.org) lists: House of Hiren Roy Suting, Umtyrnga Village, Killing Centre, Ri Bhoi, Byrnihat, Meghalaya, 793101.
  - **This confirms the office genuinely relocated over time** (Pillangkata → Umtrynga Village) rather than being a stale/inconsistent third-party listing — the MNRE and CSR Universe profiles simply reflect the older Pillangkata-era address.
- **Geographic footprint (confirmed, per FY2024-25 report's "Geographical Outreach" page):** Operations now explicitly span **two states** — **Meghalaya** (Ri-Bhoi District: Umling, Bhoirymbong, Jirang, Umsning Blocks) and **Manipur** (Senapati District: Khabungkarong, T. Khullen/Tumuyon Khullen, Koide villages) — the Manipur locations correspond to several founding members' home villages (see roster below), suggesting the Manipur footprint is closely tied to founder networks rather than a separately-scaled program area.
- **Village/reach count (FY2022-23 "Our Reach" figures):** Umling Block — 30 villages; Jirang Block — 50 villages; Bhoiryngbong — 4 villages; Umsning — 2 villages (**86 villages total** across the 4 blocks that year). By FY2023-24, the reach summary is phrased as "District-1, Block-4" (i.e., 1 district, 4 blocks — Jirang, Umling, Bhoirymbong, Umsning), consistent with the FY2022-23 breakdown.
- **Name origin:** "MOSONiE" is a word from the **Mao tribe** of Manipur meaning **"Creation of Commonwealth"** — reflecting a philosophy of "less of me, more of us." The organization describes itself as "a small drop in the world's need for shared, responsible human initiative," seeking to "Create, Recreate, and Rejuvenate the resources around us." A related description (via RCRC partner page) elaborates that the word denotes wealth created through communal nurturing of the earth, shared "according to each person's needs in an egalitarian context," and reflects "a utopian quality of togetherness" and "reverential approach to nature."
- **Mission:** "Socio-Economic Re-Architecturing for North East India" (per LinkedIn/RCRC) / "To create, recreate and rejuvenate the resources and bring about socio Economic transformation in North East India for the most marginalized and underserved communities" (stated identically in the FY2021-22, FY2022-23, and FY2023-24 annual reports) / "Empower underserved communities through collective action towards equitable access and resource regeneration" — this last, shorter phrasing is labelled **"Purpose"** (not "Mission") in the FY2024-25 report, suggesting a deliberate re-wording during the organization's FY2024-25 strategic-planning process (see below).
- **Vision:** FY2021-22/22-23 reports: *"MOSONiE SEF envisions creating a SMART North East India"* — where **SMART** = "Sustainably Managed livelihoods for the most marginalized, underserved and unreached sections of the community who can add to the economic and wellbeing of rural areas in Northeast India" (an acronym-based vision statement not found anywhere on the current website). By FY2024-25, the vision statement has been formally changed to: **"Regeneration of common wealth towards the wellbeing of people and the planet."**
- **Values (evolution confirmed across years):**
  - FY2021-22 report: **"Hardworking, Honesty, Hospitality"** plus a separate **"Teamwork culture"** principle — each given a one-line definition (e.g., "Honesty: Honesty which reflected in our conduct with all stakeholders specially community at large").
  - FY2023-24 report: shortened to **"Hardworking, Honesty and Hospitality."**
  - FY2024-25 report (post-strategic-planning retreat): **Core Values: Integrity, Dignity and Inclusivity**; **Guiding Principle: Transparency, Solidarity, Team work** — a clear, deliberate rebrand of the organization's values framework coinciding with its first five-year strategic plan (see "Major Institutional Milestone: FY2024-25 Strategic Planning" below).
- **Geography:** Primarily **Ri-Bhoi District, Meghalaya** (core operating area for almost all programs); the FY2024-25 report's own "Geographical Outreach" page confirms the footprint also formally extends into **Senapati District, Manipur** (villages: Khabungkarong, T. Khullen, Koide) — not just "tree-planting only," as Pass 1 had inferred, though Manipur-specific program activity/metrics are still not clearly itemized separately from the Meghalaya figures in any report read so far. The "Soh Marit" black pepper livelihoods program is specifically implemented in **Umling Block**, Ri-Bhoi District.
- **Headline impact claims (current website homepage, undated/cumulative):** **15,000+ households**, **50,000+ trees planted**, **20,000+ people reached** — likely aggregated across all years and programs; see `MOSONiE_Programs.md` for the year-by-year figures now available that could be summed to sanity-check this claim.

## Major Institutional Milestone: FY2024-25 Strategic Planning

The FY2024-25 annual report frames that year as a turning point for the organization ("After nine years of growth..."):

- **ViMiVASTRA workshop (12-14 August 2024):** A three-day strategic-planning workshop facilitated by the **Roots to Branches** team (the same organization that spun off from Diya Foundation's former "IDeA" capacity-building program — see cross-reference note below). Used tools including Participatory Rural Appraisal (PRA), Key Informant Interviews (KII), "River of Life," a participatory SWOT analysis with representatives from 4 villages, and the "Conscious Full Spectrum Response (CFSR) Model" to reprioritize issues and shape a 3-year strategic action plan.
- **First-ever Annual Retreat (17-18 January 2025), Chanako Eco Camp Resort, Guwahati** — themed "Reflection, Recreation, and Visioning." Activities included reflective group discussions, a "strategic planning and team-building activity" themed around food delivery to communities, creation of a collaborative "Achievement Quilt" documenting the org's milestones/partnerships/challenges, a "Traffic Game," a "Who Am I" personal-balance exercise, and a leadership-themed video session.
- **Bridgespan-facilitated 5-year strategic plan** developed as part of the NoDE (Northeast Organizational Development for Empowerment) Program in FY2024-25.
- Together, these events produced the updated Vision/Purpose/Values wording quoted above — i.e., the language differences between the FY2021-22/22-23/23-24 reports and the FY2024-25 report are not inconsistencies but a **documented, deliberate rebrand**.

## Recent Recognition & Partnerships

- Selected as part of **Dasra's Rebuild India Fund — Cohort 11** (a pooled, flexible, long-term funding vehicle for grassroots Indian NGOs; per Dasra's own materials, Rebuild has supported 300+ NGOs since November 2022, providing multi-year flexible grants and capacity-building support).
- Partner organization of **RCRC (Responsible Coalition for Resilient Communities)** — specifically both **National RCRC** and **NE-RCRC (Northeast RCRC)**, a regional coalition MOSONiE has been an active member of since at least 2021 (attending NE-RCRC's first post-COVID physical coalition-building meeting on 9 September 2021).
- Featured by **India Development Review (IDR)** and **Centre for Systems Practice** for its Eri silk/solar spinning work (a dedicated **IDR Ground Up video**: "Women weavers find a solar way to Eri silk in Meghalaya").
- Featured in independent journalism: **101Reporters** ("In a remote Meghalayan village, one solar husker is changing fortunes, freeing up time for women") and **VillageSquare.in / CEEW** ("Solar power a money-spinner for women spinning eri silk yarn").
- Registered/listed with the **Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE)** as an implementing organization for solar livelihood interventions; MOSONiE's Executive Director gave the opening statement at an MNRE/SELCO/AREAS workshop on "Distributed Renewable Energy (DRE) for Livelihood in the Northeast Region" (22 September 2023, AIC-SELCO Foundation, Guwahati).
- Core technical/implementation partner across multiple programs: **SELCO Foundation** (also involved: **Envo Renewable Energy Services Pvt. Ltd. (ERES)** and **Soltech Solar (OPC) Pvt. Ltd.** as installation contractors, and **Meghalaya Rural Bank (MRB)** for end-user loan linkage).
- **Cross-referenced with other Northeast India NGOs researched previously:**
  - MOSONiE's team made a formal **exposure visit to Diya Foundation** (Loharghat, Assam) on **21 September 2022**, meeting **Martin Rabha, Director of Diya Foundation**, to learn Diya's livelihood-intervention and MIS/documentation model. **Romen Rabha of Diya Foundation** later returned the connection, serving as the resource person at a MOSONiE farmer consultation meeting in Timbokgre Village on 20 March 2024.
  - MOSONiE received capacity-building/incubation support from **"IDeA-the ant"** (based in Chirang, Assam) across FY2022-23 through FY2024-25 — including an **Incubation Grant for the "Soh Marit" black pepper project** (FY2022-23) and multiple residential training workshops (Project Development & Management, Livelihood Promotion, Accounting & Financial Management, Monitoring/Evaluation/Documentation, and a Leadership Retreat for the Executive Director). By FY2023-24, this same funding relationship is credited to **"Roots to Branches Foundation"** for the Soh Marit project specifically — directly confirming, from the MOSONiE side, the Diya Foundation research finding that Diya's former "IDeA" institutional-capacity-building program was spun off into the independent "Roots to Branches Foundation."
  - MOSONiE has an active peer/capacity-building relationship with **SeSTA** (referenced in SNEHPAD's own materials too — "SESTA Team Visit to Uttar Negheri Weaving Centre"), including exposure visits to SeSTA's Guwahati office (31 October 2022) and Rongjuli, Goalpara, field site (19-21 February 2024), plus SeSTA-run "write-shop" trainings for case studies/success stories.
  - MOSONiE attended a workshop featuring **Achintya Ghosh, Founder & Executive Director of KABIL** — the same "Kabil, New Delhi" organization identified as a funder in Diya Foundation's older (FY2016-17/17-18) annual reports for MGNREGA promotion and irrigation-development work.
  - **This makes at least four organizations researched in this project's file set — SNEHPAD, Diya Foundation, MOSONiE, and (by reference) The Ant/IDeA/Roots to Branches — part of the same overlapping Northeast India NGO capacity-building ecosystem** (SeSTA, NE-RCRC, IDeA-the ant/Roots to Branches, KABIL, and Dhwani Foundation all recur as shared intermediaries/funders across multiple of these organizations' own annual reports.)
- Other named FY2021-22–24-25 institutional/training partners not previously captured: **GOONJ**, **Young Indians (Guwahati Chapter)**, **Round Table** (organization), **Sustain Plus Energy Foundation**, **Azim Premji Foundation (APF)**, **Central Silk Board**, **Resham Sutra Pvt. Ltd.**, **Department of Textiles, Nongpoh (Govt. of Meghalaya)**, **SHUDDHI** (menstrual hygiene awareness partner), **BAALA** (reusable sanitary pad brand/project, FY2024-25), **PRIA** (under its "CAPSTONE" program — Financial Management and Program Planning/Monitoring/Impact-Assessment workshops), **Dhwani Foundation** (the "Niranthara Sikshak" 12-month Organisational Development program, April 2021-May 2022 — the same Dhwani Foundation OD program independently confirmed in SNEHPAD's own materials as "Niranthara Northeast (NNE-1)," suggesting Dhwani runs parallel/sequential OD cohorts across multiple Northeast India NGOs), **Bridgespan** (strategic planning, FY2024-25), **Piramal Foundation** (Aspirational District Collaboration NGO Conclave, March 2022), **GOGLA** (South Asia Forum for Distributed Energy), **AREAS**, **Vocational Training Centre, Kyrdemkulai / Animal Husbandry & Veterinary Department, Govt. of Meghalaya** (piggery training), **District Horticulture Farm, Byrnihat** (farmer exposure visits), **Assam Don Bosco University (ADBU)**, **Azim Premji University**, and **University of Science & Technology Meghalaya** (student internship sources), and **Childline Nongpoh** / **District Mental Health Programme (DMHP), Nongpoh** (child-rights and mental-health awareness sessions).
