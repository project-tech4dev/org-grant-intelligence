# Society for North East Handmade Paper Development (SNEHPAD)
## Summary of Program Areas

**Organization:** Society for North East Handmade Paper Development (SNEHPAD)
**Type:** Voluntary/Non-Governmental Organization
**Established:** 18 February 2005
**Headquarters:** Jorhat Development Block Campus, Baghchung, Jorhat, Assam, India
**Legal Status:** Registered under the Societies Registration Act XXI of 1860 (No. RS/JOR/238/B/67 of 2004-05); Registered under Income Tax Act, 1961 (12AA); Certified by the Khadi & Village Industries Commission (KVIC); ISO Certified

---

## Mission, Vision & Goal

- **Mission:** To organize and empower the rural poor by providing need-based support, inputs, training, and guidance so they can realize their inherent potential and use it to achieve prosperity and social justice.
- **Vision:** To establish a peaceful, lively, and dynamic society where every individual enjoys socio-economic equality and prosperity, encouraging active participation in development.
- **Goal:** To make poor and deprived rural individuals efficient and self-sufficient so they can lead respectable, prosperous, and purposeful lives.

---

## Background

Founded in 2005 under the leadership of Sri Jatindranath Das (founder Chairman, retired Joint Director of the Agriculture Department), Syed Altaf Hussain (founder Vice Chairman), and Sri Prakash Thakur (founder Secretary), SNEHPAD began with a focus on handmade paper and natural fibre-based cottage industries. Over nearly two decades, it evolved into a multifaceted rural development organization serving Northeast India, particularly Assam. In 2010, SNEHPAD received a **National Award for Best Rural Industries Service Centre (RISC)**.

## Key Program / Focus Areas

1. **Handmade Paper & Natural Fibre Development** (founding focus)
   - Promotion of eco-friendly, traditional handmade paper and natural fibre-based cottage industries for rural employment and entrepreneurship.

2. **Handloom Sector Development** (current primary focus)
   - Cluster Development Projects among weavers and artisans.
   - Establishment of decentralized **Common Facility Centres (CFCs)**.
   - Operation of **Yarn Banks** to support weavers.
   - Preservation of Northeast India's traditional weaving heritage while promoting women's empowerment.

3. **Self-Help Group (SHG) & Joint Liability Group (JLG) Development**
   - Formation, strengthening, and livelihood support for SHGs/JLGs, particularly among women.

4. **Sustainable Agriculture & Farm Sector Development**
   - Support to organic farmers, vermicomposting, herbal gardening, bee-rearing, and integrated farming practices.

5. **Entrepreneurship Development**
   - Entrepreneurship Awareness Programmes (EAPs)
   - Management Development Programmes (MDPs)
   - Entrepreneurship-cum-Skill Development Programmes (ESDPs)

6. **Skill Training**
   - Training for youth, women, farmers, artisans, weavers, and small producers to build practical production, quality, and market-readiness skills.
   - Pottery Cluster and Artistic Weaving Cluster training.

7. **Microfinance & Micro-Insurance Awareness**
   - Financial inclusion and awareness building among rural and marginalized communities.

8. **Integrated Village Development**
   - Holistic village-level development combining livelihoods, community organization, awareness, and financial inclusion.
   - Active Village Development Plans implemented in villages such as Borkhongia, Lujonia, Deka Gaon, and Napomuwa Gaon (Jorhat District), supported by NABARD and other partners — targeting farmers, unemployed youth, women, and SHGs through convergence with government line departments.

9. **Cluster Development Programmes**
   - Thematically diverse clusters implemented (e.g., in Bongaigaon) covering vermicomposting, herbal gardening, bee-rearing, integral education, anaemia awareness, Nasha Mukti (de-addiction) campaigns, yoga camps, and handloom training.

10. **Health & Welfare Initiatives**
    - Mission AYUSH Arogyakar: health camps, AYUSH Chaupal sessions, community herbal gardens, promotion of traditional/herbal medicine.
    - General health camps, yoga training, cattle/livestock vaccination drives.

11. **Environmental Activities**
    - Tree plantation drives and distribution of fruit-bearing saplings.

12. **Market Linkage Support**
    - Connecting small producer groups (weavers, artisans, farmers) with local and external markets to move from subsistence activity to sustainable enterprise.

13. **Tools & Equipment Distribution**
    - Provision of small tools, machinery, and equipment to needy weavers and farmers.

## Target Beneficiaries
Rural youth, women, farmers, unemployed individuals, artisans, weavers, Self-Help Groups (SHGs), Joint Liability Groups (JLGs), small producers, and poor/marginalized communities across Northeast India (primarily Assam).
