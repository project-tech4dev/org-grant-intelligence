## Key Activities & Achievements (2018-19)

### 1. Gram Panchayat Development Plan (GPDP) Training

Organized **165 Gram Panchayat-level training programmes** covering **~9,000 people** across Jorhat, Golaghat, and Majuli Districts, in collaboration with the State Institute of Rural Development (SIRD):

- **50 Day-Long Trainings on GPDP** (Titabor, Baghchung, Chipahikhola blocks) — ~5,000 individuals covered (GP members, staff, SHGs, youth/farmer clubs); focus on flagship schemes (MGNREGA, NSAP, PMAY, 14th Finance Commission Grants) and inter-department convergence.
- **112 two-day trainings on GPDP for Women SHGs** — 5,600 women SHG leaders trained, with focus on women's empowerment, convergence, and Participatory Rural Appraisal (PRA) practicals.
- **3 three-day GPDP trainings** focused on Water Conservation & Organic Farming — 150 individuals covered.

### 2. Cluster Development Initiatives

- **Bee Keeping Cluster (Majuli District, under KVIC's Honey Mission):** Awareness meet reaching 120 potential beekeepers (all women, ST/SC community); 25 women selected for a 5-day Skill Development Training (started 10 Feb 2019); all 25 trainees received 10 bee boxes/colonies each (distributed 14 March 2019) — each expected to earn ~₹80,000/year. Plan to scale to 500 women total in Majuli.
- **Pottery Cluster Development (under KVIC):**
  - **Salmora Pottery Village, Majuli:** 20 women potters (selected previous year) received Energy Efficient Potter Wheels, 2 Common Soil Refiners, and 1 Potter Bhatti — production increased ~5x.
  - **Tingrimora & Kothalbari Pottery Villages, Jorhat:** New clusters identified (185 potter families, 7 decades of tradition); 40 women potters (20/village) given 10-day Electric Wheel training; each to receive an Energy Efficient Potter Wheel + shared Soil Refiners/Bhattis.

### 3. Micro Enterprise Development Programme (MEDP)

In collaboration with Entrepreneurship Development Institute of India (EDII): **2 month-long MEDPs** covering **100 women entrepreneurs** (50 in Pottery at Panichokuwa, Jorhat; 50 in Bee Keeping at Garmur, Majuli). **All 100 trainees successfully started their own micro-enterprises.**

### 4. Handloom Cluster Development (under CSIR-NEIST Project)

- **120 weavers trained** and followed up for commercial production.
- Raw material support provided to **500+ weavers**.
- **₹6,58,437** worth of handloom products sold through SNEHPAD's own sales outlet during the year.
- **2 three-month Jacquard Loom Skill Development Trainings** (Kachukhat Gaon & Kamalpur Gaon, Titabor) — 80 weavers trained (40 each), organized into Joint Liability Groups (JLGs)/SHGs for commercial units.

### 5. NEDFi Representative Office

SNEHPAD signed an MoU with NEDFi (North Eastern Development Finance Corporation, under DoNER Ministry) to host a **NEDFi Representative Office** at its Jorhat campus — providing local entrepreneurs hassle-free access to credit (previously required traveling 300 km to Guwahati).

### 6. Celebrations / Observations

- **World Environment Day** (5 June 2019, Silikhabari Village, Titabor) — with Bharat Vikas Parishad & Social Forestry Dept: 100 fruit-tree saplings distributed to women villagers + 20 planted in public spaces.
- **Foundation Day** (19 February 2019) — flag hoisting, staff/volunteer family gathering, youth football-style match between Silikhabari Yuva Sangha and Kachukhat Village Youth Club (cash prizes awarded); 100+ villagers/volunteers attended.

### 7. Training & Capacity Building of Staff

SNEHPAD staff/volunteers attended external trainings at SIRD Guwahati, EDII Bangalore, and IIT Guwahati (on GPDP planning, MEDP trainer's training, and empanelment as rural development resource persons).

### 8. Marketing Support

Runs a **Sales Outlet in Jorhat City** for weavers/artisans to display and sell products and receive bulk orders.

### 9. Internships

Hosted **6 MSW (Master of Social Work) students from Kaziranga University** for a 1-month internship, engaged in Women & Child Development programmes.

## Summary Takeaway

SNEHPAD's 2018-19 report shows an organization deeply embedded in **grassroots governance training (GPDP)** at massive scale (165 programmes, ~9,000+ people) while simultaneously running **hands-on livelihood cluster projects** (pottery, bee-keeping, handloom weaving) that combine skills training with direct provision of productive assets (potter wheels, bee boxes, looms) — resulting in concrete, quantified outcomes (e.g., 5x production increases, 100/100 MEDP trainees starting enterprises, ₹6.58 lakh in product sales).
