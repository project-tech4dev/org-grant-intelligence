# PESCH — Annual Reports (downloaded PDFs + summaries)

People's Endeavour for Social Change (PESCH) is a grassroots NGO founded in 1995
(registered 1997) working in Tamenglong and Jiribam districts of Manipur and
Cachar district of Assam, focused on natural resource management, sustainable
livelihoods, women/youth empowerment, and children's education/rights.

Source: https://pesch.org.in/reports/annual/

## 2023-2024
Reports serving 62 villages and 7,800 households. Highlights: forest/catchment
regeneration work, SALT (Slope Agriculture Land Technology) model farming
across project and non-project areas, women's income growth via the "Rural
Haat" market outlet, and youth-led integrated/orchard farming (Longchai
Student Union). The year also focused on internal governance development
(manual, strategy, fundraising, HR, communications) through the NODE-SeSTA
initiative, plus continued construction of the organization's building through
founder-village contributions.

## 2022-2023
26th year of operation, reporting reach across 57 villages and 7,113
households. Framed around COVID-19 recovery and its economic aftershocks, with
a stated shift toward "peace with nature" — forest-food regeneration,
spring-shed development, and ecosystem restoration — alongside the continuing
four focus areas (natural resource management, entitlements access,
institution building, livelihood promotion). Includes a dedicated case-study
section and a financial statement.

## 2021-2022
Continues the pandemic-recovery narrative, again reporting reach across 62
villages/7,800 households. Emphasizes the same four flagship activities as the
following year's report almost verbatim (forest/catchment regeneration, SALT
farming, Rural Haat women's livelihoods, youth orchard integration), plus
organizational governance improvements funded through founder-village
contributions.

## 2020-2021
Reports reach across 41 villages/2,300+ households (a narrower footprint than
other years, possibly reflecting COVID-era access constraints). Lists current
donor-funded projects: an environment/entitlement program with Brot for the
World (5 Tousem villages), a Global Greengrants Fund project (15 Tousem
villages), a "One School At a Time" (OSAAT) initiative, and NABARD-supported
Farmer Producer Organization and Rural Haat work. Contains a detailed
target-vs-achievement table covering village institution-building, SHG
formation, land-rights advocacy, natural resource management, and livelihood
activities such as cotton farming and yarn value-addition support for women.

## 2018-2019
Reports interventions across two districts (Jiribam and Tamenglong, Manipur)
via four projects: an Environment Development and Management Program with
RNBA/EED, OSAAT in Aben village, an ASER education survey across 20
Tamenglong and 7 Jiribam villages, and a NABARD-supported livelihood/NRM
project. Notes growing SHG capacity in bookkeeping and livelihood
decision-making, and improved land/water/forest management by target
communities. Registered under FCRA (2007) and 12A (2006); donor base by this
point included RNBA/EED, IGSSS, RNBA/JTT, RNBA/SDTT, CENRs, NABARD, ASER and
OSAAT.

## 2016-2017
Reports reach across 41 villages, 5,125 households, and 28,128 people.
Programs included the Environment Development & Management Program
(RNBA/EED/Brot), a System of Rice Intensification and food-security
initiative, a NABARD-supported "Smokeless King Chilli" biomass dryer project
for women's SHGs at Kaiphundai village, a demonstration farm at Karuluang, and
a financial-literacy awareness camp. Includes a financial statement section.

## 2014-2015
Reports the same reach figures as 2016-17 (41 villages, 5,125 households,
28,128 people), suggesting these are carried-over baseline figures across
several years' reports. Nine active projects are listed, including Environment
Development & Management (RNBA/EED), livelihood/rights work with IGSSS,
System of Rice Intensification (Jiribam and Cachar), a Diversion-Based
Irrigation and food-security program with RNBA/Jamsetji Tata Trust, flood
response in Cachar with Habitat for Humanity India, and a natural-resources/
extractives program with CENRs.

## 2013-2014
Similar structure to 2014-15, listing seven active projects/partners (RNBA/EED,
IGSSS "PEARL" livelihood-rights project, RNBA/JTT food security, Habitat for
Humanity India flood response, CENRs natural-resource management, and a
self-funded micro-entrepreneurship program). Notes PESCH's origin story and
that its donor base had grown to five partners by this point.

## 2008-2009
A project-level (not full organizational) report covering seven villages in
Jiribam (Imphal East) and Cachar (Assam) with roughly 3,000 people, focused on
identifying livelihood, empowerment, and natural-resource-management issues
(poor livestock/vegetable practices, lack of capital, water scarcity, land
rights/recognition disputes, forest denudation) and first-year activities such
as fishery pond construction, ferry-boat procurement, ring-well/spring-spot
water development, and village disaster/action planning.

## 2007-2008
An 18-month progress report (Oct 2006–March 2008) for the "Environment
Development and Management Program," implemented by PESCH as one of six
partner NGOs facilitated by RNBA and funded by EED (Germany). Covers the same
seven target villages (~3,406 people, 487 families) across Cachar (Assam) and
Tamenglong/Jiribam (Manipur), describing baseline PRA/participatory
micro-planning work, formation of ~47 SHGs, and early pilot NRM and livelihood
activities (e.g. a women's SHG banana farm).

## 2006-2007
PESCH's earliest available annual report, covering the same target
geography and demographic profile (Rongmei, Liangmei, Zeme, Hmar, Kuki, and
Cachari communities). At this stage the organization had 8 staff (3
professional, 3 support, 2 volunteer) and 9 board members, working across
three focus areas: gender/women's SHGs, natural resource management, and
primary education.
