# People's Endeavour for Social Changes (PESCH) — Organization Profile

> This document is updated continuously as new information is found. See `PESCH_Programs.md` for program-area detail. **Unlike several other Northeast India NGOs researched in this project (SNEHPAD, Diya Foundation, Projonmo), PESCH's own website document repository is fully functional** — a genuinely deep archive of 13 Annual Reports (FY2006-07 through FY2023-24) was found and downloaded, giving this research an unusually strong evidentiary base.

## Organization Overview

- **Full name:** People's Endeavour for Social Changes (PESCH) — note the official name uses "Change**s**" (plural), though it is very commonly written/referred to as "People's Endeavour for Social Change" (singular), including in the user's own framing of this research request.
- **Founded:** **5 August 1995** — twenty-eight (28) leaders from the **Rongmei, Riang, Hmar, and Kuki** communities came together across **Lungbungzang Lakhichera (Cachar, Assam)**, and **Jiribam and Tamenglong (Manipur)** to launch grassroots development initiatives. The organization was first formed as **PAFCORD — People's Action for Comprehensive Rural Development** — headquartered in Lungbungzang village.
- **Renamed & Registered:** On **23 February 1997**, the organization adopted its current identity, **People's Endeavour for Social Changes (PESCH)**, and was **officially registered under the Societies Registration Act on 3 March 1997**.
- **Certification:** **ISO-certified since 2024** (Certification No. 305024012301Q of 2024).
- **Headquarters:** Thomas Gangmei Building (Church Road), Spring Valley, Ward No. 1, Tamenglong, H/Q Tamenglong District, Manipur, PIN 795141 (per The CSR Universe profile).
- **Geography of operation:** **Tamenglong and Jiribam districts, Manipur**, and **Cachar district, Assam** — confirmed consistently across the official website's founding story and third-party profiles.
- **Contact:** office.pesch@gmail.com (per the Contact page, which auto-generates an email rather than using a web form).

## Mission & Approach

- **Self-description:** "A holistic and participatory approach to development, prioritizing sustainable livelihoods, natural resource management, women's economic inclusion, value education and community-led institutional strengthening."
- **"Why Choose Us" framing (official website):**
  - Deep Community Roots and Trust — over 27 years of work (as of website's writing) building long-term relationships with tribal councils, women's groups, youth, and village elders.
  - Proven Track Record: sustainable agriculture (SALT, kitchen gardens, agroforestry), GI-tagged produce promotion, livestock bank models, women/youth skill development, rural haats and market linkages, community education and forest protection.
  - Holistic and Integrated Approach across livelihoods/NRM, women's empowerment/institution-building, climate adaptation/disaster resilience, and education/child protection/rights-based development.
  - Focus on Indigenous Knowledge and Local Innovation — respecting/revitalizing indigenous knowledge in farming, forest conservation, and conflict resolution.
  - Youth and Women as Drivers of Change — centred in village planning, project design, and implementation.
  - Flexible, Scalable, and Collaborative — able to pilot in a single hamlet or scale proven models across districts.
- **Notable local produce/heritage focus:** GI-tagged **Tamenglong Orange** and **Sibilong Naga Chilli** (also referred to as "Naga King Chilli"); PESCH runs an annual **"Chilli & Orange Festival"** (2025 edition referenced on the website) to celebrate and promote these products, empower local producers/SHGs/youth, and support rural entrepreneurs.
- **Villages named as focal points for cultural/economic revitalization:** **Sibilong** and **Nungkao**.

## Named Partners, Funders & Government Relationships

Per the official website's "Trusted by donors, networks and government" section and "Award" page:
- **Azim Premji Foundation**
- **SELCO Foundation**
- **Government of Manipur**
- **SeSTA** (a Northeast India NGO capacity-building intermediary also found supporting MOSONiE, Diya Foundation, and SNEHPAD in this project's earlier research — further confirming SeSTA's role as a cross-cutting regional support organization)
- **ASER Centre** (India) / **ASER Centre Manipur** — education-assessment partner (ASER = Annual Status of Education Report)
- **NABARD** (National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development)
- **Rongmei Naga Baptist Association (RNBA)** — per The CSR Universe, PESCH has worked under RNBA since **2007**; RNBA in turn lists PESCH as one of its core **"Strategic Partner NGOs"** (alongside ~15 other Manipur/Nagaland-based NGOs, including People's Resource Development Association (PRDA), Development Agency for Tribal People (DATP), and others — indicating PESCH operates within a broader RNBA-coordinated network of grassroots NGOs).
- **Recognition/appreciation from government officials (per Award page):** Appreciation certificate from the **Deputy Commissioner (DC), Tamenglong**; recognition involving **Awangbow Newmai** (Forest & Environment Minister, Manipur); **Koshoni Phimu** (Under Secretary, Social Welfare, Govt. of Manipur); **D. Meidinbui** (Sub-Divisional Officer, Tousem); and the **Additional Deputy Commissioner, Jiribam**.
