## About Us

Ajagar Social Circle (ASC) is an initiative launched by **eight like-minded young practitioners** with a shared vision to drive socio-economic change, support low-income communities, and expand livelihood opportunities in the northeastern region of India. ASC is a grassroots organization that has built experience across multiple sectors, with **livelihood promotion as its core focus since inception**, complemented by initiatives in education, skill development, and relief & rehabilitation.

ASC has mobilized resources from partners including NABARD, NEDFi, GIZ, Dasra, Ashraya Hastha Trust, World Bank, Tata Trusts, NRTT, Ford Foundation, and government agencies (Goalpara District Administration, PWD, PHE, Agriculture Department, Fisheries Department).

**Current operational geography:** Assam (Goalpara, Bongaigaon, and Dhubri — aspirational districts — plus Chirang district under BTAD) and Meghalaya. Overall footprint: **2 states, 9 districts, 31 blocks, 393 villages**.

**Communities served:** Garo, Rabha, Tribal, and minority communities, and other vulnerable/underserved groups.

## Vision & Mission

**Vision:** We envision a sustainable future for the North East Region, where underserved communities thrive with opportunities and are resilient to live a dignified life.

**Mission:** To build and restore sustainable livelihoods of vulnerable and disaster-affected communities in the Northeast Region.

*(Earlier/original Vision statement, per FY2023-24 Annual Report: "To be the most respected and preferred organisation of the North Eastern region with exemplary community service, by serving 75,000 unserved and underserved people by 2025." — ASC's FY2024-25 report notes this original target was achieved, prompting the adoption of the new vision/mission statements above.)*

### Core Values
- **Community Ownership and Respect for Identity** — honoring the knowledge, culture, and identity of the communities served
- **Commitment and Compassion** — driven by strong volunteerism and unwavering commitment to individual dignity
- **Resilience and Adaptability** — building back stronger, adapting to fragile/changing contexts
- **Collaboration and Partnership** — achieving more by working with communities, governments, and institutions
- **Sustainability and Stewardship** — long-term stewardship of livelihoods and ecosystems
- **Integrity and Accountability** — accountable to communities served, committed to transparency

*(An earlier values list, per the "Why ASC?" framing, also references: Active teamwork, Just and Fair in actions, Accountability & discipline, Genuine concern, Acceptance toward change, Respect for all.)*

### Why ASC? (Organizational Differentiators, per official site)
1. **Grassroots Presence & Deep Community Connect** — deep-rooted presence across Northeast India with Garo, Rabha, tribal, and minority communities
2. **Livelihood Restoration & Resilience** — rebuilding livelihoods for communities affected by conflict, disasters, and fragility
3. **Strategic Partnerships & Systems Linkage** — links communities to government social security schemes and entitlements
4. **Capacity Building & Market Integration** — improved skills, technology, and market linkages for farmers/entrepreneurs

## History / Genesis ("Genesis of Ajagar")

| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 1998 | Eight unemployed youths from Agia locality started coal trading for self-employment — the initiative failed |
| 1999 | Shifted to agriculture, cultivating HYV paddy seed on 10 acres of land at Ajagar foothill |
| 2000 | Fully engaged in agriculture; formed **"Ajagar Associate"** under the Farming Registration Act |
| 2001 | Expanded into social activities; registered **"Ajagar Social Circle"** under the Societies Registration Act, 1860; received first rural development activity under DRDA, Goalpara |
| 2002 | Received first Government project — Jinjiram Watershed under NWDPRA through DRDA, Goalpara |
| 2004 | Received SHG Bank Linkage Programme support from NABARD; implemented Haldidara Watershed Project under NWDPRA |
| 2005 | Became **Nodal NGO** under the Assam Agricultural Competitiveness Project (AACP), funded by the **World Bank** |
| 2007 | Established Microfinance Unit ("Ajagar Microfinance"); formed **Ajagar Agro Producer Co. Ltd.** (marketing/technical support to farmers) under Company Registration Act; established **Ajagar Valley Academy** (school) |
| 2008 | Started Micro Insurance Unit in partnership with **LIC** |
| 2009 | Became representative of **North Eastern Finance Development Corporation Ltd. (NEDFi)** |
| 2010 | Involved in disaster relief and rehabilitation programmes |
| 2011 | Selected as **Field NGO** under the RCH Programme of NRHM |
| 2012 | Started livelihood projects with Indian Grameen Services and NABARD |
| 2013 | Received first **international grants** from Mercy Corps, ActionAid, and Ford Foundation |
| 2014 | Entered the education sector with development projects for minority communities |
| 2015 | Began transformation of Microfinance Unit from Society to NBFC-MFI |
| 2017 | Obtained **RBI license** and started operations as NBFC-MFI ("Ajagar Finance Pvt. Ltd.") |
| 2018 | Established **Ajagar Marketing Private Limited** to support agricultural product marketing |
| 2020 | Set up **Rural Mart** at Agia Bazar with NABARD support; promoted **Ajagar Adarsh Agro Producer Ltd.** (FPO) |
| 2022 | Merged Rural Mart with **GoalMart** in collaboration with Goalpara District Administration and ASRLM |
| 2023 | FCRA registration **renewed for another 5 years**; first-time receipt of **unrestricted funding** under the Rebuild India Fund |
| 2024 | Expanded initiatives into sustainable agriculture, education, and skill development; organization marked **25 years** of service |
| 2025 | Secured NABARD grant under the **m-Suwidha programme** (sustainable livelihood skills for 300 SHG/JLG members across 8 blocks, Goalpara) |
| 2026 | Received NABARD sanction for **FPO promotion** under the PODF-ID fund |

*(Note: per the FY2023-24 report, the microfinance NBFC entity "Ajagar Finance Pvt. Ltd." had ceased microfinance activities as of 2023.)*

## What ASC Does (Thematic Areas)

1. **Livelihood** (`/livelihood`) — sustainable agriculture, natural resource management, climate-resilient farming, livestock/fish farming diversification, handloom weaving support (training, design, market linkages), Family-Based Livelihood Planning, convergence with government schemes
2. **Skill Development** (`/skillDevelopment`) — vocational training in rubber tapping, weaving, entrepreneurship, agro-based livelihoods for rural youth and women
3. **Relief and Rehabilitation** (`/reliefRehabilate`) — disaster relief, recovery and preparedness (floods, ethnic conflict rehabilitation); District Disaster Response & Information Centre partner (Goalpara)
4. **Education — Ajagar Valley Academy** (`/education`) — a full K-12 school (established 2007) plus outreach programmes like "Mission Education" and "Every Child in School"
