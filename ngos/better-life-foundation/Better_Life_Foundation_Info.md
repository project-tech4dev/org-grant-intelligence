This profile is exclusively about the Nagaland-based **Better Life Foundation (BLF)** working in Tuensang & Kiphire districts.

> **Verification note on founding details:** This research was originally prompted by a query describing an organization "established in 1991," "Registered Society," "HQ: Senapati District, Manipur," with "12A: Yes," "80G: Likely," "FCRA: Active," and a connection to "Nagaland / Tuensang & Kiphire Districts." Having now directly reviewed BLF's official website in full, **BLF's own site clearly and consistently states it was established in 2009** (not 1991) and is headquartered in **Dimapur, Nagaland** (not Senapati District, Manipur). The **Tuensang & Kiphire Districts connection is an exact, strong match** — BLF's core Livelihood, Marketing & Value Chain, and Shodh Yatra programmes are all explicitly located there. The **Registered Society, 12A, and 80G statuses are also confirmed** directly from the official site (see below). **FCRA status could not be confirmed** — no FCRA registration number or explicit "FCRA Active" statement was found anywhere on the official site despite a full review of all pages. This discrepancy (1991 vs. 2009; Senapati/Manipur vs. Dimapur/Nagaland) is reported honestly rather than silently corrected, in case the original source was referring to a related-but-different entity or an earlier/informal precursor not documented on the current website.

## About Us / History

> **Historical source note:** An early, archival BLF web presence was located at `chungtor.blogspot.com` ("Better Life Foundation: People behind the project"), describing BLF as a "Public Benefit Organization" and listing its original team — useful for understanding organizational evolution over ~15+ years:
> - Sethrichem Sangtam — Founder and Managing Director
> - Ms. Lidingla Sangtam — Co-Founder and President (note: her role/surname as listed here differs slightly from the current official site's "Lidingla Sangtam Namjin, Operations Lead of Environmental Sustainability & Livelihood" — consistent with a person's role evolving over time within the same organization, not a different individual)
> - Er. T. Sao — Advisor and Donor; Executive Engineer, Government of Nagaland
> - Sashi Naga — Treasurer; also Vice President, Eastern Nagaland Peoples' Organization
> - Yukhaba Sangtam — Advisor (in this early listing) — **notably, this is the same individual who now serves as President of BLF's Board of Directors**, indicating 15+ years of continuous involvement from an early advisory role to the top governance position
> - A named 6-person "Bare-foot Veterinary team"
> - A 2-person "Farmers' Club of NABARD" team (Program Manager and Assistant Program Manager) — **confirming that BLF's partnership with NABARD dates back to the organization's earliest years**, not a recent development

The Better Life Foundation (BLF) was established in **2009** by a group of young, educated leaders from marginalized indigenous communities in Nagaland:

- **Founder:** Mr. Sethrichem Sangtam
- **Co-founder:** Mrs. Lidingla Namjin (Lidingla Sangtam Namjin)
- **Co-founder:** Mr. Tronglichem Sangtam

The founders had diverse professional backgrounds in Management, Community Development, and Finance, and had gained experience working abroad, but chose to return to Nagaland to address developmental challenges in their home region.

**Early initiatives** (from inception) included: health camps, educational support, and sustainable livelihood projects such as food forests, apiculture, goat farming, rabbitry, piggery, backyard poultry, and cultivation of high-value crops (large cardamom, ginger, mushrooms).

**Evolution over time:** BLF has broadened its focus to two additional key areas — **biodiversity conservation** (protecting/restoring natural ecosystems) and **preservation of Indigenous cultural heritage** (safeguarding traditions, languages, and cultural practices of Nagaland's communities).

### Founder Bio Highlights

- **Sethrichem Sangtam:** Attended the **National Law School of India University (Bangalore)** before dropping out; went on to serve in **New York as a member of the Global Youth Advisory Panel of the United Nations Population Fund (UNFPA)**, and separately had secured a U.S. H1B work visa — but chose to return to Nagaland to serve his community. Founded BLF in 2009; has helped establish over 500 Self Help Groups and multiple Farmers' Producer Cooperatives; advocate for biodiversity/sustainable farming as alternatives to shifting (jhum) cultivation.
  - **Awarded the first-ever Rohini Nayyar Prize (2022)** for outstanding contribution to rural development by an individual aged 40 or under — presented by NITI Aayog Vice Chairman Suman Bery, New Delhi, 20 December 2022. Verified details (via Nagaland Tribune, 21 Dec 2022): the prize carries a **cash award of ₹10 lakh, a citation, and a trophy**; awarded annually by the **Nayyar Foundation for Social and Economic Purpose** (named for the late Dr. Rohini Nayyar, an economist credited as "the woman behind MGNREGA"; foundation directors include **Deepak Nayyar**, Emeritus Professor of Economics, JNU, and **Dhiraj Nayyar**, Director of Economics and Policy, Vedanta Resources). Selection jury: **Ashok Khosla** (Founder, Development Alternatives), **Rajesh Tandon** (Founder, PRIA), and **Renana Jhabvala** (National Coordinator, SEWA).
  - Also serves as a **LEAD India Fellow**.
  - **Precise, dated beneficiary figure (Nagaland Tribune, Dec 2022):** "Sangtam works with **1,200 marginal farmers and their families** in eastern Nagaland" — this is a more specific, dated figure from an independent press source than the undated "2,000+ marginal families" figure quoted on BLF's own website (see companion Programs & Reports file for a full reconciliation discussion of these two figures).
  - **LinkedIn (Sangtam's own profile) confirms his role as "Managing Director, Better Life Foundation, May 2009 – Present"** — pinning down the founding month as May 2009. *(Note: a separate, older archival source — an early BLF blog hosted at chungtor.blogspot.com — instead states the organization was "founded in July 2009." This 2-month discrepancy between sources is minor and likely reflects the difference between an internal founding decision and formal registration, but is noted for completeness rather than resolved.)*
  - **New fact — 2023 Nagaland Legislative Assembly election candidacy:** Official Election Commission-sourced affidavit records (via myneta.info, run by the Association for Democratic Reforms) show Sethrichem Sangtam contested the **2023 Nagaland Assembly election** from the **Longkhim Chare (ST), Tuensang** constituency — the same constituency covering BLF's core operating area (Longkhim Block) — representing the **Republican Party of India (Athawale)**. The affidavit itself describes his profession as "Social Entrepreneur **and former chief functionary officer of Better Life Foundation NGO**" — a notable contrast with his LinkedIn profile, which lists his BLF Managing Director role as continuing "to Present" without interruption. This discrepancy (whether he formally stepped back from BLF leadership during/after the 2023 election, as the affidavit's "former" wording might suggest, or whether this was simply affidavit phrasing) is not resolved by the sources reviewed.

- **Tronglichem Sangtam:** Co-founder and Chief Functionary/Secretary; background in management; 12+ years in community development (livelihood, health, financial inclusion, conservation); member of the Consultative Committee of the Food Corporation of India.

## Vision, Mission & Goals

**Vision:** "To create thriving, self-reliant marginal communities through sustainable and inclusive growth."

**Mission:** "To empower communities through innovative and sustainable initiatives in livelihood generation, women & youth empowerment, healthcare, education, biodiversity conservation, and cultural preservation. By fostering resilience, self-reliance, and inclusive growth, we aim to catalyze holistic development and elevate the quality of life for marginal communities across regions."

**Goals:** Transform the lives of **20,000 marginalized families by 2034** (BLF's 25th anniversary), through strategic partnerships, sustainable solutions, and community empowerment.

### 10-Year Roadmap (as stated on official site)

- Expand Farmer Innovation Centre to 2 more locations
- Build one "Vital Care" wellness centre (physiology/old-age care, mental health/psychology point, drug dependency rehabilitation point)
- Set up/strengthen 2 cultural heritage centers for learning and tourism
- Develop a Naga Heritage Curriculum Model ("Roots to Routes") integrating Indigenous knowledge into formal education
- Bring 8,000 farmers into regenerative agriculture, integrated into FPOs
- Link 1,500 SHGs to fair markets
- Establish 3 biodiversity conservation sites protecting Nagaland's most vulnerable ecosystems
- Provide vocational training to 1,000 youth for entrepreneurship/formal employment
- Create/strengthen 3 more FPOs (Farmer Producer Organizations)

## Awards & Recognition

- **Rohini Nayyar Prize 2022** (first-ever recipient) — awarded to Founder Sethrichem Sangtam for outstanding contribution to rural development, presented by NITI Aayog Vice Chairman Suman Bery, New Delhi, 20 December 2022
- **ICRT (International Centre for Responsible Tourism) Responsible Tourism Awards 2025** — won at **two levels**, confirmed via icrt.global and independent travel-press coverage:
  - **Gold Award, ICRT Indian Subcontinent Awards** (regional level) — "Peace, Understanding and Inclusivity" category, awarded **13 September 2025 in New Delhi**, jointly presented by ICRT and Incredible India
  - **Silver Award (2nd place), ICRT Global Responsible Tourism Awards 2025** (global level, sponsored by Sabre) — same "Peace, Understanding & Inclusion" category, awarded **3 November 2025 in London**; BLF advanced to the global round automatically as a regional Gold winner, alongside Gold winners from WTM Africa, LATA Expo, ICRTH Southeast Asia, and ICRT Europe. **Judging panel chair: Emeritus Professor Harold Goodwin.** Award accepted by Sethrichem Sangtam (described in coverage as BLF's "founder-director" / MD).
  - **Notable programme detail surfaced by this award coverage:** BLF's responsible-tourism model includes training **former insurgents in the region as local tour guides**, explicitly framed as a peacebuilding and reconciliation mechanism between previously divided communities — a significant detail not found anywhere on BLF's own website (see `Better_Life_Foundation_Programs_and_Reports.md`, Programme 7, for full discussion).
- Founder Sethrichem Sangtam invited as panel speaker at the **UNESCO Sub-Regional (Asia) 50 Years of World Heritage Conference**, Bhopal, 17-18 April 2023
