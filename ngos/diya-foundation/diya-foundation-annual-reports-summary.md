# Diya Foundation — Annual Reports Summary

Based on 2 fully-read Annual Activity Reports (2020-21, 2021-22) plus partial reads of 2017-18 and 2014-16. All PDFs downloaded from diyafoundation.in.

---

## FY 2017-18 (Bi-annual report covering 2016-17 & 2017-18)

**Overview:** Early-stage report; org still building its livelihood-first model in Kamrup Rural (Assam) and Ri-Bhoi (Meghalaya).

**Programs:** Homestead development (Black Pepper, Assam Lemon, Pineapple, Banana, Arecanut); organic cultivation (compost, irrigation, SRI); livestock (Piggery, Goattery, Desi Poultry with low-cost sheds); Maternal Health awareness (Respectful Maternity Care); sanitation (latrines, drinking water); Digital Literacy (Internet Saathi, GVM Nalbari) reaching 54,600 women; MGNREGA sensitization; Silai Schools (USHA International).

**Scale:** 2,500 families across 86 villages (Umrangso, Rabha Hasong Autonomous Council area, Kamrup Rural to Goalpara, Assam + Ri-Bhoi, Meghalaya).

**Funders (project-tagged):** Sir Dorabji Tata Trust (Mumbai), National Foundation for India (New Delhi), Paul Hamlyn Foundation (London), Centre for Catalyzing Change (New Delhi), GVM Nalbari, Habitat for Humanity India, Kabil (New Delhi), USHA International.

**Governance:** Board: Banteilut L. Nongbri (Chairman), Sarat Ch. Das, Dilip Deka, Kripaljyoti Mazumdar, Lumlang Nengnong (Member at this stage — became Treasurer later), M. Martin Rabha (Secretary), Eliza Boro.

## FY 2020-21 (10th Anniversary Year)

**Overview:** Report celebrates "10th year of services to humanity." Three core focus areas: Livelihood Promotion, Health, Education.

**Programs:**
- **Livestock**: 1,200 families in Piggery/Goattery/Poultry/Duckery; 800 households earning ₹50,000-70,000/yr additionally.
- **Horticulture**: 2,000 households; 300 progressive commercial farmers.
- **Spices**: 2,000 households adopted Black Pepper/Ginger/Turmeric (targeted at elephant-affected areas).
- **Sericulture**: 500 households (Eri & Muga silk), ₹3,500-4,000/month additional income.
- **Renewable Energy**: 100 entrepreneurs supported via solar solutions.
- **Maternal Health**: 1,000 midwives engaged across Kamrup, Goalpara, Darrang via "What Midwifery Want" campaign; holds White Ribbon Alliance Assam secretariat.
- **Mental Health**: partnership with **The ANT** — 280+ patients registered, 55-60% regular attendance, 85-90% treatment success.
- **Education**: 550 students in remedial/creative learning.
- **COVID response**: ration/food to 3,000 households; doorstep medicine to 150 mental-health patients.
- **Institution Building**: 85 women SHGs promoted; 2 Farmer Producer Companies formed — Rangsang Agro Producer Company Ltd (600 members, spices) and Mapakkai Agro Producer Company Ltd (500 members, sericulture/livestock).

**Governance:** Board expanded to include Eliza Boro (Founder, MASK). Secretary: M. Martin Rabha.

**Financials:** "Financials 2020-2021" section present but image-only/non-extractable.

## FY 2021-22

**Overview:** Continued scale-up; new DRR (Disaster Risk Reduction) program launched in flood-prone Darrang District.

**Milestones:**
- 6,500 small & marginal households covered
- 250 entrepreneurs created around Decentralized Renewable Energy
- 631 children reached via education/child-rights program (10 villages)
- 60,550 Black Pepper plants + 55,000 Arecanut plants + 9,000 horticultural plants (mango, litchi, amla, guava) planted/raised

**Programs:**
- **Animal Husbandry**: 1,500 families; 550 households earning ₹25,000-30,000/yr extra; partnership with **RILEM** (Regional Institute of Livestock Entrepreneurship & Management, Govt. of Assam).
- **Agriculture/Horticulture**: 2,000 households; 800 commercial farmers; 1,200 households intercropped spices; 3,500 households total in spice cultivation; Black Pepper sold at ₹300-450/kg.
- **Education**: 24 primary schools across 18 villages in 2 blocks of Kamrup District; 1,500 children directly reached; focus on learning abilities, communication, critical thinking, confidence-building.
- **Institution Building**: 2 Farmer Producer Companies, **180 Women SHGs**, **85 Farmers Interest Groups (FIGs)**; FPCs leveraged **SFURTI Scheme** (MSME Dept, Govt of India) for Common Service Centres; also linked to **APART** and **ARIAS Society** (Govt of Assam programs); FIGs linked to banks.
- **COVID-19 response**: rations/nutrition to low-income families, daily laborers, homeless, pregnant/lactating mothers, PWDs; ~80 mental-health patients (Assam + Meghalaya) received doorstep medicine delivery during lockdown.

## Cross-Report Observations

1. **Consistent leadership**: Unlike The ANT, Diya Foundation has had stable leadership under founder M. Martin Rabha (Secretary) throughout all reports reviewed (2017-18 through 2021-22) — no leadership transitions noted.
2. **Steady scale growth**: 2,500 families (2017-18) → ~2,000-6,500 households per major program (2020-21/2021-22) → 10,500+ households cumulative (current, per website).
3. **Institutional maturation**: From informal SHGs (2017-18) to formal Farmer Producer Companies with government scheme linkages (SFURTI, APART, ARIAS) by 2021-22.
4. **Funder evolution**: Early funders were large Indian/international foundations (Tata Trust, NFI, Paul Hamlyn, C3) working on discrete projects; more recent partnerships (2021+) shifted toward ecosystem/network funders (Dasra's Rebuild India Fund, Child Aid Network via FCRA, SELCO Foundation, The Goat Trust) suggesting a shift toward general operating support and technical partnerships.
5. **Mental health program** (via The ANT partnership) appears to be a relatively recent addition (~2019-2020 onward) compared to the org's founding livelihood focus (2011).
6. **Financial transparency gap**: Every annual report includes a "Financial Outlook/Financials" section, but all are image-based/scanned with no extractable text — consistent limitation across all years reviewed.
