## 3. Program Areas (3 core thematic verticals)

### A. Livelihood Promotion (primary focus)
- **Animal Husbandry**: Piggery, Poultry, Goattery, Duckery with low-cost improved sheds; ~1,200-1,500 families directly involved; 550-800 households earning additional ₹25,000-70,000/year from livestock sales.
- **Horticulture**: Arecanut, Banana, Lemon, Pineapple — homestead-land intercropping with spices/sericulture; ~2,000 households adopted; 300-800 farmers turned "progressive"/commercial.
- **Spice Promotion**: Black Pepper, Ginger, Turmeric — targeted at households affected by wild elephants/animals; up to 3,500 households adopted; ₹35,000-70,000/season additional income from Black Pepper alone.
- **Sericulture**: Eri and Muga silk rearing (Rabha tribal communities); ~500 households; ₹3,500-4,000/month additional income.
- **Decentralized Renewable Energy (DRE)**: solar-powered livelihoods for ~100-250 small entrepreneurs (tailoring, Eri spinning, irrigation, elephant repellers, refrigeration, egg incubators, rice hullers, spice grinding) across Assam (Kamrup Rural, Goalpara, Darrang) and Meghalaya (Ri-Bhoi border areas). Partner: **SELCO Foundation**.
- **Farmer Producer Companies**: Co-promoted **Rangsang Agro Producer Company Ltd** (600 members/300 shareholders; spices — black pepper, turmeric, ginger; formed 2020) and **Mapakkai Agro Producer Company Ltd** (500 members/220 shareholders; sericulture, livestock).
- **Pashu Sakhi (Livestock Cadre)**: community women trained as para-veterinary workers for doorstep goat/pig healthcare (partner: **The Goat Trust**, Dept. of Animal Husbandry & Veterinary, Govt. of Assam).

### B. Health & Hygiene
- **Maternal Health**: Respectful Maternity Care advocacy; ASHA worker sensitization; holds the **Assam chapter secretariat of The White Ribbon Alliance India**; ~1,000 midwives engaged across Kamrup, Goalpara, Darrang districts via "What Midwifery Want" campaign.
- **Mental Health**: In partnership with **The ANT** (since ~2019-2020) under Project MITA — providing low-cost mental illness treatment camps in Kamrup Rural District. As of 2020-21: 280+ patients registered, 55-60% regular monthly attendance, 85-90% treatment success/control rate. Treats Schizophrenia, schizotypal/delusional disorders, Somatoform disorders, Epilepsy.

### C. Education & Literacy
- Works with Rabha, Bodo, Garo, Tea-tribe, and Assamese communities in forest villages and tea gardens of Kamrup District.
- Directly supports 23 primary schools across 25 villages (within 13km of HQ), covering 931 children aged 6-11.
- Remedial classes and creative learning for ~550-631 students; teacher capacity-building workshops; community/SMC engagement to reduce dropout and absenteeism.

### D. Institution Building
- Promoted 85+ women's Self-Help Groups (SHGs) (180 by 2021-22), federated into Village Organizations, linked to **Assam State Rural Livelihood Mission (ASRLM)**.
- By 2021-22: 2 Farmer Producer Companies, 180 Women SHGs, 85 Farmers Interest Groups (FIGs) — FPCs leveraged the **SFURTI Scheme** (MSME Dept., Govt. of India) for Common Service Centres, plus **APART** and **ARIAS Society** (Govt. of Assam) linkages; FIGs linked with banks.

### E. Humanitarian/Disaster Response
- COVID-19: ration/dry food support to 3,000 households; doorstep medicine delivery to 150 mental-health patients during lockdown.
- Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR) program launched in Darrang District (flood-prone area).
