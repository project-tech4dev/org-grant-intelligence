## 1. Overview of the Three Reports

These are the only three Annual Administration Reports currently published on the official website, covering **1999–2000, 2000–2001, and 2001–2002**. Each report follows a consistent structure: institutional set-up, then a programme-by-programme account of activities, beneficiaries, and expenditure for that financial year. No more recent Annual Administration Report is published on the site as of this review (the site's "Reports and Publications" page lists only these three).

**Consistent institutional set-up described across all three reports:**
- **At State Level:** One Directorate; One State ICDS Cell; One Deaf & Mute School; One Blind School; One Welfare Training Institute; One Anganwadi Training Centre; One Observation Home/Special Home/Juvenile Home; One State Bal Bhavan; One Working Ladies Hostel.
- **At District Level:** District Social Welfare Offices at all 9 Districts; District ICDS Cells at Imphal, Churachandpur, Ukhrul, Senapati, and twin-district cells covering Chandel & Thoubal and Tamenglong & Bishnupur.
- **At Block/Project Level:** 34 ICDS Projects (including 1 Urban Project) covering all blocks of the state.

## 2. Annual Administration Report — 1999–2000

### Institutional Network (as of this report)
**At State Headquarters:** Govt. Ideal Blind School (Takyelpat); Govt. Deaf & Mute School (Takyelpat); Welfare Training Institute (Takyelpat); Anganwadi Training Centre (Takyelpat); Working Ladies Hostel (Takyelpat); State ICDS Cell; State Bal Bhavan (Khuman Lampak).

**At District Level:** 9 District Social Welfare Offices; 4 District ICDS Cells (Imphal, Ukhrul, Senapati, Churachandpur); 2 Twin District ICDS Cells (Thoubal/Chandel, Bishnupur/Tamenglong).

**At Block Level:** Block-level ICDS Projects across Imphal East-I/II, Imphal West-I/II, Jiribam, Bishnupur, Moirang, Thoubal, Kakching, Churachandpur, Thanlon, Henglep, Singngat, Parbung, Samulamlan, Chandel, Chakpikarong, Tengnoupal, Machi, Ukhrul, Phungyar, Kasom Khullen, Kamjong, Chingai, Mao, Kangpokpi, Saikul, Purul, Pao-Mata, Tamenglong, Tamei, Nungba, Tousem, and Imphal City (Urban).

### Programme Performance, 1999–2000

**Women Welfare:**
- Raja Dumbra Destitute Women Home (run by All Manipur Women's Association) given State grant of **Rs. 20,000**.
- State-run Working Ladies Hostel accommodated **9 low-paid employees** (House Rent @ 5% of pay/month).
- Manipur State Women Development Corporation Ltd. trained women (incl. school dropouts) in handloom weaving, typewriting/shorthand, readymade garments, mosquito net weaving, leather, beauty culture, computers — with NGOs, under GoI/NORAD/STEP schemes. **Rs. 10.00 lakhs** grant given to the Corporation.

**Child Welfare:**
- **8 NGOs** ran Children's Homes for destitute/orphan children from Hills and plains; **Rs. 6.46 lakhs spent, benefiting ~287 children**.
- State Bal Bhavan (Khuman Lampak) offered Song & Dance, Sitar/Guitar/Tabla, painting, soft toy making, computer education, Library-cum-Doll House, Children's Park, Science Park; funded by National Bal Bhavan, India. **2 children — Piyaibala Ayam and M. Jotin — received the National Balshree Awards, 1999** (creative writing; innovative science respectively), honoured by the President of India. A new Science Park installed with State Science & Technology Department assistance.
- **284 dependent/fostered children** given education scholarships @ Rs. 60/month; **Rs. 2.05 lakhs spent**.
- **BADP Project** (Integrated Development Programme for Women & Children — Girl Child and Adolescent Girls) launched at the border town of **Moreh** (end of 1998–99) to reduce gender disparities for border-area girls; ran the "Comprehensive Training Programme" (hygiene, community health, social work, food/nutrition education, family welfare, gender/women's rights, maternal-child health, Rights of the Child, drugs/AIDS awareness, vocational training). **30 trained Adolescent Girls formed Self-Help Groups**; plans for a dedicated self-sustaining society with credit facilities via the State Women Dev. Corporation and banks. **Rs. 10.00 lakhs spent across 1998–99 and 1999–2000.**
- **Universal Children's Day** celebrated 14 Nov 1999 at 1st Manipur Rifles Parade Ground.
- **2-day UNICEF-sponsored workshop on "Right of Child"** (with Manipur Human Rights Commission), 10–11 May 1999.
- **State-level workshop on Rural Water Supply, Health, Sanitation & Nutrition (SPAC — State Plan of Action for Children)**, 30 Aug–1 Sept 1999, sponsored by UNICEF.

**Correctional Services:**
- **1-day UNICEF-sponsored workshop** on "Implementation of the Juvenile Justice Act," 17 February 2000.

**Welfare of the Disabled:**
- **Govt. Ideal Blind School, Takyel** — only school of its kind in the state; Diet allowance Rs. 500/month for hostellers; free books/stationery; Reader teachers, Braille Instructor, Dance/Music classes. **96 students enrolled** during the year. Notably, **Km. Sunita Nepram**, a National-level Song & Music awardee student, was appointed as a Department employee.
- **Govt. Deaf & Mute School** — **56 children** educated up to Class X using sign language/demonstration techniques; hostel + diet allowance provided.
- Combined: **Rs. 7.30 lakhs spent on diet allowances, benefiting 152 disabled children** across both schools.
- **Rs. 1.20 lakhs spent on scholarships, benefiting 321 disabled students** (inside/outside state schools/colleges).

**Prohibition Education:**
- International Day against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking observed statewide (June 1999) with mass rallies, placards/festoons by NGO volunteers.
- Awareness camps on HIV/AIDS organised with the Medical Department.

**Welfare of the Aged, Old and Infirmed:**
- Manipur Old Age Pension Rules — Rs. 100/month per pensioner. **Rs. 47.70 lakhs spent, benefiting 3,974 old-aged persons.**

**State Social Welfare Board:**
- Manipur State Social Welfare Advisory Board (MSSWAB) ran Awareness Generation (rural/poor women), Condensed Courses of Education for Adult Women, vocational training, socio-economic programmes, and grant-in-aid via NGOs; Border Area Welfare Extension Projects delivered Balwadi, Craft, and Social Education services.
- **Rs. 6.00 lakhs + Rs. 10.00 lakhs** maintenance grants given to MSSWAB during the year.

**Special Nutrition Programme:**
- **150.425 metric tonnes of Skimmed Milk Powder** procured for Anganwadi Centres under ICDS.
- **Rs. 2,05,80,000 sanctioned**, benefiting **93,940 children** and **24,639 expectant/nursing mothers**.

**Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS) — 100% Centrally Sponsored:**
- **34 ICDS Projects, 6 District ICDS Cells, 1 State ICDS Cell.**
- **4,181 Anganwadi Centres** actively running with **4,181 Anganwadi Workers and 4,181 Helpers** (paid monthly honorarium).
- Free educational/teaching materials (blackboards, furniture, charts, maps) and feeding items supplied to AWCs.
- **93,940 children (age 6 months–6 years)** benefited; **24,639 pregnant/nursing mothers** given supplementary nutrition; **1,12,202 pre-school children** given facilities.
- **Rs. 958.13 lakhs spent** under ICDS.

## 3. Annual Administration Report — 2000–2001

### Institutional Network
Same core structure as above: 1 Directorate, 1 State ICDS Cell, 1 Deaf & Mute School, 1 Blind School, 1 Welfare Training Institute, 1 Anganwadi Training Centre, 1 Observation/Special/Juvenile Home, 1 State Bal Bhavan, 1 Working Ladies Hostel at State level; 9 District Social Welfare Offices; District ICDS Cells at Imphal, Churachandpur, Ukhrul, Senapati + twin-district cells (Chandel & Thoubal; Tamenglong & Bishnupur); **34 ICDS Projects (incl. 1 Urban) running 4,310 Anganwadi Centres.**

**Schemes/Programmes implemented (as listed in report):** Welfare of Women; Child Welfare; Welfare of the Disabled; Training; Welfare of the Aged and Infirm (Manipur State Old Age Pension); Correctional Services; Special Nutrition Programme; ICDS (CSS); Grant-in-aid to NGOs under GoI schemes; NSAP; Balika Samridhi Yojana (BSY).

### Programme Performance, 2000–2001

**Welfare of the Women:**
- **International Women's Day** observed 2nd week of March 2001 with Mahila Kalyan Samiti, Imphal.
- **9 working women** accommodated at Working Ladies Hostel, Takyel.
- Annual State Grant continued to one Destitute Women Home, Imphal.
- **Women's Empowerment Year 2001** programme chalked out at first State-Level Committee meeting (7 March 2001), assigning responsibilities across State Departments.

**Child Welfare:**
- **International Children's Day** observed 14 Nov 2000 at all District HQs.
- State Bal Bhavan continued dance/music/computer/drawing classes with summer/winter camps; **Mr. Kh. Jachson** of Manipur Bal Bhavan selected for the prestigious **Bal Shree Award** (2000-01).
- New **mini Children's Science Park** added at Bal Bhavan (with State Science & Technology Dept. assistance).
- 8 NGO-run Destitute Children's Homes continued to receive annual financial assistance.
- **248 school-going children** given financial assistance @ Rs. 60/month.

**Welfare of the Disabled:**
- 2 special schools (Blind; Deaf & Mute) continued; producing matriculates annually; Rs. 500/month diet allowance for hostellers.
- **142 students** given disability scholarships.
- Detailed **census for identification of disabled persons** conducted under the Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995; **Disabled Identity Cards and Pass Books** issued; a **State-level coordination committee** constituted.
- **Gramee Purnavas Yojana** (District Disability Rehabilitation Centre) launched; Medical Superintendent, J.N. Hospital, Imphal designated as DDRC in-charge.

**Welfare of the Aged & Infirmed:**
- **3,965 pensioners** benefited under Manipur Old Age Pension Rules (Rs. 100/month) — cumulative figure as of this report.

**Correctional Services:**
- One Observation Home/Special Home maintained at Takyel with minimum required staff (Centre-funded).
- **1 juvenile** kept under Juvenile Court order during the year.
- **15 young girls delivered from the Observation Home, Bengalore [sic]** to their respective guardians/parents.

**Grant-in-Aid Programme:**
- Continued annual State-share grants to: Manipur State Social Welfare Advisory Board; Border Area Welfare Extension Projects (3 nos.); Manipur State Women Development Corporation.
- **MSSWAB (2000–01):** Financial assistance to **217 crèche units** (@ Rs. 18,450/centre, run by registered voluntary orgs); **33 awareness camps** (25–30 women capacity each, run by 33 registered voluntary orgs) @ Rs. 10,000/camp.
- **Border Area Welfare Extension Projects (Ukhrul, Churachandpur, Chandel):** 15 Balwadi/Pre-Primary Education centres; 15 Crèche centres; 15 Maternity Service centres; 15 Craft Training Programme centres.

**Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS):**
- **34 ICDS Projects**, 9 Districts, 33 blocks + 1 Urban Project; 1 State ICDS Cell; 6 District ICDS Cells.
- GoI-approved AWCs: **4,501**; State-sanctioned: **4,411**; functioning as of 31.3.2002: **4,310** — with **4,310 Anganwadi Workers and 4,310 Helpers**.
- **Nutritional status of children identified:**

| Grade | Number | % |
|---|---|---|
| Normal | 15,081 | 9.17% |
| Grade-I | 1,25,256 | 76.15% |
| Grade-II | 23,421 | 14.24% |
| Grade-III & IV | 721 | 0.44% |

- **52,744 boys and 52,925 girls (age 3–6)** received pre-school education.
- Health check-ups, immunization, IEC conducted at AWCs (coordination with Health & Family Welfare Dept.); World Breast Feeding Day, National Nutrition Week, ICDS Day, Children's Day, Republic Day, Mother's Day observed at District/Project levels.
- **ICDS Training (UDISHA):** 4 AWTCs (NGO- and State-run) conducted training; **420 AWWs given Job Course Training; 400 Helpers given Orientation Training.**
- **ICDS General Expenditure:** Rs. 1,060.90 lakhs spent against a budgetary requirement of Rs. 1,716.38 lakhs — staff salaries/AWW-Helper honoraria paid only up to January 2002 due to shortfall.
- **10 best Anganwadi Workers** selected for awards (based on 1999–2000 performance).
- **Pradhan Mantri Gramodaya Yojna (PMGY) — Nutrition Component:** introduced in Manipur from 2000–01 to reduce malnutrition in under-3 children; **Rs. 3,64.20 lakhs sanctioned (2001–02)** for procurement of **412.9 MT of Amul Skimmed Milk Powder**.

**National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP):**
- **National Old Age Pension Scheme:** Rs. 75/month; **35,061 persons** given pensions during the year.
- **National Maternity Benefit Scheme:** **5,635 families** benefited.
- **National Family Benefit Scheme:** **599 families** benefited.

**Balika Samridhi Yojna (BSY):**
- 100% centrally sponsored; covers girl children (BPL families) born on/after 15 August 1997; post-birth grant of **Rs. 500**.
- GoI released **Rs. 10.75 lakhs** for BSY implementation via the Finance Department; **physical achievement for 2000–01 was "practically nil"** due to a bill-encashment ban by the Government, despite timely financial sanctions.

## 4. Annual Administration Report — 2001–2002

### Institutional Network
Identical core structure to 2000–01 report (1 Directorate; 1 State ICDS Cell; 1 Deaf & Mute School; 1 Blind School; 1 Welfare Training Institute; 1 Anganwadi Training Centre; 1 Observation/Special/Juvenile Home; 1 State Bal Bhavan; 1 Working Ladies Hostel; 9 District Social Welfare Offices; District ICDS Cells as before).

### Programme Performance, 2001–2002

**Welfare of the Women:**
- **11 working women** accommodated at the Working Ladies Hostel, Takyel (up from 9 the previous year).
- Annual State Grant continued to the Destitute Women Home, Imphal.
- **Women's Empowerment Year 2001** observance continued into 2001–02 with monthly seminars/workshops across State Departments.

**Child Welfare:**
- **Grant-in-aid to 8 Destitute Children's Homes:** **321 destitute/orphan/neglected children** provided shelter and care (up from prior years).
- **Financial Assistance to Destitute Children:** **288 school-going children** (age 6–14, BPL) given Rs. 60/month.
- **State Bal Bhavan (Khuman Lampak):** training in music, dance, science, home management, computer, etc.; workshops/camps/competitions held throughout the year. **Cumulative total: 7 children from Manipur have received the National Bal Shree Award since 1995.**
- **Bal Kendras** operating in tribal areas of Senapati and Churachandpur districts; a Bal Bhavan-cum-children's park at **Moreh** (Indo-Myanmar border town) was "under process."

**Welfare of the Disabled:**
- 2 special schools continued producing matriculates; Rs. 500/month diet allowance; **142 students** given scholarships.
- **2 identification/assessment camps** for disabled persons organised at **J.N. Hospital, Porompat**, with sittings of the State Medical Board; permanent disability certificates and Identity Cards/Pass Books issued.
- **Gramee Purnavas Yojana / District Disability Rehabilitation Centre (DDRC):** Medical Superintendent, J.N. Hospital designated Nodal Officer; **18 wheelchairs/tricycles distributed** to registered disabled persons (cumulative).

**Welfare of the Aged & Infirmed:**
- **4,172 pensioners** benefited under Manipur Old Age Pension Rules (Rs. 100/month, paid up to Feb 2002) — cumulative figure, up from 3,965 the previous year.

**Correctional Services:**
- Implementing the **Centrally Sponsored "Programme for Juvenile Justice"** (50:50 GoI/State financing) under the Juvenile Justice Act, 1986.
- One Observation Home, one Juvenile Home, one Special Home at Takyel.
- **2 boys (delinquent juveniles)** kept at the Home under Juvenile Court orders during 2001–02.
- **Draft Manipur Juvenile Justice (Care & Protection of Children) Rules, 2001** under process, aligning with the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child.

**Grant-in-Aid Programme:**
- Continued grants to: Manipur State Social Welfare Advisory Board; 3 Border Area Welfare Extension Projects; Manipur State Women Dev. Corporation (same 15-centre-each structure at Ukhrul, Churachandpur, Chandel as in prior years: Balwadi, Crèche, Maternity Service, Craft Training).

**Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS):**
- **34 ICDS Projects**, 9 Districts, 33 blocks + 1 Urban Project; 1 State ICDS Cell; 6 District ICDS Cells.
- GoI-approved AWCs: **4,501**; State-sanctioned and functioning as of 31.3.2002: **4,460** — with **4,460 Anganwadi Workers and 4,460 Helpers** (up from 4,310 the prior year).
- Same nutritional-grading breakdown reported (Normal 9.17%, Grade-I 76.15%, Grade-II 14.24%, Grade-III&IV 0.44%) — **52,744 boys, 52,925 girls** given pre-school education.
- **ICDS General Expenditure:** Rs. 1,060.90 lakhs spent vs. Rs. 1,716.38 lakhs required — salaries/honoraria again paid only through January 2002.
- **Medicine/PSE Kits:** **5,000 Medicine Kits** procured and distributed to all AWCs via District Consignees/CDPOs; PSE (Pre-School Education) kits also procured/distributed at project level.
- **Kishori Shakti Yojna (KSY)** (renamed from the Adolescent Girls Scheme): launched in **13 ICDS Projects** — Tengnoupal, Chandel, Chakpikarong, Ukhrul, Phungyar, Chingai, Kasom Khullen, Kamjong, Imphal East-I (Sawombung), Imphal West-I, Jiribam, Moirang, Bishnupur. Aimed at healthy transition from girlhood to womanhood (ages 11–19). **Rs. 14.30 lakhs provided (@ Rs. 1.10 lakh/project)**; **780 adolescent girls given Orientation Training**.
- **10 best Anganwadi Workers** awarded (based on 2000–01 performance).
- **PMGY Nutrition Component:** **Rs. 3,64.20 lakhs sanctioned (2001–02)** for **412.9 MT of Amul Skimmed Milk Powder**.

**National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP):**
- **National Old Age Pension Scheme:** Rs. 75/month; **35,061 persons** given pensions (same figure reported as prior year).
- **National Maternity Benefit Scheme:** **5,635 families** benefited.
- **National Family Benefit Scheme:** **599 families** benefited.

**Balika Samridhi Yojna (BSY):**
- Implemented in the state since 1997; post-delivery grant of **Rs. 500** to mothers of girl children.
- **Cumulative girls benefited under the scheme: 3,208.**

**ICDS Training (UDISHA):**
- 4 AWTCs (NGO- and State-run) conducted training; **385 AWWs given Job Course Training** during 2001–02.

## 6. Observations on Report Content (Analytical Notes)

- **Budget shortfalls were a recurring theme in ICDS financing:** both the 2000–01 and 2001–02 reports note that only **Rs. 1,060.90 lakhs** was spent against a required **Rs. 1,716.38 lakhs**, resulting in Anganwadi Worker/Helper honoraria being paid only through January of the reporting year.
- **The Balika Samridhi Yojna** faced early implementation delays (2000–01: "practically nil" achievement due to a bill-encashment ban) before showing cumulative progress by 2001–02 (3,208 girls benefited).
- **Disability services matured over the three years** — from ad hoc scholarship disbursement (1999–2000) to formal census/ID-card issuance and dedicated identification camps at J.N. Hospital (2000–01 and 2001–02), alongside the launch of the Gramee Purnavas Yojana/DDRC rehabilitation centre.
- **Kishori Shakti Yojna (KSY)**, the renamed Adolescent Girls Scheme, was a notable new programme introduced in the 2001–02 reporting year, targeting 13 ICDS projects and 780 adolescent girls.
- **No Annual Administration Report more recent than 2001–2002 is currently published** on the official website's Reports and Publications section — this is the most detailed and current primary-source programme performance data publicly available from the Department as of this research.
