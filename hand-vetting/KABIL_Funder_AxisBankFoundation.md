# KABIL Funder Profile — Axis Bank Foundation (ABF)

**Part of KABIL's current funder research series.** This file covers only **Axis Bank Foundation**. See separate files for Azim Premji Foundation, SBI Foundation, and Tata Trusts.

---

## KABIL Connection (confirmed)
ABF partners with **KABIL + Assam State Rural Livelihood Mission (ASRLM)** on a livelihoods project in **Udalguri, Assam** — described as a 4-year project (started April 2018) targeting **15,000 SHG-affiliated families**, focused on productivity enhancement, entrepreneurship development, and market linkages, addressing lack of water-lifting infrastructure, capital, and market access among tea-tribe/Adibasi, Rabha, Garo, and Bodo households.
**Sources:** https://www.axisbankfoundation.org/partners/kabil.html; https://give.do/discover/3WF/kabil

## 8. Funding Trends (Is Giving Increasing, Decreasing, or Shifting?)

**Verdict: Strongly INCREASING, with clear thematic diversification.**

### Year-over-year CSR spend (₹ crore, Axis Bank standalone figures)
| Fiscal Year | Amount Spent | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| FY2018-19 | ~₹137 cr | Pre-pandemic level |
| FY2019-20 | ~₹101 cr | Decline begins |
| FY2020-21 | ~₹91 cr | COVID-driven profit decline cut the CSR budget sharply (2% of net profit mandate) |
| FY2021-22 | ~₹138 cr | Recovery begins |
| FY2022-23 | ~₹202 cr (bank) / ~₹221 cr (group) | Strong rebound |
| FY2023-24 | ~₹269 cr | Continued growth |
| FY2024-25 | **~₹427 cr** | Nearly **60% jump YoY**; spend slightly exceeded the statutory 2%-of-profit obligation |

**Growth pattern:** A COVID-era trough (~₹91 cr, FY20-21) was followed by a sustained climb to ~₹427 cr by FY24-25 — **nearly a 4.7x increase in four years**, tracking Axis Bank's rebounding profitability (CSR spend is legally pegged to 2% of average 3-year net profit) plus expanded scale after the Citibank retail-banking acquisition.

### Thematic shift: from "livelihoods-only" to a diversified 7-pillar model
- Historically (illustrative FY2020-21 breakdown), **Sustainable Livelihoods dominated at ~58%** of spend.
- By FY2024-25, ABF's official theme buckets had expanded to seven: **Lives & Livelihoods, Environmental Sustainability, Education, Health & Nutrition, Financial Inclusion & Literacy, Sports, and Humanitarian & Relief** — showing clear diversification beyond the original livelihoods focus.
- **Environmental Sustainability is a fast-growing newer emphasis** (afforestation, watershed/water-harvesting, agroforestry — including the Assam-based Balipara Foundation partnership).
- **Sports is a notably new addition** (e.g., an Olympic Gold Quest-style partnership), not part of ABF's original mandate.
- A **₹100 crore, 5-year commitment to the National Cancer Grid** signals a growing appetite for large, long-horizon institutional partnerships rather than only small NGO project grants.

### Mission completion → reinvestment (not retrenchment)
"Mission 2 Million" (launched 2018, targeting 2 million households) was **completed/surpassed in FY2024-25** (~2.05 million households, 300 districts, 32 states/UTs). Rather than winding down after hitting the target, ABF immediately launched a **larger successor commitment — "Mission 4 Million"** (2025, targeting 2 million *additional* households by 2030/31) — a clear signal of expanding, not contracting, ambition.

### Structural shift: more multi-year, milestone-based funding
Of the FY2024-25 total, roughly ₹287 cr was spent in cash while ~₹140 cr was provisioned/carried forward as "unspent obligation" tied to ongoing multi-year projects — indicating a shift toward long-horizon, milestone-based programming rather than one-off annual disbursement.

### Context
India's total corporate CSR spend has been rising nationally (~₹31,000 cr in FY22-23, per government data), but **ABF's growth rate (nearly 60% YoY in FY24-25) outpaces the national average**, suggesting Axis Bank is prioritizing CSR growth ahead of the broader market trend.
