## Honesty Notes / Gaps

- **No KABIL-specific grant amounts** were found for any of the four funders — all size/duration figures are either (a) general funder-wide ranges/policies, or (b) specific to named KABIL projects only where a duration (not amount) was disclosed.
- **Tata Trusts' current, active connection to KABIL could not be confirmed** beyond the website listing — see `KABIL_Funder_TataTrusts.md` for full detail.
- Eligibility criteria (12A/80G/FCRA/CSR-1/minimum turnover) are **explicitly published only for SBI Foundation** among the four.
- This research relied on funder websites, CSR/annual reports, news coverage, and grant-database references as of the research date; funder policies, leadership, and program names may change over time and should be re-verified before use in a live fundraising strategy.
