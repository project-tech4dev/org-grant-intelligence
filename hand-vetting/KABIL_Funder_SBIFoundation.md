# KABIL Funder Profile — SBI Foundation (SBIF)

**Part of KABIL's current funder research series.** This file covers only **SBI Foundation**. See separate files for Axis Bank Foundation, Azim Premji Foundation, and Tata Trusts.

## KABIL Connection (confirmed)
SBIF funds the **"SBIF-CONSERW Jaldhara Project"** in Udalguri — focused on aquatic ecosystem/water-resource conservation, including repair of traditional **Dong systems** (community irrigation channels), water conservation, and minor irrigation structures, implemented alongside SeSTA/BRLF. Documented KABIL field training under this project: 11 June 2026 (Hydrology & Watershed Management; Dong Systems). SBIF also appears connected to KABIL via recruitment of Community Resource Persons (CRPs) at its Udalguri office, referencing "SBIF" — possibly linked to the separate "Gram Seva" program active in the same district (implemented there by fellow KABIL-partner Gramya Vikash Mancha/GVM).
**Sources:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kabilindia_kabil-sbifoundation-conserw-activity-7475541652640620544-Bnms; https://sbifoundation.in/conserw

## 8. Funding Trends (Is Giving Increasing, Decreasing, or Shifting?)

**Verdict: Strongly INCREASING (roughly 3x in three years) with a clear diversification from a health/education base into environment, rural development, and inclusion.**

### Year-over-year CSR spend
| Fiscal Year | Total SBI CSR Spend | Allocated to/via SBI Foundation | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY2022 | ₹204.10 cr | ₹102.56 cr (~50%) | ₹71 cr of this went to COVID-19 relief; ~250 NGOs, 380 activities |
| FY2023 | ₹316.76 cr | — | ~54 lakh beneficiaries reached, pan-India |
| FY2024 | ₹502.32 cr | ₹301.24 cr (~60%) | Foundation sanctioned ₹288.57 cr across 145 projects; 173 initiatives across 80 Aspirational Districts |
| FY2025 | ₹610.77 cr | ₹485.83 cr sanctioned / ₹264.00 cr disbursed (180 projects) | 1,408 total CSR activities; 65 lakh+ beneficiaries; 339 initiatives across 94 Aspirational Districts |
| FY2025-26 (preliminary) | ~₹709.01 cr (reported) | — | Figure from a single secondary source (India CSR Network); **not yet independently corroborated** against an official SBI report |

**Growth pattern:** SBI's total CSR spend — and the share funneled through SBI Foundation — has grown **roughly 3x in three years** (₹204 cr FY22 → ₹610+ cr FY25), and the **share of the Bank's total CSR budget routed through the Foundation itself has also grown** (~50% in FY22 to ~60% in FY24), meaning SBIF is becoming a progressively larger share of SBI's total CSR machinery, not just riding a rising CSR tide.

Notably, the gap between **sanctioned** (₹485.83 cr, FY25) and **disbursed** (₹264 cr, FY25) amounts is significant — indicating a shift toward multi-year project commitments with staggered payouts, similar to the pattern seen at Axis Bank Foundation and Azim Premji Foundation.

|---|---|---|---|

Health has consistently been the **largest single share (~40%)** of Foundation funding, but the thematic *mix* is broadening: newer RFPs (2025-26) explicitly invite proposals spanning **Disability & Inclusion, Environment & Sustainability, Livelihood/Skilling/Entrepreneurship, Education, Women Empowerment, and Child Development** — i.e., more categories than the original 6-pillar mandate, with **Environment/CONSERW (the vertical funding KABIL's Jaldhara project) representing one of the newer growth areas** rather than a legacy program.

### Timeline of thematic vertical launches (diversification over time)
- **SBI Youth for India Fellowship (YFI)** — oldest flagship, since **2011**
- **SBI Gram Seva** — since **~2017** (5-year milestone celebrated in 2022)
- **SBIF CONSERW (incl. Jaldhara)** — a **newer** vertical; the specific Jaldhara call for proposals targeted projects "starting FY2025-26," making KABIL's grant one of the more recently-added funding relationships in this file
- **Muskaan (child development), Sashakti (women), LEAP (startup incubation)** — more recently branded verticals, per SBIF's current site structure

### Geographic/institutional scale-up
- Aspirational District coverage grew from **80 districts (FY2024) to 94 districts (FY2025)** — a direct sign of deepening reach into underserved regions (a category most of Northeast India/Assam districts, including Udalguri, fall under).
- Since inception (2015) through FY2023-24, the Foundation reported **cumulative funding of over ₹500 crore, impacting over 31 million lives**.
- Recent large multi-year, institutional-scale commitments signal a shift toward bigger, fewer grants: **₹10 crore to ALIMCO** (assistive devices for persons with disabilities, FY2025); **₹21.34 cr to RSETI** rural-livelihood infrastructure in FY2025 (up from ₹30.11 cr spent on RSETI societies in FY2023 — figures fluctuate but show sustained large-ticket investment in this space).
- **Governance tightening** alongside the growth: newer grants require dedicated SBI bank accounts for fund utilization, monthly/quarterly reporting, and signed MoUs/NDAs — indicating SBIF is professionalizing its oversight as ticket sizes and total spend increase.

### Overall takeaway
SBI Foundation's funding trend is one of **strong, sustained growth** (~3x in three years) coupled with **diversification away from a health-dominated base** toward environment (the vertical relevant to KABIL), rural development, and inclusion — alongside a structural shift toward fewer, larger, multi-year institutional partnerships with stricter compliance requirements.
