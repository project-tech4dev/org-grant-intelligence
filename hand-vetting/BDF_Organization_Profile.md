## 2. History & Actual Age

- BDF was **established on 16 August 2021** — so it is officially **~4 years old** as of late 2025/2026 activity being reported. (One of BDF's own domains, bdfoundation.co.in, states the organization has been "working... since 2019," suggesting founder Monjib Mochahari and colleagues may have been doing informal/preparatory community work for ~2 years before formal Section-8 incorporation in Aug 2021 — directly analogous to the KABIL pattern of informal work preceding formal registration.)
- **Origin story:** Founded against the backdrop of the Bodoland Territorial Region's more-than-three-decades of socio-political unrest (the Bodo insurgency/autonomy movement), which left "fractured communities, disrupted livelihoods, and stalled progress" affecting over 3.5 million people in the region. BDF frames itself as "a beacon of change" emerging in the post-conflict recovery period following the 2020 BTR peace accord.
- **5th anniversary** was marked in August 2026 ("What began on August 16, 2021, has grown into five remarkable years of purpose, impact, and community service" — LinkedIn post).
- This makes your original estimate — **"started around 2020"** — very close to accurate for BDF (off by only about a year), unlike KABIL (which was actually founded in 2012, much earlier than a 2020 guess would suggest).

## 3. Mission, Vision & Core Values

- **Vision:** "A self-reliant, sustainable and dignified society."
- **Mission:** "To foster self-reliant communities by driving participatory development, advocating for effective policies, enhancing capacity building, and implementing strategic interventions for long-term impact."
- **Core Values:** Inclusivity & Equity; Transparency & Accountability; Integrity; Respect; Compassion; Pro-Action; Excellence; Collaboration.

  1. Skill Training
  2. Youth Empowerment
  3. Women Empowerment
  4. Livelihood Promotion
  5. Climate Action
  6. PWDs (Persons with Disabilities) Livelihood
  7. Training & Capacity Building

## 6. Funders — What's Confirmed vs. Not Disclosed

Unlike KABIL, **BDF does not publicly list a distinct "Funding Partners/Donors" section** separate from its general "Partners" list — meaning most of BDF's listed partners (SELCO Foundation, Resham Sutra, etc.) function as **both technical/programmatic partners AND likely funders/co-funders** of specific initiatives (this is typical of how foundations like SELCO operate — providing both hardware/technology and grant funding together). No partner-specific grant amounts, or program budgets, were found publicly disclosed for BDF's projects.

**What IS disclosed:**
- Authorised share capital ₹10 lakh; paid-up capital ₹60,000 (these are corporate-registration figures, not indicative of programmatic budget/turnover)
- FY2024-25 revenue: "less than ₹10 Cr" (Tracxn's broad bracket — exact figure undisclosed)
- BDF **does publish Annual Reports** (FY2021-22, FY2022-23, FY2023-24, FY2024-25) and **Audit Reports** on its own website (bdfoundation.org.in/annual-reports/ and /audit-reports/) — this is a meaningfully higher level of public financial transparency than KABIL, which has no such public reports. However, **the actual PDF/report content could not be extracted during this research** (the report pages on BDF's site returned empty/placeholder content when fetched — likely a technical issue with how the documents are embedded, not necessarily unavailability). **Recommend a follow-up visit to https://bdfoundation.org.in/annual-reports/ directly in a browser to download the PDFs, which should contain detailed funder-wise and program-wise financial breakdowns.**

**What's NOT disclosed (per this research):**
- Exact grant/contribution amounts from SELCO Foundation, Resham Sutra, Katidhan, RuKart Technologies, or any other partner
- Whether BDF holds 12A/80G/FCRA registration (its Assam Flood appeal claims "Verified 80G Tax-Exempt Donation Drive," suggesting BDF likely does hold **80G status**, but the certificate number was not found)
- Government (BTC) funding/grant amounts for the various Agriculture Department training programs

## 10. Challenges (Analytical Assessment)

> **Note on methodology:** BDF has not published its own "challenges/risks" statement. The points below are an **analytical assessment inferred from patterns in the available public evidence** — not a direct quote or admission from BDF itself. Each point is grounded in evidence gathered during this research and cited accordingly.

### Fundraising
- **Signs of revenue volatility in latest filing:** Tracxn's MCA-derived financial statement summary for BDF shows the following year-over-year figures (units as published by Tracxn; likely ₹ thousands, exact unit not labeled by source):

  | Metric | FY2021-22 | FY2022-23 | FY2023-24 | FY2024-25 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Revenue | 6720 | 6838 | 6793 | **3682** |
  | EBITDA | 1476 | 8997 | 8051 | 5039 |
  | Assets | 4450 | 6837 | 9354 | 1521 |
  | Liabilities | 8728 | 4930 | 1651 | 8574 |

  Revenue in FY2024-25 appears **roughly 46% lower** than the prior three fiscal years (which were relatively stable ~6,700–6,840). This pattern — three years of stable income followed by a sharp single-year drop — is consistent with either a lost/non-renewed grant, a timing/reporting gap, or genuine funding contraction, and is a relevant flag for fundraising due diligence. **This figure should be independently verified against BDF's own audited financials before drawing firm conclusions, as the unit basis was not explicitly labeled by the source.**
  **Source:** https://tracxn.com/d/legal-entities/india/bodoland-development-foundation
- **Reliance on a concentrated set of technology/implementation partners for funding-adjacent support:** Much of BDF's flagship programming (Resilient Futures, Eri Weaves, DRE Livelihood Solutions) is anchored to a small number of partners — principally **SELCO Foundation** and **Resham Sutra** — who appear to provide both technology and funding-in-kind support together. No evidence was found of a broad, diversified base of independent financial donors/foundations comparable to KABIL's four named funders.
  **Source:** https://bdfoundation.org.in/programs; https://bdfoundation.org.in/impact
- **Active emergency/crowdfunding appeal suggests reliance on unrestricted public donations for surge needs:** BDF is running a live "Assam Flood" relief donation appeal (assamflood.bdfoundation.org.in) for 500,000+ affected people — a sign that, absent large standing institutional reserves, BDF turns to public appeals for emergency response funding.
  **Source:** https://bdfoundation.org.in (Assam Flood banner)
- **Young organization still building institutional funder trust:** At ~4 years old (incorporated Aug 2021), BDF has a shorter funding track record than KABIL (13 years), which may affect its ability to secure larger, longer-duration institutional grants until a longer performance history is established.
  **Source:** https://bdfoundation.org.in/about-us

### Technology / Data
- **Impact metrics are not independently verifiable or exportable:** BDF's "Impact" page presents its headline numbers (schools reached, beneficiaries trained, etc.) as **JavaScript-animated counters** rather than static, citable text or a downloadable dataset. During this research, the final numeric values could not be extracted via standard page-fetching tools, meaning an external stakeholder without live-browser access would only see "0+" placeholders.
  **Source:** https://bdfoundation.org.in/impact (counter behavior observed directly during this research)
- **Annual/Audit Report pages exist but content is not accessible:** BDF has structured its website to list Annual Reports (FY2021-22 through FY2024-25) and Audit Reports, which is a positive transparency signal — but the actual report pages returned **no extractable content** (empty body text) when accessed directly during this research, suggesting either a technical/hosting gap (e.g., broken embed) or that documents are not actually attached/uploaded yet.
  **Source:** https://bdfoundation.org.in/annual-reports/ and https://bdfoundation.org.in/bdf-doc/annual-report-fy-2024-25/ (both navigated directly; no report text or embedded PDF/download link was found in the rendered page)
- **No visible independent MIS/beneficiary database:** No evidence was found of BDF operating its own management information system, beneficiary tracking database, or program dashboard (in contrast to, e.g., BRLF's "DWIMU Dashboard" used by other Bodoland-region CSOs). Program monitoring appears to rely on partner-supplied tools (e.g., SELCO Foundation's own DRE product tracking) rather than a BDF-owned system.
  **Source:** Absence noted across bdfoundation.org.in, bdfoundation.co.in, and all partner pages reviewed

### Impact Communication
- **Heavy reliance on social media as the primary storytelling channel:** BDF's most detailed, frequent impact updates (training completions, new MoUs, beneficiary testimonials like Hangma Daimari's) are published via **Instagram and LinkedIn**, rather than as dated blog posts, case-study PDFs, or a news archive on its own website.
  **Source:** https://www.instagram.com/bdfoundationofficial; https://in.linkedin.com/company/bodoland-development-foundation
- **Gap between stated transparency infrastructure and actual accessible content:** BDF's site architecture (dedicated Annual Reports, Audit Reports, Compliances, and Publications pages) signals a strong intent toward transparent impact communication — better, on paper, than KABIL's complete absence of such pages — but the **practical inaccessibility of the report content** (see Technology/Data above) undermines this intent for an external researcher or prospective funder trying to verify claims independently.
  **Source:** https://bdfoundation.org.in/annual-reports (structure exists; content not extractable)
- **Testimonial-driven rather than data-driven impact narrative:** BDF's homepage relies substantially on **first-person beneficiary testimonials** (e.g., the Dimakuchi restaurant owner, the Eri spinning trainees) to convey impact, which is compelling for storytelling but is not a substitute for aggregate, verifiable data — especially given the JS-counter data-access issue noted above.
  **Source:** https://bdfoundation.org.in (testimonials section)
