# Research summary (condensed, 13 Nov 2026)

Check anything you rely on against its source. Figures below came from public pages and press coverage read through search.

## Sangam compared with Kindora

Sangam already matches Kindora on cited, agent-built answers on top of a funder base. The gap is scale and presentation. Kindora covers about 270,000 funders, shows proof numbers, offers a demo that needs no signup and publishes prices. Sangam's funder table held 37 funders on 23 Oct 2026. Kindora's own figures disagree across its pages, so treat its numbers as self-reported.

Kindora builds its funder base from IRS filings, UK, Canadian, Australian and other registers, 360Giving, IATI and funder websites, with an LLM extracting grant lists from websites. Its seven foundation types (family, private, operating, faith-based, community, corporate, donor-advised fund sponsors) come from IRS data.

## Data strategy

1. Start with free sources: IRS 990-PF, the OECD philanthropy datasets, IATI, 360Giving. Add the UK Charity Commission and Dutch ANBI registers as rosters later.
2. Screen by India giving as a signal after building the pool, not as a gate.
3. Candid (Grants API listed at about $1,199 a year at the discounted tier by a third-party review) is deferred until a pilot shows need.
4. Germany, Switzerland and the Nordics have no open grant data.
5. Estimated cost for a three-month programme: about Rs 10 to 15 lakh, mostly people. These are estimates.

## OECD facts

India received USD 6.2 billion of philanthropy over 2020 to 2023, 65 percent from domestic sources (Third Edition). The 2018 edition counted 138 international foundations giving to India in 2013 to 2015, USD 1.2 billion. The Taking stock study (2022) covers 183 domestic organisations for 2016 to 2019. OECD data is by survey edition. Annual India series exist only for regular reporters and the roughly 40 large foundations with project-level data.

## FCRA

Section 7 (2020 amendment) bars passing foreign contribution to another person, so an Indian intermediary funded from abroad generally cannot re-grant it. The FCRA (Amendment) Rules 2026 require certificates to name purposes and states. The FCRA Amendment Bill 2026 was before a joint committee and was not law at the time of reading. Public visibility of donor-wise FC-4 data is unclear: a 2021 report says MHA restricted it, and a July 2026 government FAQ describes a searchable database.

## Legal and privacy

Public filings and open datasets are safe with attribution. Respect robots.txt. Organisation-level data carries little privacy risk. Named individuals carry DPDP risk (see decisions.md).
