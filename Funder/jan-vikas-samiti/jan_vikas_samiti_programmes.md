# Jan Vikas Samiti (JVS) — Programmes, Projects, Partners & Impact

Source pages: janvikassamiti.org/disability-2, /community-health-2, /inclusive-education, /community-learning-centres, /livelihood, /ccrt-course, /disaster-relief, /our-impact, /womens-empowerment-2

## Overview
JVS delivers an integrated, community-driven development model centred on **Self-Help Groups (SHGs)** and grassroots collectives (Inclusive Children's Parliaments, Adolescent Girls Groups, Expectant/Young Mothers Groups), across six core programme areas: Disability Inclusive Development, Community Health, Inclusive Education, Community Learning Centres, Skill Development & Livelihood, and Disaster & Relief. JVS's largest and longest-running body of work is **disability inclusion**, delivered largely as a **strategic partner of Stichting Liliane Fonds (Liliane Foundation)** for North India.

## 1. Disability Inclusive Development
- **Mission:** build/strengthen community-based rehabilitation (CBR) to improve quality of life for children and youngsters with disabilities (CwDs) and their families, addressing the full **CBR Matrix** — health, education, livelihood, social inclusion, and empowerment.
- **Scale:** serving **over 6,000 children and youngsters with disabilities** via a network of partner organisations across **14 states of North and North-East India**.
- **Capacity building:** a key focus is strengthening the capacity of *other* organisations working with persons with disabilities (not just direct service delivery).
- **Community-Based Rehabilitation Program highlights (recent reporting period):** 235 children/youngsters supported with health/para-medical rehabilitation (corrective surgery, medical checkups, medication, physiotherapy); 400 children/youngsters assisted with educational supports (inclusive/special/regular education).
- **Project "Sambhav" (Tele-Rehabilitation)** — ongoing since 01-04-2018; tagline "Reaching the Unreached"; provides affordable tele-rehabilitation to children across **14 states of North and North-East India**; funded by **Stichting Liliane Foundation**; winner of the **Zero Project Award 2022**.
- **Prosthetics & Orthotics Support Unit** — inaugurated **11 March 2024**; a sustainable, non-profit model producing/distributing prefabricated orthoses and prostheses, plus follow-up/maintenance/customisation/gait-training services, targeted at rural/remote persons with disabilities. Contact/orders: +91 90054 38108 (call/WhatsApp).
- **CCRT — Certificate Course in Rehabilitation Therapy** — launched **7 August 2023**, approved by the **Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI)**, New Delhi. Trains Rehabilitation Therapist Assistants for work in CBR setups, NGOs, hospitals, and special schools.
  - **Duration:** 1 year, full-time; **Fee:** ₹40,000 (2 instalments) + ₹200 application fee.
  - **Eligibility:** 50% marks in Class 12 (45% for SC/ST/OBC/PwD categories); ability to understand/read/write/speak English.
  - **Syllabus (8 papers):** General Anatomy & Physiology; Personal & Professional Studies; Disabilities, Development & CBR; Clinical Studies; Rehabilitation Intervention I & II; Clinical Placement; Rural Placements; plus a Basic Computer Course.
  - Minimum 80% attendance required; pass marks 40% (theory)/50% (practical) per paper, 45% aggregate; certified by RCI New Delhi.
- **Case studies:**
  - **Nikita** (age 7, Buipal village, Khandwa, MP) — hemiplegic cerebral palsy; referred to the "Sambhav" tele-rehabilitation service via a CBR professional of partner **St. Paul's Service Society, Khandwa**; after therapy (standing frame, parallel bar, home visits), she can now sit unsupported, stand with support, and is starting to walk.
  - **Savitri** (age 24, Paramanandpur, Varanasi) — contracted polio after a childhood fever; faced severe family rejection/stigma; supported by JVS via the **Liliane Foundation (LF) project** (wheelchair, school enrolment); now works as a teacher at an Anganwadi/ICDS school and gives tuitions; dropped from the LF project October 2013; publicly credited "JVS-LF" for her transformation.

## 2. Community Health
(Focus areas: Safe Motherhood & early intervention, TB/contagious disease eradication, Community Eye Health, WASH)
- **Safe Motherhood Initiative** — reduces mother/newborn mortality via reproductive healthcare, education, community engagement (street plays, wall paintings); rights-based approach aiming to empower **8,000 rural women and girls** to access comprehensive maternal healthcare.
- **WASH (Water, Sanitation & Hygiene)** — improves sanitation/hygiene in grassroots villages of Uttar Pradesh and Bihar, with specific attention to the needs of Persons with Disabilities (PwDs) and marginalised girls, under the Government of India's **Swachh Bharat Abhiyan**. Uses Village Monitoring Committees (VMCs) to sustain toilet use/maintenance.
- **Impact highlights:** 30 Adolescent Groups formed/functional; 30 Village Health and Sanitation Committees formed; 34,424 people assisted through health camps.
- **Named projects/partners:** TB awareness and prevention programme (Pindra/Harahua); Vision Centre with **Sight Savers India** (Harahua/Pindra); Community Mobilization for Safe Motherhood with **Manos Unidas** (Harahua); Community-based HIV/AIDS screening with **SAATHI** (Varanasi/Siddharthnagar/Allahabad/Gautam Buddh Nagar); Day Care & Rehabilitation Training Centre with **Stichting Liliane Fonds** (Varanasi); "Secured Hygiene and Healthful Living for Girls" with **Missio Internationales** (Durgawati, Kaimur/Bihar); Vitamin supplementation for pregnant women/young children with **Vitamin Angels** (Harahua).

## 3. Inclusive Education
- **Mission:** eliminate barriers for children with disabilities in elementary schools across **Lucknow, Barabanki, and Sitapur districts** (Uttar Pradesh) — addressing enrolment denial, inaccessible infrastructure, and untrained instructors; equips government primary schools with accessible, adapted teaching-learning materials (TLMs).
- **Project "RAISE"** — a **5-year initiative** with **Liliane Foundation, CBM, and Light for the World**, spanning **5 states of North-East India** through **15 partner organisations**, to make **77 SSA (Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan) schools** in the region inclusive; partner organisations become resource centres for inclusive education, each linked to 3–5 neighbouring SSA schools.
- **"We Ring the Bell" campaign (India)** — organised by JVS in collaboration with **34 organisations across 11 states** working in disability, held **10–15 March 2022**; reached **1,10,016 people** (children, parents, teachers, government officials, elected representatives). *(This is the Indian iteration of Liliane Fonds's global "We Ring the Bell" advocacy campaign — see `liliane_fonds_programmes.md`.)*
- **Impact highlights:** 107 children with disabilities mainstreamed into government schools; 12 SSA schools in UP made accessible; 88 disability-friendly toilets constructed/modified in SSA schools and CwD homes.
- **Named projects/partners:** Non-formal education & vocational training for CwDs with **Kiran Foundation** (Varanasi); "Mother's Care" educational support for poor children, and "Blossom," both with **Bread Noida** (Varanasi); Elementary Education for Slum Children with **Manos Unidas** (Varanasi); Inclusive Education for the Visually Impaired with **Sight Savers India** (Harahua/Pindra).

## 4. Community Learning Centres (CLC)
- **Context:** cites UDISE 2020-21 data — primary-level dropout 0.8%, but secondary-level (Classes 9–10) dropout **14.6%**; girls from marginalised communities (Musahar, Vanvasi) especially affected.
- **Model:** integrates out-of-school/marginalised children back into mainstream education via Community Learning Centres, Community Libraries, and enrolment support under the **Right to Education (RTE) Act**.
- **Current scale:** **11 CLCs in Harahua block**, with **446 children** attending regularly; focus on **Foundational Literacy and Numeracy (FLN)**.
- **Broader goal:** shift community attitudes toward education, health, hygiene, and communal development — not just academic outcomes.

## 5. Skill Development & Livelihood
- **Focus:** improve women's position through education, literacy, training, and Self-Help Group-based inclusive livelihood activities (also extends to persons with disabilities); skill training + microenterprise establishment; entrepreneurship, marketing, financial literacy, leadership, and communication training for group leaders. Framed as contributing to India's **Aatmanirbhar Bharat** initiative.
- **Impact highlights:** 98 outlets established by women for livelihood activities; 430 adolescent girls/young women engaged in tailoring and beautician training.
- **Case studies:**
  - **Savita Devi** (Khadagsipur village, Kerakat, Jaunpur) — joined a JVS-facilitated SHG ("Sita" group, formed Jan 2021, became Secretary); trained in sewing (leggings, kurta, palazzo, lowers) via the Kerakat Block Office; opened a home sewing centre with 5 machines, training 10–15 girls daily; now saves ₹3,500–5,000/month.
  - **Tara Devi** (Deduwana village, Kerakat, Jaunpur) — joined the "Baba Bheem Rao Ambedkar" SHG (April 2019) as Secretary; trained in washing-powder manufacturing/marketing; diversified into goat-rearing; later selected by her SHG to run a village **Ration Control Shop**, which she operates alongside SHG activities.
- **Named projects/partners:** "Promotion of Sustainable and Alternative Livelihood Opportunities Through Grassroots Community Institutions" with **Manos Unidas** (Harahua & Sikhad blocks); "HSBC Skills for Life" with **Swades Foundation** (Varanasi); "Promoting Inclusive Livelihood Opportunities Through Grassroots Community Mobilization" with **Missio Internationales** (Pindra/Kerakat); "Computer Skills for Inclusive Employment" with **Stichting Liliane Foundation** (UP & MP); "Training of Youngsters with Disabilities in Food & Beverage" with **Stichting Liliane Foundation** (Varanasi).

## 6. Disaster & Relief
- **Track record:** ground-zero response to the **Kosi Flood**, the **Nepal Earthquake**, the **COVID-19 nationwide lockdown**, and recent **Ganges flooding** — leveraging its village-level SHG network for outreach/distribution.
- **COVID-19 relief drive:** reached **11,769 families** with free preventive-medicine kits (Paracetamol, Doxycycline, Ivermectin, Zincovit Multivitamin, Vitamin C + Zinc).
- **Named projects/partners:** "Flood Relief Program – Ranipatti" with **Caritas India** (Saharsha, Bihar); "Flood Relief and Rehabilitation Project" with the **Government of Japan** (Saharsha, Bihar); "Child Focused Community Development Program for Flood-affected Children" with **Manos Unidas** (Harahua/Shikhad, Mirzapur, UP); Humanitarian assistance for flood-affected people (Shikhad, Mirzapur); COVID-19 Relief Program with **Missio** (Harahua); "COVID-19 Emergency Relief Program for PwDs and Families Affected," with **Stichting Liliane Foundation**, across **14 states of North India**.

## Women's Empowerment ("Sensitization & Awareness of Women's Issues")
- Educational campaigns, workshops, and community engagement on health/well-being and gender-based violence; fosters dialogue/networking among formal and informal women's groups.
- **Impact:** 132 training programmes on Constitutional Rights; 2,931 awareness-building programmes conducted.
- Per the History page: SHGs and Cluster-Level Committees actively intervene in cases of domestic violence/alcohol-related harassment reported by members, and have helped shut down illegal liquor production with administrative support.

## Additional Livelihood & Awareness Activities (from Gallery / News & Events)
Beyond the named projects above, JVS's ongoing activity log (janvikassamiti.org/news-events, /gallery-1) shows a broader range of recurring trainings and awareness drives, including:
- **Livelihood/vocational skills:** LED bulb assembling, mushroom cultivation, book-keeping and financial literacy, sanitary pad manufacturing, washing powder manufacturing, tailoring/leggings-making.
- **Health/awareness campaigns:** wall paintings and street plays on Safe Motherhood and domestic violence; menstrual and reproductive health training; nutrition-week celebrations; COVID-19 vaccination drives, mask/N95 distribution, and emergency relief kit distribution.
- **Disability-specific:** high-risk newborn screening, early intervention training, home-based therapy, disability screening for tele-rehabilitation, proper use of assistive devices, family involvement in rehabilitation, and a dedicated "Web Portal of Tele-Rehabilitation."
- **Recent 2026 activity examples:** labour-rights awareness session at a Puwari Kala brick kiln (Apr 2026); Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana assessment camp at **Aasra Kendra** (Mar 2026); agri-based business training for women farmers; Women Farmers' Conference & Recognition Ceremony (Feb 2026); sustainable/organic farming training (Jan 2026); nutrition-material distribution to pregnant/lactating brick-kiln workers (Jan 2026); nutrition-kit distribution to tribal families with **Gaspar Empowerment Foundation** in Chaka Banwasi block (Mar 2026).
- **Additional named partner (historical):** **NABARD** — JVS implemented NABARD's SHG Bank Linkage Programme and a livelihood entrepreneurship development programme in 2018-19, benefiting 1,000+ SHG women (see `jan_vikas_samiti_organisation.md` for testimonial detail); **Mentaid** (Kolkata) — partner since April 2012, focused on training parents in skill development for children with intellectual disabilities.
