## Vision & Mission
- **Vision:** "We visualize an inclusive humane society based on the values of equity, justice and freedom."
- **Mission:** "Empowerment of the marginalized, including women, children and persons with disabilities, through a process of sensitizing and organizing them for socio-economic upliftment."
- Core interventions: Community Health & WASH, Livelihoods Promotion for Women, Rehabilitation of Children and Youngsters with Disabilities, Women Empowerment, and Skill Training for Youth.

## History
(Source: janvikassamiti.org/history)
- Founded by **Immanuel George (Abhishiktananda)** and like-minded friends, motivated by widespread superstition, discrimination, exploitation, and lack of education among marginalised communities (Dalits, Musahars, women) around Varanasi.
- Began with **adult education and community health programmes in 20 villages of Harahua block**, Varanasi — focusing on Dalits and women, who initially distrusted the organisation (women did not attend early meetings; trust was built gradually through women animators' persistent outreach).
- **2001** — 60 SHGs linked with banks for credit; has since grown to **832+ SHGs**.
- **2003** — 22 children with disabilities identified for rehabilitation; this cohort has since grown to **over 6,000** children/youngsters served.
- **2008 & 2020–21** — JVS engaged in disaster-relief/rehabilitation work during the **Kosi floods** (Ranipatti, Bihar) and the **COVID-19 pandemic**, respectively.
- **2012** — JVS became the **Strategic Partner Organisation for Liliane Foundation for North India**, for the project on Inclusive Development of Children and Youngsters with Disabilities — a foundational, long-running partnership.
- **2018** — The **Tele-rehabilitation project ("Sambhav")** launched — described as innovative and replicable, later winning the Zero Project Award (2022).
- Ongoing strategy: continuous animation and advocacy among men and women to organise into groups (SHGs, Inclusive Children's Parliaments, Adolescent Girls Groups, Expectant and Young Mothers Groups) for participatory, sustainable development, with strong community ownership of project planning, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation.

## Governance
(Source: janvikassamiti.org/leadership, /good-governance)

### Governing Board
- 9 members, drawn from social and development sectors; sets vision, mission, and broad organisational guidelines. Entrusts day-to-day functioning to the **Secretary cum Executive Director (ED)** — the Chief Functionary, who reports directly to the Governing Board.
- Current Secretary cum Executive Director: **Fr. Chandran Riymonds** (received the 2024 State Award on behalf of JVS).

### Advisory Committee
Non-binding strategic advisory body, members serve ~3-year terms; meets annually. Members include:
- C.A. Sagar Gupta (Sagar Gupta & Co., Varanasi)
- Mr. Kanhaiya Lal Pathik (representative of the Editor of Dainik Jagran, Varanasi)

- Dr. Mahendra Rai (Amrit Milan Hospital, Chandmari, Varanasi)

### Program Management Team (PMT)
Senior-level staff overseeing day-to-day departmental operations, reporting to the Executive Director; meets monthly to review activities and plan ahead.

### Policies
JVS operates under a set of Board-reviewed/approved policies: HR Policy, Child Protection Policy, Finance Policy, Sexual Harassment Policy, Data Protection & Retention Policy, Risk Management Policy, Policy Monitoring & Evaluation, and Policy Against Corruption & Bribery.

### Monitoring, Evaluation & Audit
- **Internal monitoring** by respective programme teams; select projects also undergo **external impact evaluation**.
- **Management Information System (MIS)** — monthly reports prepared/analysed by MIS Officers, reviewed by the PMT.
- **External audit** — conducted annually by a qualified Chartered Accountant, appointed by the Governing Body.
- **Internal audit** — conducted at least once a year (more frequently if required).
- **Financial year:** 1 April – 31 March.

## Aasra Kendra (Elderly & Disability Support Centre)
- A dedicated centre at JVS's Murdaha (Varanasi) premises, used for assistive-device assessment camps for **elderly people and persons with disabilities**, run under Government of India schemes including the **Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana (RVY)** (a national scheme providing assistive living devices to senior citizens below the poverty line).
- Example activity: an assistive-devices assessment camp held 25 April 2026 at Aasra Kendra.
