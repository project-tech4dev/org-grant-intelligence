## 3. History

- **1974 — Founding:** Rev. Art Simon, pastor of Trinity Lutheran Church on Manhattan's Lower East Side, gathered about a dozen church leaders (Roman Catholic, Presbyterian, Lutheran) to address root causes of hunger. Quote: *"It's better to build a fence at the top of a cliff than to have an ambulance at the bottom."*
- **Project 500:** first grassroots recruitment effort — training 500 advocates — foundation of the national network.
- **1975 — First "Offering of Letters"** campaign, on the right to food: fewer than 10,000 members generated 100,000+ letters to Congress, contributing to the **Right to Food Resolution**. Remains Bread's signature annual organizing strategy.
- **1975 — Bread for the World Institute established** — published the **Hunger Report** annually from 1990–2020.
- **1991 — Rev. David Beckmann** (Lutheran pastor, 15 years at the World Bank) succeeded Art Simon as President.
- **2001 — Alliance to End Hunger established** (a Bread affiliate).
- **July 1, 2020 — Rev. Eugene Cho** succeeded Beckmann as President and CEO.
- **2025 — 50th Anniversary**, marked by the annual Advocacy Summit in Washington, D.C.
- **June 29, 2026:** Board announced Rev. Eugene Cho's resignation, effective July 17, 2026. **Rev. Heather Taylor** (Managing Director of Policy and Programs, 8+ years at Bread) named **Interim President and CEO**.

**Cumulative track record:** Policies/programs Bread has advocated for have impacted **320 million Americans** and **more than 7 billion people around the world**.

## 9. Communities Disproportionately Impacted by Hunger

- **African American/Black:** food insecurity at 3x the rate of white households; 1/3+ of hungry people globally live in Africa
- **Latino:** 1 in 5 Latino(a) families in the U.S. struggle to feed their children; ~50M affected in Latin America/Caribbean
- **AAPI:** faces "hidden hunger"; 1 in 6 Native Hawaiian/Pacific Islander community members below poverty level
- **Indigenous:** 1 in 4 Native Americans face hunger; 15% of world's extreme poor are Indigenous
- **Rural:** 84% of highest-childhood-hunger U.S. counties are rural; 2/3 of global extreme poor live rurally
- **Women:** ~60% of hungry people globally are female; higher for single mothers/women of color

## 12. 2025 Annual/Impact Report — "Made for This Moment: 50 Years and Counting"

**President's letter (Rev. Eugene Cho):** 2025 was one of the most challenging years in recent memory amid policy changes/funding cuts; advocates helped protect "hundreds of millions of dollars for hunger and nutrition programs."

### 2025 Policy Wins
- **International aid:** Global nutrition $168M; Feed the Future $960M; Food for Peace $1.619B; McGovern-Dole Food for Education $240M — protected despite rescission attempts; significant restoration in FY2026.
- **Child Tax Credit:** prevented reversion to pre-pandemic refundability levels; per-child benefit raised from $2,000 to $2,200.
- **WIC:** served ~40% of all U.S. infants (2024); secured full funding at $7.6B (FY2025) and $8.2B (FY2026 continuing resolution).

### Grassroots Activity
- 450 congressional meetings in D.C.; ~350 grassroots congressional meetings; 150,000 personalized letters to 536 elected leaders via 399 Offerings of Letters.

### 2025 Advocacy Summit ("Nourish Our Future")
- Marked the 50th anniversary; 450+ advocates in Washington, D.C.
- Bipartisan Capitol Hill kickoff: Rep. Jahana Hayes (D-CT-05) and Rep. Darin LaHood (R-IL-16).
- Student advocacy: ~23% (3.8M) of college students experienced food insecurity in 2020; led Rep. Suzanne Bonamici (D-OR) to introduce a bill for student SNAP access; students met Sen. John Boozman (R-AR), Chair of Senate Agriculture Committee.
- Legacy supporter spotlight: Leota Ester, a Bread supporter since the early 1970s and former board member.

## 14. Hunger Facts (per official "Hunger Explained" page)

- **Myth:** Hunger = not enough food. **Fact:** The core issue is *access* to (especially nutritious) food.
- **Myth:** Food banks alone can solve hunger. **Fact:** Federal nutrition programs provide ~10x more assistance than all private charities/churches combined.
- **Myth:** U.S. hunger mostly affects able-bodied people unwilling to work. **Fact:** Many working people face "hidden hunger" due to low wages; ~13.5% of U.S. households (47.4M people, incl. 13.8M children) struggle to put food on the table.
- **Historical progress:** In 1960, 1 in 3 people globally was hungry; today chronic hunger affects 1 in 11 people (700M+ worldwide).
- Nearly **14 million children** in the U.S. worry about their next meal.
