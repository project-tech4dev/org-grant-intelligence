## FY 2024-25 — "Diversity Makes Us Human, Inclusion Makes Us Equal"
*(Year 1 of the new 2024–2031 strategy)*

### Headline Reach
- **20,65,905** (~20.7 lakh) people received services through core activities
- **1,15,061** persons with disabilities reached directly
- **35** active projects across **17** states/UTs
- 2031 target: reach **80,00,000 (8 million) people**, including **8,00,000 (800,000) persons with disabilities**

### Inclusive Health (14 states)
131 inclusive vision centers · 16,55,037 people received medical services · 3,325 surgeries/procedures · 7,800 assistive devices distributed.
- Karnataka's "Let Me Walk, Let Me Rise" clubfoot surgery project — 50 children treated
- Asha Kirana initiative — trained 2,040 community health workers, who screened 20.7 lakh people
- New West Bengal vision centers (Murshidabad, North 24 Parganas) — part of a 3-year, 3,000-cataract-surgery project
- Telangana inclusive eye-care programme — originally for trans women, expanded to other marginalised groups
- Mizoram "Project Thrive" — 30 nursing scholarships
- Featured story: **Samad** (13), treated for keratoconus at the Nandagudi Inclusive Vision Center with partner Minto Ophthalmic Hospital

### Inclusive Education
3,208 children learning across settings · 292 teachers trained · 360 caregivers trained · 5 community learning centers.
- Gujarat: new Inclusive Education Resource Center (25 children)
- Tripura: Samagra Shiksha partnership expanded to 568 schools (1,683 children with disabilities)
- West Bengal (Sundarbans): tuition/therapy centres (75 children)
- Madhya Pradesh: "mohalla classes" reaching 1,300+ children
- Featured story: **Anupam**, a boy with an intellectual disability supported by special educator Eliza, passed his Class 10 boards

### Inclusive Livelihood
3,442 people trained in skills development · 308 accessed financial services · 142 community-based organisations formed/strengthened.
- Gujarat CBID programme — 175 people trained, 112 households given seed funding
- Uttarakhand — 500+ trained in beekeeping/farming
- UP "Swabhiman" project — 1,000+ trained, 31 entrepreneurs with disabilities funded, 50 self-help groups
- West Bengal (Sundarbans) — climate-resilient livelihoods (goat rearing, pisciculture)
- Featured story: **Shardaben**, a woman with a locomotor disability who built a tailoring business and kirana store

### Inclusive Humanitarian Action
1,002 people reached via humanitarian assistance · 1,171 families received improved cookstoves · 15,770 mangrove saplings planted.
- Karnataka/Uttarakhand cookstove distribution — 265 + 370 families
- West Bengal — mangrove restoration + 8 accessible tubewells
- Cyclone Remal disaster response — 1,000+ people reached
- Featured story: **Lalitha** — improved cookstoves reduced smoke exposure and freed up time

### Inclusive Play and Sports
Sponsored **17 Para athletes** across 6 states, 10+ disciplines (badminton, boccia, shooting, athletics, swimming).
- **Rakshitha Raju** — first Indian woman to compete in the 1500m at the Paris Paralympics
- **Shams Aalam** — first paraplegic swimmer to complete a record open-water swim
- Medal winners: Hem Chandra, Medha Jayath, Manjunatha L.H., Siddanna S. Sahukar, Shabana
- **Shilpa Puttaraju** — first Indian in the Wheelchair Tennis world Top 100
- Featured story: **Bhavani** (Andaman) — Para athlete and special educator supporting 350+ children

### Capacity Building & Research
- CBID certificate course — 3rd batch graduated (40/40 passed); 4th cohort enrolled; expanding to train 400 rehabilitation workers (persons with disabilities themselves) across 10 Karnataka districts (with National Fund for PwDs, RCI, Dept. of Empowerment of PwDs)
- New MoU with Regional Institute of Education, Mysuru, for inclusive-education teacher training (expanding via NERIE, RIE-Ajmer, RIE-Bhopal, RIE-Bhubaneswar)
- Developing an accessible e-learning course on disability inclusion for corporates
- Hosted Shri Rajesh Aggarwal, Secretary, Dept. of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (Govt. of India)

### Accessibility
Audited 20 government schools in Gujarat, LV Prasad Eye Institute (Telangana), a Bengaluru apartment complex, and 6 facilities of a multinational corporation. New academic collaborations with Delhi University, RVS Architecture College (Chennai), and KCG College of Technology (Chennai) to train student auditors.

### Donors (selected)
CBM International, CBM Switzerland, Standard Chartered Bank, SBI Foundation, ANZ, Fidelity Asia Pacific Foundation, IQVIA, Kuehne + Nagel, Cardinal Health, The Hans Foundation USA, National Fund for Persons with Disabilities, Rehabilitation Council of India.

### Governance/Statutory
No trustee is related to another; no trustee remuneration paid in FY24-25. Child/Adult-at-Risk Safeguarding Policy training conducted; POSH Act compliance maintained (Internal Committee notified, annual report filed with District Officer).

### Offices
National Office: Bengaluru (Chamrajpet). Field offices: Bodeli (Gujarat), Delhi, Jigani (Karnataka), Narmadapuram (MP), Naugarh (UP), Patharpratima (West Bengal), Tarikhet (Uttarakhand).

## FY 2023-24 — "Shifting Tides"

### Overview
A year of leadership transition — **Sony Thomas joined as Executive Director in 2023**. Recipient (historically) of the **Zero Project Innovation Award 2017** and the **Divyangjan Swabhiman Samman 2023**. Announced the new **2024–2030 strategy** ("Extending the Boundaries of Disability Inclusion: Sharing Joy").

### Headline Reach
- **32,99,940** (~3.3 million) people served through core activities
- **26** states and union territories covered
- **52** active projects

### Inclusive Health (20 states)
31,71,745 people received medical services · 2,14,728 surgeries/procedures · 1,96,440 assistive devices distributed · 183 inclusive vision centers.
- New vision center in Bheri, Haryana (with RIO Rohtak)
- Pediatric eye care launched in Andhra Pradesh coastal districts (targeting 180,000 children)
- School eye-health pilots: Sikkim (11,000+ children screened); South 24 Parganas, WB (42,000+ children, 200+ teachers trained)
- Transgender-inclusive eye camps, Hyderabad (400+ trans women)
- Low-vision centers in Karnataka (Chitradurga & Dharwad) — prompted a **state government directive to replicate statewide**
- Featured story: **Shyam**, overcoming fear of cataract surgery via Project Ujjalo

### Inclusive Education
36,981 children learning across settings · 948 teachers trained · 578 caregivers/parents trained · 10 inclusive community learning centers.
- Karnataka: Community Learning Centers (Doddaballapur, Jigani Hobli)
- Gujarat: CBID project, Chhotaudepur
- Assam: training with Samagra Shiksha
- Tripura: programme across 400 government schools (1,700+ children, 100 special educators)
- Meghalaya: learning center (160+ children)
- Madhya Pradesh: "Inclusive Hamara Gaon" project, Narmadapuram (800+ children)
- Featured story: **Harshitha**, a child with intellectual disability who progressed enough to join mainstream school

### Inclusive Livelihood
8,452 people trained in skills development · 989 community-based organisations formed/strengthened · 4,965 accessed financial services · 3,349 accessed employment.
- Climate-resilient/multi-layer farming, Siddharthnagar (UP)
- Dairy-farmer support, Kushinagar (1,000 farmers)
- Poultry/beekeeping livelihoods, Meghalaya (450 families)
- Tailoring & livestock programmes for women, Bengaluru (250+ people)
- Featured story: **Radheshyam**, a farmer with mobility impairment who improved dairy income and secured a Unique Disability ID and tricycle

### Inclusive Humanitarian Action
3,412 people reached · 12,073 trained on disability-inclusive disaster risk reduction · 2,845 families received improved cookstoves · 33 households received biodigesters.
- Flood relief, Himachal Pradesh (419 families)
- Search-and-rescue equipment, Bihar & West Bengal
- Sundarbans mangrove restoration (15,600 saplings; SHG livelihoods)
- Uttarakhand cookstove project achieved **Gold Standard registration** (Oct 2023) for carbon reduction
- Featured story: **Sushmita**, a mangrove-restoration participant in West Bengal gaining income/independence

### Inclusive Play and Sports
Launched **"Ananda Mela"** — Kolkata's first inclusive sensory park (7 Oct 2023), a CSR project with **LTIMindtree** and the New Town Kolkata Development Authority. Sponsored **8 Para athletes** heading toward the 2024/2028 Paralympics. Featured story: **Keshav**, a Para shooting athlete from Hubballi.

### Training, Capacity Building & Research
- CBID programme (RCI-accredited) — 2nd batch graduated (40/40 passed); 3rd cohort enrolled
- 3-day state-level Continuing Rehabilitation Education programme (24 participants, 9 experts)
- Training module developed for the Indo-German Chamber of Commerce on diversity/inclusive workplaces
- Published research: *"The Unmet Needs of People with Disability in India: Prevalence, Costs and Opportunities for Government Investments"* (SSRN, June 2023)
- End-term evaluation of Phase 1 "Inclusive Vision Centers" project

### Accessibility
Empaneled (Feb 2024) by the Dept. of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment) as an access auditor for 5 years. Audits conducted: 3 Sadguru Netra Chikitsalaya hospitals (Chitrakoot, MP); 2 vision centers (Namkhana & Pathar Pratima, WB); 10 government primary schools (Bodeli block, Gujarat).

### Donors (selected)
CBM International, CBM Switzerland, ANZ Support Services India, Arcadis Consulting, Cardinal Health International, Cognizant Foundation, George Clinical, LTIMindtree Foundation, PwC Service Delivery Centres (Bangalore & Kolkata), Rehabilitation Council of India, SBI Foundation, SMFG India Credit, Standard Chartered Bank, Technicolor India, The Hans Foundation USA.

### Governance & Advisors

Board of Advisors: Sajan Thomas (HR/Social Synthesis), Mallikarjuna Sarma (IT/data security), Dr. Dominic Misquith (Community Health, St. John's), Kanikaraj (labour law), Dr. M. Kandasami (NGO financial management), Kanti Joshi (POSH advisor/SASHA), T.D. Dhariyal (Disability Inclusion Advisor, former Delhi State Commissioner for PwD).

### About CBM India Trust (as stated in the report)
CBM India Trust is a development organisation committed to improving the quality of life of persons with disabilities and other disadvantaged communities in India, working across 26+ states to remove cultural and environmental barriers to full participation in health, education, and livelihoods.

**Vision:** An inclusive society in which all persons with disabilities achieve their full potential.
**Mission:** Improve quality of life of persons with disabilities/disadvantaged communities without discrimination; promote inclusive/sustainable development to break the poverty-disability cycle; promote enabling environments for empowerment.
**Values:** Inclusion · Innovation · Integrity · Collaboration · Compassion.

## FY 2019-20

### Overview
Written by **Dr. Sara Varughese, Managing Trustee** — frames the year as one of "significant change," capped by the onset of COVID-19 at year-end, to which CBM India says it responded "from the very start... with enthusiasm and courage." Vision cited: *"an inclusive world in which all persons with disabilities enjoy their human rights and achieve their full potential."* Operating across **26 states**, aligned with the UN 2030 Agenda/SDGs ("leaving no one behind") and the disability-movement motto **"Nothing about Us without Us."**

### Headline Numbers
- **24,91,655** lives touched · **48** project partners · **57** projects initiated
- Health: 24,00,341 reached · Livelihood: 82,571 reached · Education: 8,743 reached
- 1,72,424 eye operations supported · 1,81,100 assistive devices distributed · 18,644 medical professionals trained · 8,985 people obtained financial services

### Inclusive Health Care
22,67,518 people received medical eye services · 70,953 ear-condition services · 38,753 orthopaedic services · 7,895 community mental-health services.
- Field-tested WHO's Primary Ear and Hearing Care resources (Bangalore/Vellore, with St. John's Hospital & CMC Vellore)
- Launched "Project Sarvabhaumik Nainika" (Kuppam block, Vellore, Tamil Nadu) for comprehensive eye care
- New cataract initiative with Sundarban Social Development Centre, West Bengal
- Featured stories: **Kamla Prasad** (weaver, Varanasi) and **Radha** (Mathura) — both restored to independence via cataract surgery

### Inclusive Livelihood
10,350 vocational trainees · 7,490 gained employment · 8,985 accessed financial services · 26,825 reached overall.
- **Jagruti Project** (5 districts, 4 states) — mainstreaming PWDs into sustainable livelihoods; goat-farming training with the Central Institute for Research on Goat (CIRG), Mathura
- **Parivartan** (flagship UP programme) — economic/social development for PWDs; featured **Surendra**, an amputee turned bicycle-shop owner
- Featured story: **Usha** (deaf woman, UP) — SHG/DPO-enabled goat farming and retail business via Purvanchal Gramin Seva Samiti

### Inclusive Education
7,576 children with disabilities enrolled in school · 7,204 teachers trained · 1,535 parents/caregivers trained · 75% of PWDs enrolled in inclusive (not segregated) settings.
- Co-published "N for Nose" State of Education Report 2019 (with UNESCO & TISS)
- Launched "Assessment for All" tool with Pratham NGO
- Inclusive playgrounds in Tura and Guwahati (Australian High Commission-funded); community resource centre at a BMC school, Mumbai (HDFC-funded); disabled-friendly computer labs in Tripura (ONGC-funded)
- Featured stories: **Wandalin Char** (teacher, Bethany Society, Shillong) and **Marker Kato** (hearing-impaired student, Arunachal Pradesh)

### Humanitarian Response
- **Cyclone Fani** (Odisha, May 2019): 23,638 people reached; medical camps for 2,600+; 272 children given braille kits; 20 PWDs' livelihoods restored (with Thomson Reuters Foundation)
- **Cyclone Bulbul**: 233 vulnerable families supported (South 24 Parganas, WB)
- **Kerala Floods — "Project Shakthi"**: rebuilt livelihoods for 150 fishing families (34 new boats, 48 repaired, 15 new engines, gear for all); 50 women-headed households got mollusc-processing units; cold storage set up; new DPOs/SHGs; disability-inclusive disaster-risk-reduction training. Featured story: **Gopi P.K.**, fisherman (Vechoor, Kerala), supported with Wipro

### Capacity Building
Trainings on community mental health (Mussoorie), intellectual/developmental disabilities (PGSS Gorakhpur), clinical low vision (Minto Eye Hospital Bengaluru; Joseph Eye Hospital Trichy), patient-care counselling (MGM Eye Institute Raipur), OT standard protocols (LV Prasad Eye Institute Vishakhapatnam), goat farming, FPOs, and personal safety/sexuality education.

### Mainstreaming Disability
Accessibility audits of corporate buildings for **Mercedes-Benz R&D India, Amadeus, and Walmart** (Bangalore/Pune). State-level consultation with 16 universities/colleges (with Kristu Jayanti College) to embed disability in social-work curricula. Input provided to India's Draft National Education Policy 2019. Capacity-building of 4 partners + 32 DPOs for inclusive elections in Jharkhand, Bihar, Chhattisgarh.

### Resource Mobilisation
Secured Standard Chartered Bank funding for **44 disability-inclusive vision centres** across MP, UP, and Maharashtra (3-year project, ~22 lakh population coverage). Corporate partners: Tata Capital, Tata Capital Financial Services, SBI Life Insurance, Thomson Reuters, VSL India, Endurance India, Collective Good Foundation, Wipro Cares, Sanofi, ONGC, CRISIL, NASSCOM, Adobe, CSRBox, The Hans Foundation, HDFC. Also: International Deaf Summit (Goa) support, 92 school awareness sessions, 70+ press mentions, 15+ donors, 30+ volunteers.

### Governance
Board of Trustees: Dr. Francis Joseph Stanley, Dr. Sara Varughese (Managing Trustee), Dr. Vinod Shah, Mr. Varghese Chandy, Mr. George Abraham. Board of Advisors: Dr. Harpreet Kapoor, Dr. Suneela Garg, Dr. Dominic Misquith, Dr. M.N.G. Mani, Dr. G.V.S. Murthy, Dr. M. Kandasami, T.D. Dhariyal. POSH Act, 2013 compliance confirmed (Internal Committee in place, annual compliance report filed).

### Offices
Bangalore (Commerce Cube, Chamarajpet) and Delhi (PHD House, August Kranti Marg).
