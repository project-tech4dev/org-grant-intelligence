## 2. Vision, Mission & Core Values
Source: https://www.cbmindia.org/about-us/

**Vision:** "An inclusive society where persons with disabilities achieve their full potential."

**Mission:** CBM India is committed to:
- Improve the quality of life of persons with disabilities and other disadvantaged communities without any discrimination.
- Promote inclusive and sustainable development to break the vicious cycle of poverty and disability.
- Promote enabling environments for the empowerment of persons with disabilities as vibrant, contributing members of society.

**Core Values:**
- **Inclusion** — persons with disabilities are equal partners in creating interventions, not just beneficiaries.
- **Integrity** — resources used effectively; work grounded in transparency and accountability.
- **Innovation** — programs designed to stay relevant and adaptable to shifting needs.
- **Compassion** — genuine empathy, respecting individuals' dignity and rights.
- **Collaboration** — partnering with communities, governments, and stakeholders to amplify impact.

## 3. History & 2030 Strategy
Source: https://www.cbmindia.org/about-us/

CBM India's work began over 55 years ago (~1967), evolving from an organisation focused on education, rehabilitation, and eye care into a national champion for disability inclusion. The India Trust entity itself was formally registered in 1994.

**Looking ahead to 2030 — "Extending the Boundaries of Disability Inclusion; Sharing Joy":** CBM India is expanding its strategy to reach **80,00,000 people (8 million)**, including **8,00,000 persons with disabilities (800,000)**, across healthcare, education, livelihoods, humanitarian response, and environment. Planned approaches:
1. **Strengthening the voice and autonomy of persons with disabilities** — empowering self-advocacy and participation in decisions affecting their future.
2. **Building inclusive communities** — with equal opportunities for economic empowerment, education, and health.
3. **Addressing avoidable impairments, disasters, and disabling environments** — tackling avoidable blindness/visual impairment in high-need areas, scaling cross-disability programmes, improving accessibility, strengthening inclusive disaster preparedness, and developing inclusive climate-change approaches.
4. **Demonstrating excellence in sustainable inclusive development** — with a strong focus on results and accountability.

## 5. What We Do — Programme Areas
Source: individual `/inclusive-*` and related pages

### Inclusive Health (flagship programme)
Focuses on treatment, rehabilitation, and prevention for persons with disabilities and marginalised communities facing financial, accessibility, and stigma-related barriers to healthcare.
- **Inclusive Vision Centers** — community-based eye-care model; outreach/screening + partner-hospital referrals for specialised treatment.
- **Low-Vision and Rehabilitation Services** — supporting private/government/NGO hospitals to set up low-vision clinics; specialised professional training.
- **Eye Screening Initiatives** in schools and communities, plus awareness activities.
- **Assistive Devices Distribution** — wheelchairs, hearing aids, eyeglasses, walkers, canes, etc.
- **Ear and Hearing Care** — newborn screening, hearing-aid distribution, speech/hearing therapy.
- **Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation** — clubfoot identification/treatment in children, parent counselling, ongoing therapy.
- **Capacity Building of Health Workers** — training on disability rights/inclusion.
- **FY2024-25 impact:** 16,55,037 people received medical services · 7,800 assistive devices distributed · 3,325 surgeries/procedures supported · 131 inclusive vision centers.

### Inclusive Education
Aims to bridge learning gaps and ensure smooth transition to mainstream classrooms, with early intervention and rehabilitation integrated throughout.
- **Whole School Model** — transforms mainstream schools (removing physical/digital barriers, teacher training, strengthening School Management Committees); Universal Design for Learning approach; inclusive sports/STEM/science labs.
- **Inclusive Community Learning Centers (ICLCs)** — medical screenings, education interventions, therapy, assistive devices, caregiver training/counselling; mostly on government school campuses.
- **Disability-Inclusive Resource Centers** — academic support, rehabilitation, health resources, cultural opportunities; staffed by special educators, physiotherapists, speech therapists.
- **Professional Training for Educators** — research-backed resources and regular training on inclusive teaching practices.
- **FY2024-25 impact:** 3,208 children learning across educational settings · 292 teachers trained · 360 caregivers/parents trained · 5 inclusive community learning centers.

### Inclusive Livelihood
Creates pathways to economic independence for persons with disabilities facing employment barriers, prejudice, and inadequate support systems.
- **Skills Development** — customised training (farming, animal husbandry, beekeeping, mushroom cultivation, handicrafts, tailoring, etc.) with local-expert-led sessions.
- **Government Access & Entrepreneurship Support** — helping participants access social-protection schemes, financial aid, assistive devices; seed funding for micro-enterprises plus infrastructure/sourcing/customer-connection support.
- **Community Inclusion and Participation** — building inclusive Self-Help Groups (iSHGs), Organizations of Persons with Disabilities (OPDs), and inclusive Farmers Producer Organizations (iFPOs).
- **FY2024-25 impact:** 3,442 people trained in skills development · 127 accessed government schemes · 308 accessed financial services · 142 inclusive community-based organisations formed/strengthened.

### Inclusive Humanitarian Action (incl. Climate/Environment)
Addresses the disproportionate impact of climate change and humanitarian crises on persons with disabilities.
- **Emergency Response** — crisis assistance plus post-disaster rehabilitation.
- **Capacity Building** — Village Disaster Management Committees, Task Force Groups, community risk-mapping, search-and-rescue equipment.
- **Climate Action** — cleaner/safer energy & cooking methods, reforestation for coastal-community protection.
- **FY2024-25 impact:** 15,770 mangrove saplings planted · 1,002 people reached via humanitarian assistance · 1,171 families received improved cookstoves · 900 people trained on climate-resilient agriculture. (Notable past response: Cyclone Remal recovery support.)

### Inclusive Play & Sports
Promotes accessible play spaces and supports Para athletes.
- **Ananda Mela** — Kolkata's first inclusive sensory park (inaugurated 7 Oct 2023), a CSR-funded project with New Town Kolkata Development Authority, built to National Accessibility Guidelines/Universal Design standards.
- **Para Athlete Support (launched 2023)** — backing athletes across Para badminton, wheelchair tennis, shooting Para sport, Para athletics, Para swimming, toward Paralympic-level competition (e.g., swimmer Shams Aalam, 1500m runner Rakshitha Raju — 2024 Paris Paralympics).

### Capacity Building & Research
- **Certificate Course in Community Based Inclusive Development** — a 6-month course, CBM India is an RCI (Rehabilitation Council of India)-approved training institute; co-designed with RCI and the University of Melbourne, training grassroots rehabilitation workers.
- **Capacity building for educators, healthcare professionals, and grassroots workers** — inclusive teaching methods, disability-inclusive healthcare practices.
- **Evaluation and Research** — project documentation/evaluation, contributing reports and papers on disability inclusion in the Indian context.

### Accessibility
CBM India is an **empaneled access auditor** under the Government of India's **Accessible India Campaign (Sugamya Bharat Abhiyan)**.
- Conducts detailed physical-space assessments (offices, government buildings, schools, healthcare facilities) guided by Universal Design principles, helping organisations comply with the **Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act**.
- Past access audits cover: educational institutions, corporate workplaces, hospitals/health facilities, public buildings and transport.
- Accessibility consulting enquiries: info@cbmindia.org.

## 10. Privacy Policy & Donation Refund Policy
Source: https://www.cbmindia.org/privacy-policy/ ; https://www.cbmindia.org/donation-refund-policy/

**Privacy Policy highlights:** Applies to cbmindia.org (property of CBM India Trust, reg. 431/1994). Collects contact info, billing info (never credit card numbers — handled by payment partner), user-posted content, demographic info, and passive tracking data (IP, browser, Google Analytics/cookies). Used for donation confirmation, responding to queries, service improvement, site-trend analysis, security, and marketing communications. Shared with service-performing third parties (under confidentiality agreements), for legal compliance, or in an organisational succession — but **never sold**. Email opt-out available via info@cbmindia.org (processing may take ~10 days; transactional messages continue regardless). Grievance Officer: Valentina Gazzola (contact above). Policy last updated **30.09.2021**; disputes governed by Indian law.

**Donation Refund Policy:** CBM India will examine and attempt to process refund requests for donations made in error, but is **not obligated** to refund — particularly once a tax-exemption (80G) certificate has been issued. Refund requests must be made within **5 days** of the donation; later requests will not be processed. Additional supporting documents may be requested.
