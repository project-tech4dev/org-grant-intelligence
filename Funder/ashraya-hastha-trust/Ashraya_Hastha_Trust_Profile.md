## 2. Our Story / History
Source: https://ashrayahasthatrust.org/who-we-are/ ; https://www.forbesindia.com/article/leadership/k-dinesh-and-family-the-generous-givers/91219/1

Ashraya Hastha Trust (AHT) is a charitable foundation that advances ideas and supports organisations promoting a better, more equal world. For over 20 years, it has supported efforts to advance healthcare and education for all, preserve the environment, strengthen livelihood opportunities, champion animal welfare, and make philanthropy more effective in service of historically marginalised communities.

**Founding:** The foundation was established in **2000** by **(retd.) Major S. Nanjundiah** and his family — his daughter **Asha Dinesh**, her husband **K. Dinesh**, and their daughters **Divya Dinesh** and **Deeksha Dinesh** — to help India's rural poor. Per Forbes India, K. Dinesh co-founded the trust with his wife Asha and father-in-law S. Nanjundiah in 2000, while daughters Divya and Deeksha (associated informally from the start) formally joined as trustees around 2013/2016.

K. Dinesh began devoting more time to personal philanthropy after stepping down from the Infosys board in 2011, though the family had institutionalised its giving through AHT a decade earlier, in 2000.

The Trust's philanthropic approach and core grantmaking areas remain connected to the founding family's principles and values.

## 3. Mission & Vision
Source: https://ashrayahasthatrust.org/who-we-are/ ; homepage

**Vision:** "To be a change agent and partner in building an inclusive, equitable and sustainable world for people, animals, and nature."

**Homepage framing:** "Advancing a just, equitable, and abundant future where people and planet thrive" — AHT supports work to enhance health outcomes, protect endangered ecosystems, promote access to education, create dignified livelihood opportunities, and champion animal welfare.

**Three stated strategic thrusts (homepage):**
1. **"We expand human potential"** — empowering mission-driven organisations to expand the power and potential inherent in the grassroots, where under-resourced communities fight for equal access to education, health, and opportunity.
2. **"We advocate for human-animal coexistence"** — fostering coexistence, acceptance, and tolerance where the interests of both people and animals are satisfied, supported by healthy ecosystems.
3. **"We enable conversations for growth"** — supporting organisations deploying solutions for socially progressive and equitable growth, accelerating progress toward a more inclusive and sustainable world.

**Quote (K. Dinesh, Co-founder Infosys & Managing Trustee, AHT):** "We are primarily a grantmaking organisation leveraging our funds to accelerate progress towards an equitable society. It is in this spirit that we award grants to nonprofits furthering social and environmental wellbeing."

## 5. What We Do — Focus Areas & Grantee Snapshots
Source: https://ashrayahasthatrust.org/what-we-do/ and individual `/project-category/` pages

AHT funds nonprofits across six focus areas. Below is each area's description plus the "grantee snapshot" examples published on the official site.

### Health
*"Facilitating equity-focused solutions to improve health outcomes for underserved populations."* AHT supports organisations strengthening healthcare infrastructure and enhancing availability of quality healthcare to accelerate improved health outcomes for India's most vulnerable populations.
- **Smile Train** — enhancing comprehensive cleft lip and palate care for children in need. (Per Smile Train India: partnership started 2019, extended for 5 years, supporting 10,000 cleft surgeries and 5 capacity-development trainings for local medical professionals, plus safety/mobilisation infrastructure at partner hospitals.)
- **NIMHANS** (National Institute of Mental Health and Neurosciences) — piloting a holistic, comprehensive rural mental health programme in two taluks in Karnataka and Uttarakhand.
- **Swami Vivekananda Youth Movement** — improving maternal and neonatal health among tribal communities.

### Education
*"Working to eliminate learning gaps and facilitate equitable access to education, knowledge, and resources."* AHT fosters school continuation for adolescent girls, makes education more inclusive for children with disabilities, provides skills training leading to meaningful employment, and advocates for safe work environments for women.
- **Project Potential** — capacity-building support for a grassroots organisation championing sustainable rural development.
- **Karnataka Health Promotion Trust** — empowering adolescent girls to champion conversations related to their education, health, and early marriage.
- **Durga India** — advocating gender justice and deterring sexual harassment to create gender-equitable public spaces.

### Livelihoods
*"Helping to increase socioeconomic mobility for communities with low incomes and limited opportunities for dignified livelihoods."* AHT works to create inclusive and sustainable livelihoods across rural India so people have reliable incomes and accelerated prosperity.
- **SELCO** — leveraging sustainable energy solutions to catalyse local livelihoods.
- **Mantra4Change** — improving livelihood opportunities via skills training for women and youth in villages along the Line of Control.
- **SeSTA** — empowering marginalised women through agri-based livelihood diversification.

### Animal Welfare
*"Protecting an increasingly vulnerable population that plays a key role in balancing our fragile ecosystem."* AHT partners with organisations taking a proactive approach to animal-welfare concerns, threats, and uncertainties in both natural and urban environments.
- **Under the Mango Tree Society** — training smallholder farmers in honeybee preservation and sustainable beekeeping to safeguard biodiversity and food security.
- **CUPA** (Compassion Unlimited Plus Action) — establishing a fully equipped animal birth control centre for street dogs.
- **People for Animals, Bangalore** — providing a state-of-the-art enclosure for rescue, recovery, and rehabilitation of confiscated and seriously injured reptiles.

### Environment
*"Accelerating adoption of environment-friendly tools, technologies, and solutions to help cities combat and adapt to climate change."* AHT funds nature-based solutions that build climate resilience, supporting the transition to a regenerative economy via community-led biodiversity and ecosystem work.
- **Samerth Charitable Trust** — supporting water conservation and livestock development to improve livelihoods of marginalised communities.
- **Hasiru Dala** — piloting a sustainable zero-waste management solution aiming to create a model village.
- **Center for Youth and Social Development** — rejuvenating water sources and improving soil fertility through sustainable agriculture practices.

### Disaster Relief
*"Supporting regions impacted by natural calamities by ensuring timely access to medical aid, shelter, food and water."* AHT coordinates with on-ground partners and aid organisations to address the immediate needs of disaster-hit communities with life-saving aid.
- **Assam Flood Relief** (Assam, 2022) — distribution of medicines, food kits, hygiene kits, and other life-saving aid.
- **Cyclone Amphan** (Kolkata, 2020) — ensuring access to food for families impacted by the cyclone.
- **COVID-19** (Pan-India, 2020–2021) — providing food, medical equipment, and other support during COVID-19 and the lockdowns.

## 6. How We Work

### Our Approach
Source: https://ashrayahasthatrust.org/how-we-work/
1. AHT identifies organisations and projects through outreach and directly approaches them when it sees opportunity and alignment with its vision, values, and philanthropic priorities.
2. It encourages work driving long-term, systemic change in underserved and unreached communities.
3. It seeks dynamic, trust-based, highly engaged partnerships centred on bold agendas, shared learning, and mutual accountability.

### Grants
"Increasing access to opportunities" — AHT's grantmaking is structured to prioritise funding projects and solutions that are **scalable, cost-effective, sustainable**, and designed to improve the wellbeing of people and planet. It welcomes enquiries from the community and actively seeks funding partnerships offering creative, measurable solutions to community challenges.

### What We Fund
AHT prioritises high-impact organisations that promote radical social change and deliver measurable impact, encouraging nonprofits to apply for funding on pioneering projects addressing the most pressing health, economic, and environmental challenges.

### Submitting a Proposal
Source: https://ashrayahasthatrust.org/start-your-proposal/
"We fund projects that support urgent, large-scale transformation, with a focus on catalysing social equity." Prospective grantees are directed to read detailed guidelines before submitting an application via the site's "Start Your Proposal" / "Submit your Query" form (fields: organisation name, contact name, email, phone, and a query of ≤100 words).

### Grant Partner Criteria
Source: https://ashrayahasthatrust.org/how-we-work/ (Grant Partner Criteria tab)
AHT's partnerships and grantmaking are best suited to organisations that meet **all** of the following criteria:
1. Registered as a nonprofit in India — as a Section 8 company, Trust, or Society — and **12A/80G certified**, for at least **3 years**.
2. Working to solve complex socio-economic issues that impact a significant section of society.
3. Have a programme/solution in the **post-pilot or validation stage** (not early/untested ideas).
4. On a clear path to scale, with a replicable solution — via government partnerships or otherwise.
5. Staffed with a dedicated full-time team.
6. Aligned with AHT's commitment to measurement, evaluation, and learning in a demonstrable manner.
7. Share AHT's core values and sense of urgency to accelerate social progress.

### Funding Focus — Summary Pillars
Source: https://ashrayahasthatrust.org/how-we-work/
AHT summarises its funding commitment around three pillars: **Sustainable Approaches**, **Scalable Solutions**, and **Measurable Impact** — applied across education, healthcare, environment, livelihoods, and animal welfare.

### Evaluation & Selection Process for Applicants
Source: https://ashrayahasthatrust.org/general-instructions-for-grant-seeking-organisations/
After an Expression of Interest (EOI) is submitted, evaluation and selection typically takes **3–6 months** (depending on application volume), following four steps:
1. **EOI evaluation** — if not selected, the organisation is informed by email.
2. **Request for a detailed project proposal and budget** (for shortlisted organisations).
3. **Initial presentation and discussion** of the proposed project with the AHT team.
4. **Final decision communicated** to applicants on whether their proposal has been selected for funding.

## 9. Legal, Privacy & Anti-Fraud Notices

### Privacy Policy (grantee confidentiality)
Source: https://ashrayahasthatrust.org/privacy-policy/
AHT states it is committed to protecting the confidentiality of grant applications/proposals via: limiting internal access (with signed NDAs and training for authorised personnel), secure storage (password-protected servers/locked cabinets), need-to-know information sharing, anonymising information where necessary, and secure disposal of information no longer needed.

**Scam/fraud alert:** AHT explicitly states it does **not** communicate through generic email addresses, social media, instant messaging, postal mail, fax, or unofficial websites. Legitimate AHT correspondence always originates from an **@ashrayahasthatrust.org** domain address. AHT does not use social media to solicit beneficiaries, approach organisations via third-party agents, charge any fees when awarding grants, offer prizes via any channel, request registration fees for grant opportunities, solicit investment, or solicit public donations. Suspicious communications claiming to be from AHT should be reported to the Trust by email.

### Terms of Use
