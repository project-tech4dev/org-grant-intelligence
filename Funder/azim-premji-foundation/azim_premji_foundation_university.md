# Azim Premji University

Source pages: azimpremjiuniversity.edu.in, azimpremjiuniversity.edu.in/azim-premji-foundation, azimpremjifoundation.org/who-we-are/how-we-are-organised

## Overview
Azim Premji University (APU) is a **private, not-for-profit university** established in **2010** by the Azim Premji Foundation, under the Azim Premji University Act (2010), recognised by the **University Grants Commission (UGC)** under Section 2F. It is funded entirely by the philanthropic endowment of Azim Premji, former Chairman of Wipro Limited.

- **Motto:** "Towards a just, equitable, humane and sustainable society"
- **Chancellor:** Azim Premji
- **Vice-Chancellor (Bengaluru):** Richa Govil
- **Vice-Chancellor (Bhopal):** Gautam Pandey (founding VC of the University overall was Anurag Behar)
- **Director (Bengaluru):** Arima Mishra
- **Type:** Private, unfunded/philanthropic university
- **Students:** ~3,200+ (roughly 1,300 undergraduates, 880+ postgraduates as of recent data, growing with new campuses)
- **Staff:** ~415+

## History
- Grew out of the Azim Premji Foundation (founded 2001), which initially worked on improving India's government school system in rural/disadvantaged areas.
- Founded in **2010 in Bengaluru**, initially offering two programmes (Education and Development) plus courses for working professionals.
- Operated from an Electronic City campus until 2020, then moved to its own dedicated **90-acre campus** on Sarjapur-Attibele Road, Bengaluru.
- A second, **50-acre campus opened in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh in 2023**.
- Further campuses planned/underway in **Ranchi (Jharkhand)**, operational from 2026, and **Palashbari (Assam)**.

## Campuses
- **Bengaluru Campus** — 90 acres; classrooms, residential facilities, a public-facing library (43,837 sq ft), seminar halls, an amphitheatre, sports facilities, and one of the Azim Premji Schools (a free K-12 school for disadvantaged children) located on campus.
- **Bhopal Campus** — 50 acres, opened 2023; local Madhya Pradesh architectural influences; strong sustainability features (rainwater harvesting, water treatment); full accessibility for persons with disabilities.
- **Ranchi Campus** — 150-acre site; construction underway; will house the University's health-sciences programmes plus the Foundation's first large hospital (see Health file); operational from 2026.
- **Northeast Campus (planned)** — location cited as Palashbari, Assam.

## Academic Programmes

**Undergraduate (4-year Honours degrees):**
- BA: Economics, English, History, Media and Communications, Philosophy, Politics and Economics (PPE), Social Science
- BSc: Biology, Chemistry, Environmental Science and Sustainability, Physics, Mathematics, Information Science, Psychology
- Integrated Teacher Education Programme (ITEP), BSc BEd dual degree
- Every UG student also completes an "Occupational Minor"

**Postgraduate:**
- MA in Development
- MA in Economics
- MA in Education
- MA in Early Childhood Care and Education (ECCE)
- MSc in Climate Change and Sustainability
- Master of Public Health (MPH) — launched 2023, Bhopal campus
- Executive MBA (social-sector focused)

**Diplomas / Short & Continuing-Education Programmes:**
- ~150 short-term diploma/certificate/professional-development courses across education, development, policy, governance, and sustainability (e.g., Inclusive Education, Teaching Children with Learning Disabilities, Early Childhood Education, Educational Assessment, "Research for Social Action," "Exploring Sustainability in the Indian Context," GIS mapping).
- Continuing-education/capacity-development programmes reach **over 1 lakh (100,000) practitioners annually** — teachers, senior government officials, and civil-society members — in areas like early childhood education, inclusive education, disability, assessment, educational leadership, social-sector leadership, and local democracy.

## Research Centres
- **Centre for Climate Change and Sustainability** (est. 2019) — publishes reports such as "Navigating India's Climate Future" and "Cities and Canopies," plus the "Indian Coastal Region: Climate Projections 2021–2040" report.
- **Centre for Sustainable Employment** (est. 2017) — known for the widely-cited **State of Working India (SWI)** report series covering labour-market issues, NREGA, urban employment schemes, and COVID-19's impact on workers.
- Dedicated research centre on **Local Democracy (Panchayats)**.
- A **University Research Funding Programme** supported by the Foundation (including a Health Research Funding stream, ₹5–40 lakh per study, 12–24 months).

## Publications & Public Resources
- Produces a large collection of free teaching-learning material: subject magazines, development case studies, online textbooks.
- **Anuvada Sampada** — a collection of higher-educational resources translated into Indian languages.
- Public digital archive of articles and titles from the Foundation and affiliates.

## Fees & Financial Aid (indicative figures, 2026 batch)
- **UG Tuition (Bengaluru):** ~₹3,37,100–4,01,500/year (rising annually); total ~₹14.75 lakh over 4 years; accommodation ~₹80,000/year.
- **UG Tuition (Bhopal):** ~₹3,68,700/year flat; total ~₹14.75 lakh over 4 years (regulated under the MP Private University Regulatory Commission).
- **ITEP BSc BEd:** significantly subsidised (~₹60,000–71,500/year tuition).
- Additional costs: ~₹1.2–1.5 lakh over 4 years for internships/field projects; food ~₹5,000–6,000/month.
- **Need-based scholarships:** available for students with annual family income under ₹10 lakh, covering tuition/accommodation (and in exceptional cases food); awarded via documentation review and third-party verification.
- Group insurance (life, hospitalisation, accident) provided at no extra cost.
- **PG programmes:** tuition ~₹2.65 lakh/year with accommodation ~₹60,000/year (with a 50% tuition-waiver option available in some programmes).
- Overall, **~60% of students are on scholarships**; the University itself is **entirely philanthropic** — currently **89% of expenditure is borne by the Foundation**.

## Accreditation & Rankings
- **NAAC accredited** ("A" grade reported by Times Higher Education; some sources cite A++).
- Ranked around **#11 by NIRF** (India's National Institutional Ranking Framework) in the relevant category, per Careers360.
- Recognised by relevant statutory bodies for specific professional programmes (e.g., teacher-education recognition).

## Student Body & Placements
- **Over 5,300 students across 13 cohorts** have graduated from the masters-level programmes.
- Almost **100% of master's programme graduates** have received job offers on campus; **~90% have chosen to work in the social sector**, many in grassroots field locations.
- **140+ placement organisations** visit for recruitment, largely from the social/development sector, NGOs, policy, education, and public health domains.
- Student body drawn from **29 states** and varied socio-economic backgrounds; 100+ student clubs and events; 2,500+ student residences across campuses.

## Distinctive Features
- Entirely philanthropic, not-for-profit model — no dependence on tuition for sustainability, enabling subsidised fees and scholarships.
- Strong focus on the **social sector**: education, development, public policy, governance, sustainability, and equity.
- Houses one of the **Azim Premji Schools** directly on the Bengaluru campus.
- Library network open to the public.
- Strong interdisciplinary, practice-oriented pedagogy; mandatory "Occupational Minors" for UG students.
- Integrates real grassroots/systemic knowledge (from the Foundation's Field Institutes) into policy, research, and continuing education — the University and the Foundation's on-the-ground operations are explicitly designed to inform each other.
