# Summary of All Downloaded PDFs — Azim Premji Foundation / University

This file gives a short summary of every PDF report downloaded from the official Azim Premji Foundation (azimpremjifoundation.org) and Azim Premji University (azimpremjiuniversity.edu.in) sites, so you don't have to open each one individually. Files are grouped by topic. (See `azim_premji_foundation_only_pdfs.md` for the Keep/Remove relevance recommendation on each.)

## Flagship Organisational Reports

**`Azim_Premji_Foundation_Report_June_2025.pdf`** (36 pages)
The Foundation's own half-yearly/annual report. Covers approach & objective, organisational snapshots, the three operating units (Field Institutes, Grants, University), and a breakdown of direct work in Education, Health, Livelihoods and Other Areas. This edition updates financial figures as of 31 March 2025; the narrative content is otherwise unchanged from the December 2024 edition.

**`Azim_Premji_Foundation_Report_June_2026.pdf`** (40 pages)
Latest edition of the same flagship report. Reports the Foundation working with **2,900+ members and 1,640 partner organisations**; institutional presence of **59 District Institutes (296 Teacher Learning Centres), 16 offices, 11 Azim Premji Schools, and 3 university campuses** (1 more planned). Covers Education, Health, Livelihoods, and Other Areas with updated figures as of March 2026.

## Livelihoods / Employment Research

**`APU_Report_StateOfWorkingIndia_2018.pdf`** (170 pages)
The first "State of Working India" (SWI) report from APU's Centre for Sustainable Employment — the flagship annual/periodic study of India's labour market. Covers who is looking for work, where the work is, how good the work is, and who does the work. *(Note: this file is a scanned/image PDF with no extractable text layer — contents inferred from its known published structure, not machine-read text.)*

**`APU_Report_StateOfWorkingIndia_2019.pdf`** (146 pages)
SWI 2019 — continuation of the labour-market series (informal employment, wages, employment trends).

**`APU_Report_StateOfWorkingIndia_2021.pdf`** (236 pages)
"State of Working India 2021: One Year of Covid-19" — analyses the pandemic's impact on jobs, incomes, and informal workers one year into COVID-19; published by APU's Centre for Sustainable Employment.

**`APU_Report_StateOfWorkingIndia_2023.pdf`** (239 pages)
"State of Working India 2023: Social Identities and Labour Market Outcomes" — examines how caste, gender, and other social identities shape employment and earnings outcomes in India.

**`APU_Report_StateOfWorkingIndia_2026_ExecutiveSummary.pdf`** (Executive Summary only; full report exceeded the 100MB download limit)
"Youth in the Labour Market: Pathways from Learning to Earning" — traces how young workers transition from school/college into employment, and how that transition has changed over the last 40 years, amid India's peaking demographic dividend.

**`APU_Report_MGNREGA_Employment_Guarantee_Covid.pdf`** (80 pages)
"Employment Guarantee During Covid-19: Role of MGNREGA in the Year After the 2020 Lockdown" — evaluates how India's rural employment guarantee scheme (MGNREGA) functioned as a safety net for workers in the year following the 2020 lockdown. Directly relevant to the Foundation's Livelihoods work, which relies heavily on MGNREGA entitlements.

**`APU_Report_Reviving_Employment_Livelihoods_Covid.pdf`** (66 pages)
"Reviving Employment and Livelihoods in India: COVID-19 and After" — a joint report by the Confederation of Indian Industry (CII) and Azim Premji University (authored by Dr. Amit Basole), published Feb 2021. Assesses pandemic damage to employment/livelihoods and proposes recovery policy measures.

**`APU_Report_USENET_UrbanEmployment.pdf`** (46 pages)
"Creating Udyog Sahayak Enterprises Network (USENET) for Employment Generation and Scale-up in the MSME Sector" — a policy proposal (authored by T. Muralidharan, Bino Paul & Amit Basole) developed for a Government of India Working Group of Ministers on Employment and Skill Generation (2020), submitted to the Prime Minister, in partnership with FICCI and TISS.

**`APU_Report_Farmer_Producer_Companies_2022.pdf`** (48 pages)
Report on Farmer Producer Companies (FPCs) — India's model for farmer collectivisation — examining their functioning and outcomes (2022 edition/"Report 2").

**`APU_Report_Farmer_Producer_Companies_PastPresentFuture.pdf`** (106 pages)
"Farmer Producer Companies: Past, Present and Future" by Richa Govil, Annapurna Neti, and Madhushree R. Rao — a comprehensive history and assessment of the FPC model in India and its outlook, an earlier/broader companion to the 2022 report above.

**`APU_Report_Microenterprises_In_India_2019.pdf`** (47 pages)
"Microenterprises in India: A Multidimensional Analysis" by Amit Basole (APU) and Vidya Chandy (Global Alliance for Mass Entrepreneurship/GAME), October 2019 — a data-driven look at India's microenterprise sector (size, informality, challenges), published jointly with GAME/Junior Achievement India.

## Rural Deprivation / Geographic Targeting Research

**`APU_Report_RuralDeprivationIndex_Jharkhand.pdf`** (large file, 2025)
"Rural Multidimensional Deprivation in Jharkhand: A Data-Driven Analysis 2025" — constructs a Rural Deprivation Index (RDI) using village-level infrastructure-provisioning data (not just income/consumption) from the 2019 Mission Antyodaya Survey, covering 31,175 of Jharkhand's 32,620 villages (95.6%). Produces block/district/state-level policy recommendations — directly usable for targeting NGO/funder work in Jharkhand.

**`APU_Report_RuralDeprivationIndex_Chhattisgarh.pdf`**
"Rural Multidimensional Deprivation in Chhattisgarh: A Data-Driven Analysis" — same RDI methodology applied to Chhattisgarh, decomposable to block/taluka/district level; intended to help Panchayats and policymakers identify which villages lack which specific amenities.

## Health

**`APU_Report_HealthCareEquity_UrbanIndia.pdf`** (172 pages)
"Health Care Equity in Urban India" — a study of healthcare access and equity gaps among the urban poor, aligning with the Foundation's Health programme focus on informal settlements.

**`APF_Report_Arent_We_Frontline_Warriors.pdf`** (71 pages)
"Aren't We Frontline Warriors? Experiences of Grassroots Health Workers During COVID-19" (2021, Azim Premji University) — documents the working conditions, challenges, and recognition (or lack thereof) faced by grassroots/community health workers (e.g., ASHA-type workers) during the pandemic.

## Education

**`APF_Report_LearningLoss_During_Pandemic.pdf`**
"Loss of Learning During the Pandemic" (2021) — the Foundation's own widely-cited flagship study quantifying learning setbacks among children due to COVID-19 school closures; became a key reference point alongside ASER/UNICEF data on India's pandemic learning loss.

**`APU_Report_FieldStudies_OpenSchools_LearningLoss.pdf`** (12 pages)
"Open Schools, Focus on Recovering Lost Learning: Clear Voice of Teachers" (Field Studies in Education series, August 2021) — a short field-research brief from the Foundation's Research Group, based on direct engagement with teachers, on reopening schools and recovering lost learning post-pandemic.

**`APU_Report_IssuesInEducation_Vol2.pdf`** (83 pages)
"Issues in Education, Volume 11: The Pandemic and School Education" — part of APU's ongoing "Issues in Education" essay/analysis series, this volume focused specifically on how the pandemic affected school education in India. *(Note: internally titled "Volume 11" despite being referred to elsewhere as "Vol 2" of a a related sub-series.)*

## Governance / Urban / Society (General Research — Less Directly Tied to the Foundation's Funding Themes)

**`APU_Report_PostCovid_Governance_Challenges.pdf`** (218 pages)
"Understanding Post-Covid-19 Governance Challenges in India" (March 2022) — produced by APU's School of Policy and Governance in association with the Constitutional Conduct Group; a wide-ranging review of governance failures/challenges exposed by the pandemic across sectors (public health, education, urban planning, vital statistics, digital governance) with policy recommendations.

**`APU_Report_Bengaluru_COVID_Impact_Survey.pdf`** (79 pages)
"The Impact of Covid-19 on Bengaluru's Urban Poor" (March 2023) — a survey-based study by APU's Centre for Sustainable Employment on how the pandemic affected income, employment, and welfare among Bengaluru's low-income/informal-settlement population.

**`APU_Report_Gender_Welfare_Mobility_Shakti_BMTC.pdf`**
"Gender, Welfare, and Mobility: Impact of the Shakti Scheme on BMTC Transport Transformation" — assesses Karnataka's free public bus travel scheme for women (launched June 2023), based on ridership data from Jan 2023–Mar 2025; finds a major surge in bus usage and a shift toward women outnumbering men on many routes, framed within debates on universal vs. targeted welfare.

**`APU_Report_Karnataka_Crime_Victimisation_Survey_2019.pdf`** (13-page excerpt)
"Karnataka Crime Victimisation Survey 2019" — described as the first academically rigorous, survey-based (rather than police-record-based) study of crime victimisation in Karnataka, aiming to supplement official NCRB crime statistics with real victim-experience data.

**`APU_Report_Politics_Society_Between_Elections_2019.pdf`** (254 pages)
"Politics and Society Between Elections 2019" — produced by APU's Centre for Regional Political Economy; a study of political attitudes, behaviour, and society in India outside of election campaign periods.

**`APU_Report_Campus_Biodiversity_Register.pdf`**
"Campus Biodiversity Register 2024–25" — a first-of-its-kind student/faculty-led documentation of the flora and fauna (trees, birds, insects, reptiles, small mammals) on APU's Bengaluru (Sarjapura) campus, compiled by BSc Environmental Science and Sustainability students.

## Foundation Narrative / Storytelling

**`APF_Report_Stories_of_Change.pdf`** (350 pages)
"Stories of Change: Case Study Challenge" — a large compilation of case studies/narratives documenting impact and change across the Foundation's areas of work, likely gathered via a case-study competition/challenge format.

**`APF_Report_Lockdown_Distress_StrandedWorkers.pdf`** (32 pages)
"21 Days and Counting: COVID-19 Lockdown, Migrant Workers, and the Inadequacy of Welfare Measures in India" (published 15 April 2020) — by the Stranded Workers Action Network, a collective that included APU faculty/researchers (Rajendran Narayanan, Seema Mundoli) among its contributors; documents the distress faced by migrant workers stranded by India's 2020 COVID lockdown and gaps in welfare response.
