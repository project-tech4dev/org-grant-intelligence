# Azim Premji Foundation — Livelihoods Program

Source pages: azimpremjifoundation.org/what-we-do/livelihoods, /who-we-are/where-we-work/jharkhand, /chhattisgarh, /who-we-are/how-we-are-organised/field-institutes, Foundation "Stories of Change" (Fruits of Labour); *Azim Premji Foundation Report – June 2026*

## Overview
Livelihoods is one of the Foundation's three core direct work areas (alongside Education and Health), guided by the idea: **"Employment, income, dignity — we work to connect the dots of this area of human development and well-being."** The goal is to bring about **significant and sustained improvement in household incomes** of India's most vulnerable and marginalised communities — small/marginal farmers, landless households, and Adivasi (tribal) communities — without increasing the risks they face, by fostering local economic development aligned with local resources, culture and strengths, while also connecting communities to external markets.

## History and Origins
- The Foundation built its Field Institute network from ~2012, initially focused only on school education.
- Livelihoods (like Health) emerged as a newer domain of *direct* on-the-ground work, leveraging the institutional presence already built for education.
- **2022** — first direct steps in Livelihoods, in the central Adivasi belt: **Raigarh district (Chhattisgarh)** and **Gumla district (Jharkhand)**; later expanded to **Simdega district (Jharkhand)**.
- Separately, the Foundation supported livelihoods work through **grant-making to partner NGOs for over a decade**, well before beginning direct implementation.

## Strategic Framework: Three Levels of Intervention
Interventions are organised across three nested levels, tied to three guiding principles — **Safety, Dignity, and Prosperity**:
1. **Household level (Safety)** — the most fundamental unit. Focus: improving access to government entitlements (e.g., MGNREGA, National Food Security Act) and boosting income from existing/traditional livelihood activities.
2. **Cluster level (Dignity)** — a group of 4–5 Gram Panchayats (~20 villages, ~2,000 households). Focus: better market linkages and prices, access to credit through collectivisation, and value addition to products.
3. **Region level (Prosperity)** — a group of clusters, a district, state, or larger region. Focus: large-scale processing/production industries.

## Key Activities
- Encouraging **value addition** to traditional livelihoods.
- Spreading awareness of **entitlements** under government schemes (MGNREGA, National Food Security Act, state schemes such as Jharkhand's *Birsa Harit Gram Yojana*).
- Strengthening **collectives** (farmer/producer groups) for better market access and credit.
- Promoting **small livestock development**.
- Supporting **natural resource management (NRM)**, e.g., water-harvesting structures.
- Horticulture/orchard promotion — documented success story **"Fruits of Labour"**: in Saktar village, Gumla, an elderly couple (Mahangu Kumhar, ~80, and wife Bhutni Devi) transitioned from pottery to mango-orchard farming under MGNREGA and the Jharkhand government's Birsa Harit Gram Yojana, with guidance from the Foundation's Livelihoods team — one of 400+ households across three Gumla blocks who took up mango orchards through this scheme.

## Institutional Delivery Model
- Delivered through the Foundation's **Field Institutes / District Institutes (DIs)** — permanent, embedded local institutions (not "projects") — and through **partner NGOs**.
- **59 District Institutes** across six states (Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Uttarakhand), staffed by ~2,000 Foundation members, covering roughly 10% of India's population.
- Example: the **Raigarh DI** (70 members) runs livelihoods work for small farmers and landless households in one block, alongside its education, health, and creche work.
- Livelihoods staff include specialists in **entitlements, horticulture, livestock, and natural resource management (NRM)**; hiring calls for "Resource Persons" typically require a postgraduate/graduate degree in a relevant field, 3 years' social-sector experience, and Hindi fluency.
- Organisationally, Livelihoods is described as a vertical/group **"in formation"** (less mature than the Education and Health verticals).

## Current On-the-Ground Reach (state examples)
- **Jharkhand**: Livelihoods work started 2023 — 10 Gram Panchayats covered, 4,900 households covered, 12,000 people covered.
- **Chhattisgarh**: Livelihoods work started 2022 in selected clusters of Dharamjaigarh block, Raigarh district — 42 villages covered, 11,400 households covered, 38,000+ people covered.

## Scale-Up: North-East Karnataka Initiative
A more ambitious, market-linked initiative in **three districts of North-East Karnataka**, aiming to:
- Significantly raise incomes of **over 1 lakh (100,000) households across ~500 villages**.
- Build a strong **production and business hub** over a **7–10 year period**.
- Emphasis on finding market niches, collectivised community production (household-level work alone is insufficient at market scale), and building quality production, logistics, and marketing systems.
- Similar opportunities are being assessed in **Barmer district, Rajasthan**, with further expansion explored in **Raigarh, Gumla, and Simdega**.

## Grant-Making / Partner Support (Philanthropy arm)
- "Livelihoods" is also one of the Foundation's philanthropic **grant-making themes**, defined as: *"Income increases for small and marginal farmers, rural landless, and vulnerable urban communities."*
- The Foundation supports **over 550 NGOs across 23 states** with grants for various aspects of livelihood improvement (grant sizes roughly ₹10 lakh–₹5 crore, average duration ~3 years).
- Related theme: **"Land, Water, Forest"** — 50+ supported organisations working with vulnerable small/marginal farmers, vendors, artisans, weavers on Adivasi forest rights, pastoral rights, and Rural Livelihoods (improved agricultural practices, reduced cultivation costs, market access, farmer collectives, cash crop/livestock diversification). See `azim_premji_foundation_other_areas.md` for the "Land and Forest" theme in detail.
- Notable state-level collaboration in **Andhra Pradesh**: improving incomes and reducing reliance on synthetic chemical inputs among small/marginal farmers via **natural farming promotion**.
- In **Odisha**, plans exist to support **2 lakh vulnerable households** with livelihoods improvement, as part of intensive block-level work (alongside nutrition, health, and water).

## University Connection
Azim Premji University (Bengaluru, Bhopal, and soon Ranchi from 2026) offers postgraduate/diploma/short programmes and conducts research on **Livelihoods and Employment Creation** — including the widely-cited **State of Working India** report series from its Centre for Sustainable Employment — feeding an integrated pipeline between field practice, research, and higher education.
