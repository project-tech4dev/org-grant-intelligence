# Azim Premji Foundation — Health Program

Source pages: azimpremjifoundation.org/what-we-do/health, /what-we-do/health/azim-premji-health-equity-fellowshipfor-postgraduate-doctors, /who-we-are/where-we-work/karnataka, /jharkhand, /chhattisgarh; azimpremjiuniversity.edu.in/azim-premji-foundation

## Origin & Philosophy
- Health became a formal Foundation domain around **2022**, growing out of the Foundation's large-scale **COVID-19 relief and vaccination work**, and leveraging 20+ years of institutional expertise built in School Education.
- Began at slum clusters in **Bengaluru**, then expanded (2022–2023) to districts along the **Chhattisgarh–Jharkhand border** in the central Adivasi (tribal) belt.
- Guiding philosophy: treat health as a **public good**; strengthen the *public* health system rather than build parallel private infrastructure — intervening from first point of community contact, through primary care, up to secondary/tertiary hospitals, complementing/supplementing government systems where needed.

## Current Scale
- Active in **12 districts across 6 states**; work directly supports roughly **1.1 lakh (110,000) people**, with larger reach via partner collaborations.
- Community health/primary care in Bengaluru alone reaches **~1 lakh people** living in informal settlements.

## Key Program Components

### 1. Urban Community Health — Bengaluru
- Operating clusters: Bellandur, Hebbal, Kumbalgodu, Peenya, Thubarahalli (five informal-settlement clusters).
- Runs **4 Health Centres** serving ~91,000–100,000 people / ~25,000 households.
- Services: household-level screening & counselling; community-level health centres; a network of primary centres with outpatient services, laboratories and pharmacies; referral support/help-desks at select government hospitals.

### 2. Rural Tribal Health — Chhattisgarh & Jharkhand
- **Chhattisgarh**: started October 2022 in Dharamjaigarh block, Raigarh district; now covers 4 blocks (Dharamjaigarh, Lailunga, Patthalgaon in Jashpur, Sitapur in Surguja) — 14 Primary Health Centres (PHCs) and 109 Sub-Health Centres.
- **Jharkhand**: work began January 2024 in Ranchi (Bero, Itki blocks), Gumla, Simdega, Khunti — 5 PHCs, 40 Sub-Health Centres, serving ~3 lakh people.
- **Karnataka**: rural health work also runs in Jevaragi block, Kalaburagi district.
- Focus areas: maternal & child health, adolescent health, communicable/non-communicable disease control, family planning, last-mile delivery of essential services, in partnership with state governments.
- The Foundation has identified **100 target districts nationally** for future expansion (about half tribal-majority, with poor health indicators).

### 3. Creches (Child Nutrition)
- For children aged 7 months–3 years, to improve nutritional status of vulnerable children — daily care, hot cooked meals, early stimulation, growth monitoring ("Shishu Ghar").
- Examples: 6 creches for ~175 children in Bengaluru; 367 creches enrolling 7,500+ children in Jharkhand; 390 creches enrolling 5,500 children in Chhattisgarh; overall ~1,784 creches serving 30,000+ children across the Foundation (per FY2025-26 report).

### 4. Hospital Network (major new initiative)
- Building a network of **30 "public-spirited" hospitals** across India — ranging from 100–200-bed rural facilities to 1,000+ bed superspeciality centres — free for poor households, subsidised for others.
- **First hospital:** a planned 230-bed superspeciality hospital (eventually up to 1,300 beds) plus a medical college/school of health sciences, being built on a 150-acre Azim Premji University site in **Itki block, Ranchi, Jharkhand**. Construction underway; foundation stone laid by Jharkhand CM Hemant Soren (Jan 2024); targeted to begin operations by **January 2027**.
- The Ranchi university/hospital project alone is reported as a **~₹5,000 crore** undertaking; if successful, ~15 more such facilities are planned nationally, with reported planned annual investment of up to **₹7,500 crore** on health nationally.

### 5. Education & Workforce Development
- **Master of Public Health (MPH)** — 2-year postgraduate degree launched 2023 at Azim Premji University's Bhopal campus, for mid-career practitioners (5+ years' experience) from medical, development, or allied backgrounds.
- Planned as part of the future Ranchi medical college/school of health sciences (UG, PG, and doctoral programmes in medicine, nursing, allied health, and public health).
- **Azim Premji Health Equity Fellowship** — an ~18-month fellowship for postgraduate (MBBS/MD/MS) doctors in specialities such as Family Medicine, Community Medicine, General Medicine, Obstetrics & Gynaecology, Paediatrics, General Surgery, and Anaesthesia, to practise at 11 participating institutions in remote, resource-scarce, high-need geographies.
  - Provides an honorarium of ₹50,000/month plus a biannual payout of ₹1.8 lakh, plus relocation/travel support (explicitly an honorarium, not a salary).
  - Aims to address the poor availability of health professionals willing to work with the most vulnerable communities in rural/remote areas.
  - Fellows work as part of medical teams on location (1–2 Fellows per location depending on specialty availability).

### 6. Research & Storytelling
- A dedicated **Health Research** effort to identify priority public-health challenges and build data-driven understanding, guiding programme direction.
- **"The Health Worker"** — a storytelling platform sharing frontline stories, insights and perspectives from health workers across India (part of the Foundation's Publications).

### 7. Grants & Partnerships
- Beyond direct operations, the Foundation funds external partner organisations (hospitals/NGOs) to expand healthcare access and community health work over wider geography.
- Notable systemic collaborations: nutrition/hygiene programme with the **Odisha** government; in **Tamil Nadu**, centres set up in district hospitals to rescue, treat, and rehabilitate mentally ill homeless individuals; partnership with **CMC Vellore**; partnership with the **Karnataka government** on health.
