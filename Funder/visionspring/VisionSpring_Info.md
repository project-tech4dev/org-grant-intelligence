## 4. What We Do — Strategic Framework (3 Pillars)

VisionSpring's strategy is built on **3 interlocking pillars**:

### 4.1 DELIVER — Sight Tests and Eyeglasses
Direct delivery channels:
- **See to Earn** — a livelihoods intervention for working-age adults in factories, farms, and artisan communities, to extend productive years and income. Sub-channels: Community Outreach, Livelihoods in Focus (embedded in coffee/tea/cocoa/flower value chains), Clear Vision Workplaces (garment factories).
- **See to Learn** — vision correction for students and teachers.
- **See to be Safe** — vision correction for drivers and transport workers (launched 2015).
- **Community Agents** — last-mile sales agents (largest channel by volume).
- **Pharmacy Program** — retail pharmacy partnerships.
- **Mission-Aligned Wholesale / Institutional Partnerships** — supplying hospitals, vision centers, and NGOs.

### 4.2 CATALYSE — Collaborative Action
- **CatalEYES™** — VisionSpring's flexible technical-assistance/training model (publicly launched at the Skoll World Forum in 2025) that helps other social-impact organizations and governments integrate vision screening and eyeglasses into their existing programs/services — even organizations outside the eye-health sector. Delivered via training modules, program design support, fit-for-purpose glasses supply, and demand-generation strategy. Contact: **partnersuccess@visionspring.org**

### 4.3 INFLUENCE — Systems Change
- **Research & Evidence** — rigorous studies (RCTs) that quantify the economic/social impact of eyeglasses, used to build the "case for investment" with funders and governments.
- **Shaping Systems** — VisionSpring is a founding member and serves on steering committees for global eye-health coalitions; engages directly in policy advocacy (e.g., tax reform).

## 5. Research & Evidence (Randomized Controlled Trials and Studies)

VisionSpring has built one of the sector's strongest evidence bases:

| Study | Finding | Publication |
|---|---|---|
| **PROSPER** (Productivity of Presbyopia Elimination) | Reading glasses for rural Indian tea workers boosted **productivity by 22%** — the largest productivity gain ever recorded in a health trial | Lancet Global Health, 2018 |
| **PROSPER II** | Near-vision correction for garment sewing-machine operators in India boosted productivity by 6%; **16x return on investment** ($3.37 gained per $1 spent in first 12 weeks) | Publication expected April 2026 |
| **THRIVE** (Tradespeople and Hand-workers Rural Initiative for a Vision-enhanced Economy) | Reading glasses distributed by community health workers in rural Bangladesh increased **median monthly income by 33.4%** — first RCT to quantify eyeglasses' impact on income | PLOS ONE, 2024 |
| **THRIFT** (upcoming) | Examines impact of reading glasses on mobile banking adoption in Bangladesh | Manuscript expected Q4 2026 |
| **DRIVES** (upcoming) | Measures impact of glasses on safe driving | — |
| **"Seeing Beyond the First Pair"** | Surveyed 1,000+ customers in India who got reading glasses 5–6 years earlier: **93% still active users**, 61% purchased replacement pairs at market prices, 32% still using original pair — proves lasting market demand from a single intervention | 2025 (supported by Livelihood Impact Fund) |
| **Pharmacy Effectiveness Study (Bangladesh)** | Marketing-supported pharmacies saw 31% repeat purchases vs. non-supported pharmacies | Nov 2025 |
| **Truckers' Driving Safety Study (India)** | ~10,000 commercial drivers screened across 8 states; 17.7% had blurry distance vision; over half reported major driving-safety improvement after receiving glasses | Ophthalmic Epidemiology, June 2025 |
| Referenced external study | Vision correction reduced students' probability of failing by **44%** (Rural Northwest China) | ScienceDirect, 2012 |

VisionSpring's research also underpins the global report **"The Value of Vision: The Case for Investment,"** launched at the UN, calling on governments to invest in eye health.

## 7. 2026–2030 Strategy — "Three Big Bets"

Adopted by the Board of Directors in 2025. Strategic goal: **20 million more people** reached with eyeglasses in 5 years (2026–2030), building toward closing the gap by 2050.

### Big Bet 1: Scale Up Vision Access Services
- Concentrate direct delivery (See to Earn, See to be Safe, See to Learn) in geographies to find the "tipping point" where market coverage shifts from expensive "push" delivery to cheaper customer-led "pull" (most communities currently under 20% coverage; testing what happens at 40%).
- Acts as VisionSpring's "impact and learning lab."

### Big Bet 2: Expand with Partners & Retail
- Extend reach via pharmacies, community health networks, primary care, and livelihoods programs using **CatalEYES™** technical assistance (training, program design, glasses supply, demand generation).

### Big Bet 3: Accompany Government as Payer@Scale
- Help governments integrate eyeglasses into public health/social welfare systems (aligned with the **WHO SPECS 2030 strategy**) via bulk glasses supply, technical assistance, and policy advocacy.

**Strategy enablers:** People & culture; Organizational effectiveness (systems/tech); Financial resources (deepening philanthropy, corporate fundraising, large-scale co-funding incl. government-as-payer).

**Mission measures / KPIs:** Value of new sustained earning potential; first-time wearers; gender parity; (future) eyeglasses coverage rate; (future) market value created.

## 8. 2025 Highlights by Big Bet (Detailed, from Annual Report)

### Big Bet 1 — Scale Up
- **See to Earn** exceeded targets by 15%; Clear Vision Workplaces reached ~70k garment workers across Bangladesh, Vietnam, India, Kenya.
  - New MoU with **Bangladesh Garment Manufacturers and Exporters Association (BGMEA)** — access to 3,000 member factories.
  - **BRAC Bank** invested in vision care as a workplace benefit, screening 46k workers.
  - New Cambodia collaboration launched with **Deckers Brands**.
- **See to be Safe** crossed **1 million** cumulative drivers screened since 2015; in 2025 corrected vision for 94k drivers (Nigeria, India, Zambia, Bangladesh) — 3x original target.
  - Anonymous donor's **$750k grant** expanded screening ambitions 2x+ mid-year.
  - Nigeria: 65k drivers reached via Federal Road Safety Corps & National Transport and Road Workers Union partnerships.
  - First replication in Bangladesh via the Bloomberg Philanthropies Vision Initiative (Bangladesh Road Transport Owners Association, Dhaka Transport Coordination Authority).
  - VisionSpring is part of **UN Friends of Vision**, advocating for vision correction as a core road-safety intervention ahead of the UN's 2026 High-Level Meeting on Road Safety.
- **DigitEYES™** (proprietary digital data-capture platform, replacing pen-and-paper) rolled out to all remaining markets in 2025 (started in India, 2022); cut data processing time from 30–180 days to under 3 days.

### Big Bet 2 — Expand with Partners & Retail
- **RGIL (Reading Glasses for Improved Livelihoods):** 633k pairs delivered — Bangladesh hit 174% of target (487k pairs via BRAC Health Program, BRAC Microfinance, SAJIDA Foundation); Africa hit 135% of target (142k pairs via Council of Churches in Zambia, Malawi Council of Churches, Church of Uganda) with 64% first-time-wearer rate.
- Piloted demand-generation improvements with BRAC in 4 Bangladesh districts → **30% increase in average sales per outreach camp**; rollout planned for 2026.
- **Pharmacy channel:** 66k pairs sold across Nigeria, Ghana, Bangladesh; Ghana's **"Pefee" campaign** (Twi for "So Clear") delivered a **5x increase in sales** across 200+ Kumasi-area pharmacies via radio/billboard ads, point-of-sale branding, and pharmacist training.
- **CatalEYES™** publicly launched at the Skoll World Forum in 2025; trained **4,193 individuals** (pharmacists, community health workers, nurses) during the year.
- **Nigeria's Presidential Presbyopia Initiative ("Jigi Bola"):** VisionSpring responsible for Kwara and Ogun states (of 12 total); trained staff at 89 Primary Health Centres, dispensing 62k reading glasses in first 6 months.

### Big Bet 3 — Accompany Government as Payer@Scale
- Institutional Partnerships channel crossed **1 million pairs delivered in a single year** for the first time (795k via private/mission-aligned institutions; 220k via government tenders).
- **World Sight Day trek:** Founder Jordan Kassalow led a team through the Himalayas (Himachal Pradesh, 15,000 feet), screening 5k people, marking VisionSpring's 20th anniversary in India; led the state government to launch a program to screen 400k schoolchildren.
- **Skoll Foundation** awarded a **$1M no-interest loan** (VisionSpring's first program-related investment) to help manage working-capital needs of slow government payment cycles.
- **India government partnerships:** Largest government contract in VisionSpring's history secured in **Tamil Nadu** (335k+ pairs over 2 years); new tenders in Gujarat and Himachal Pradesh; 5-year MoU extension in Meghalaya; **Rajasthan's first Eye Voucher Initiative** — a 3-year, **$9M program**, with VisionSpring named lead implementing partner (Jan 2026), with runway to deliver up to 1.5M pairs.

## 9. Systems Change / Policy Wins (2025)

- **India tax reduction:** India's General Sales Tax Council reduced the tax on corrective glasses from **12% to 5%** (September 2025) after years of coalition advocacy by VisionSpring Foundation and other NGOs — affecting the estimated 550 million Indians with uncorrected vision. (Context: many countries charge 15–25% tax on eyeglasses.)
- **UNICEF Supply Catalogue:** VisionSpring glasses added to UNICEF's Supply Catalogue for governments and NGOs worldwide.
- **Bloomberg Philanthropies Vision Initiative:** VisionSpring named an anchor partner in this **$75 million** initiative — one of the largest philanthropic commitments ever made to eye health — serving as an implementer/anchor for sight tests and eyeglasses dispensing in Bangladesh, Nigeria, and India.
- **"The Value of Vision: The Case for Investment"** report launched at the UN General Assembly, drawing on VisionSpring's PROSPER, THRIVE, and PROSPER II RCTs; also announced the first-ever **Global Summit on Eye Health**, to be held in 2026.
- Participation in the **Vision and Road Safety (VARS)** coalition — with India Vision Institute, VisionSpring Foundation India, and Sightsavers India — celebrating 2 million drivers' vision screened across India (announced at IAPB IN SIGHT LIVE, Nepal).
