# Kailash Satyarthi Children's Foundation (KSCF) — Programmes, Campaigns & Global Initiatives

Source pages: satyarthi.org.in/programmes, /kscf-ngo, /justice-for-every-child, /ending-child-trafficking, /child-trafficking, /institute, /the-freedom-fellows-education-fund, /other-initiatives, /laws-and-policies, /position-papers, plus KSCF Annual Report 2024–25 (downloaded).

## Overview
KSCF's programmes are community-level interventions (both rural and urban) aimed at **child-centric community development** — ensuring no child is in labour or trafficked, every child is enrolled in and attends school, children are well-nourished/healthy, and children are empowered to participate in local decision-making. Work spans **flagship preventive programmes** (Bal Mitra Gram, Bal Mitra Mandal), **curative/justice programmes** (Justice for Every Child), **educational support** (Freedom Fellows Education Fund), **campaigns** (Tackling Child Trafficking), and **research/policy/training** (via the Satyarthi Global Policy Institute for Children).

## 1. Bal Mitra Gram™ (BMG) — "The Child Friendly Village"
- **Flagship rural programme** to prevent all forms of child exploitation and ensure education/empowerment in rural communities.
- **Guiding principles:**
  1. Withdraw all child labourers from work
  2. Enrol and retain all children in school
  3. Enable children's democratic participation via **Bal Panchayats** (elected children's councils)
  4. Ensure Bal Panchayats are formally recognised by the village council (Gram Panchayat)
  5. Empower communities (including women and youth) for overall village development/transformation
- **Geographic footprint:** functional across **6 states** — Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Karnataka, Bihar, and Jharkhand (per programmes page; the 2024-25 Annual Report references activity across **7 states**).
- **Cumulative reach metrics tracked (2001–2018 baseline, updated in later reports):** number of states/BMGs functional, number of BMGs handed over to communities (i.e., achieved self-sustaining status), number of Bal Panchayats, number of Child Leaders, number of girl members in Bal Panchayats, number of children protected daily, number of children withdrawn from labour.
- **FY2024-25 highlights** (from KSCF Annual Report 2024–25):
  - **Child Protection:** 1,300+ people trained in child rights laws; ~1,700 trained to identify/intervene in abuse; film screenings on trafficking reached 8,400+ people; cyber-safety sessions for 758 children; democratic-rights workshops for 1,201 children; Akshaya Tritiya campaign against child marriage. Concrete outcomes: 10 child marriages stopped, 13 children rescued from labour, 810 businesses pledged never to employ minors, illegal liquor shops closed, unsafe public spaces secured; 10,000+ people participated in rallies/workshops/campaigns.
  - **Access to Education:** 1,128 children newly enrolled in school; 45 irregular students returned; 2 children rescued from labour and reintegrated into school; retention campaigns reached ~3,000 children; remedial classes (1,237 classes) benefited ~14,700 students; leadership/dialogue sessions for 1,800+ children; 35,000+ people engaged via rallies/festivals/School Chalo Abhiyan.
  - **Community Leadership & Engagement:** 1,500+ members received leadership training; 742 youth received career guidance/life skills; National Bal Maha Panchayat included 1,400+ child leaders (11 elected across 7 states); Bal Mitra Ghar initiative — 2,900 families pledged to prioritise child safety/education at home; 18,500 children participated in democratic (Bal Panchayat) elections across states.
  - **Social Protection:** 60+ wall writings/100 murals on welfare entitlements; health camps reached 2,701 people; documentation drives (Aadhaar/PAN/voter ID/birth certificates) for 1,200+ people, generating ₹2.6 lakh+ in scheme-linked support; 2,500 individuals accessing welfare schemes monthly (Bal Sangopan Yojana, Kishori Samriddhi, Palanhar, Maiya Samman Yojana [500 women benefited], Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana, etc.).
  - **Institution Building:** 350 School Development & Monitoring Committee (SDMC) members trained across 20 villages on RTE/NEP; partnerships with the Labour Department and Legal Services Authority (incl. NALSA helpline) for welfare/legal aid access.

## 2. Bal Mitra Mandal (BMM) — Child-Friendly Urban Slum Communities
- Adapts the award-winning **Bal Mitra Gram™** model to **urban slum communities**, tackling child protection, gender inequality, and community empowerment.
- **Objectives:**
  1. Prevent child labour, child marriage, and abuse through awareness/vigilance
  2. Ensure quality education and skills for all children
  3. Empower **Bal Parishad** (children's council), **Yuva Mandal** (youth group), **Mahila Mandal** (women's group) to lead change
  4. Link families to welfare schemes for protection
  5. Strengthen institutions to sustain child-rights systems
- **Cumulative impact (as of FY2024-25 report):** **59,343 lives impacted**; **13,843 children protected**, across **4 child-friendly communities**.
- **FY2024-25 (Sept 2024–Mar 2025) period specifically:** reached **16,294 community members**; directly impacted **2,477 children**; achieved **zero reported child marriages** and **100% enrolment of out-of-school/dropout children** in the reporting period.
- **Partnerships:** Yuva Mandal, Mahila Mandal, BMM Samiti, Bal Parishad (community structures); government linkages with RWAs (Resident Welfare Associations), MCD, NDMC, PMC (Pune Municipal Corporation) — active notably in **Pune**.

## 3. Justice for Every Child (Campaign)
- Ensures victims of **child sexual abuse (CSA) and rape** get timely justice with full **legal and mental-health support**, enabling survivors to heal and continue their lives with dignity.
- Original target: intervene in **5,000 CSA cases** — this target has been **far exceeded (~3x)**.
- **Cumulative impact reported:**
  - **14,991 cases** of child sexual abuse supported (reported as "nearly 15,000")
  - **678 convictions achieved**
  - **₹14,89,61,203** (~₹14.9 crore) worth of compensation awarded to CSA victims
- **Rationale:** delayed trials cause additional hardship for survivors (social isolation, school dropout, gender-based discrimination) and erode public trust in the justice system while perpetrators go unpunished; KSCF works to fast-track case disposal.
- **Second phase focus:** tackling **Child Marriage** as a form of child sexual abuse ("Child Marriage is Child Rape").

## 4. Freedom Fellows Education Fund
- Provides **educational, financial, and training support** to children rescued from the worst forms of child labour, trafficking, abuse, or other violence, who typically lack the financial means to pursue education.
- Nurtures "future change agents" — notable example: **Mehboob Hussain**, rescued and supported through the fund, now studying law with the goal of becoming a judge ("I want to become a Judge and stand up against all forms of injustice").
- **Impact to date:** has supported **63 vulnerable children** in pursuing their education (figure per site; likely grows over time — check latest annual report for updates).
- Related fund: **Surakshit Bachpan Fund** ("Save Childhood Fund") — aims to assist **200 child survivors** of sexual crimes/abuse over 2 years, providing immediate medical, legal, educational, and rehabilitation assistance.

## 5. Tackling Child Trafficking (Campaign)
- **"Intensive Area Action"** strategic intervention across **100 high-risk/trafficking-prone districts** in India.
- **Strategic actions:**
  1. Action in 100 high-risk districts for child trafficking
  2. **Survivor-Led Intelligence Network** across 2,000 of the most vulnerable villages in 20 high-risk districts
  3. Intensive capacity building of the survivor-led network alongside law enforcement/stakeholders
- **On-the-ground interventions:** awareness campaigns (film screenings, pamphlets, wall writing, nukkad natak/street theatre, community baithaks); "Jan Samvad" public dialogues; mapping vulnerable children/families; capacity building of police/Child Welfare Committees; activating Panchayati Raj for child protection; Village Child Protection Committees (VCPC); Village Migration Registers (VMR); Anti-Human Trafficking Units (AHTUs); Child-Friendly Police Stations (CFPS); Child-Friendly Courts (CFC).
- **Survivor-Led Intelligence Network:** a bicycle/bike-based awareness campaign launched by KSCF across **20 districts in Bihar and Jharkhand (2020–21)**, building on a model used since 1997 in trafficking-prone areas. Result: identified **1,826 traffickers**.
- **Operation Khoj:** a joint campaign with Khunti District Administration and Khunti Police (Jharkhand) to identify missing children via Village-Level Child Protection Committee (VLCPC) meetings across all 757 villages of Khunti district, tracking children who moved out for work, went missing, or were trafficked/POCSO survivors — supported by the "Mukti Caravan Rath" awareness vehicle.
- Context cited: per NCRB, 373 human trafficking cases were registered in Jharkhand in 2018, of which 314 involved minor girls (highest of any Indian state that year).

## 6. Satyarthi Global Policy Institute for Children (SGPIC)
- Envisions bringing global child-rights stakeholders together to "congregate, deliberate, learn and innovate" on child rights/protection issues.
- Conducts **research, training & capacity building**, disseminates best practices, and offers **academic courses** to build expertise in child well-being domains.
- **Guiding values:** justice, equality, peace, and compassion.
- **Four centres of operation:**
  1. Centre for Research and Policy
  2. Centre for Innovation and Incubation
  3. Centre for Dialogue, Learning and Capacity Building
  4. Centre for Academics
- **Location:** currently operates from KSCF's Delhi office; a dedicated campus is planned in **Raisen district, Madhya Pradesh** (pending land acquisition).
- Absorbed two former KSCF divisions: **Research and Policy** and **Training & Capacity Building**.
- **University partnerships (MoUs signed)** to establish "Centre for Child Rights" units offering Certificate/Diploma/UG/PG programmes and research: **Amity University, SRM University, Assam University, BML Munjal University (Haryana)**, among others.
- **Policy contributions cited:** The Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (Amendment) Act, 2019; POCSO Rules, 2020; National Tracking System for Missing and Vulnerable Children; Madhya Pradesh Juvenile Justice Rules, 2020; Madhya Pradesh Child Protection Policy, 2020.
- **Laws & Policies resource library** (site's "Laws and Policies" page) catalogues international conventions (CEDAW, UN Convention Against Transnational Organized Crime, ILO Minimum Age Convention No.138, Optional Protocol on Sale of Children/Child Prostitution/Pornography, UN Trafficking Protocol) and Indian laws/schemes (Commissions for Protection of Child Rights Act 2005; Immoral Traffic Prevention Act 1956; POCSO Amendment Act 2019; Juvenile Justice Act 2015; Persons with Disabilities Act 1995; SC/ST Prevention of Atrocities Act 1989; Pre-Natal Diagnostic Techniques Act; Model Rules/SOPs on child labour, adoption, foster care, One Stop Centres, etc.)
- **Position papers published** include: *Strategic Dialogue on Barriers in Rehabilitation of Rescued Child Labour in India*; *Trafficking of Persons (Prevention, Protection and Rehabilitation) Bill, 2018*; *COVID-19 and the Emerging Threats to Child Protection, Health and Education*.
