## Vision & Mission
- **Vision:** A world where every child is free, safe, healthy, and educated — envisioning a world free of violence against children.
- **Mission:** End all violence against and exploitation of children by focusing on **three Ps: Policy, Practice, and People**. Its mission is to end child exploitation through compassion-driven, community-led solutions.
- Strategic pillars (per site): **Child Rights** (protect via tackling violence, ensuring safety/rehabilitation/education/health), **Global Voice** (recognised voice against child abuse/exploitation via campaigns), **Research & Policies** (address data/evidence gaps in child protection/education/health/nutrition, influence global & national policy), **Capacity Build-up** (build capacity of governments, corporates, grassroots orgs), **Global Platform** (propagate best practices, act as a trustworthy bridge between stakeholders).

## Our Founder — Kailash Satyarthi
(Source: satyarthi.org.in/our-founder, /nobel-peace-laureate-kailash-satyarthi)
- Born and raised in **Vidisha, Madhya Pradesh**. As a child, he was struck by the fact that a cobbler's son next door worked instead of attending school — a formative moment that shaped his life's mission.
- Trained and worked as an **electrical engineer** before giving up a conventional career. Launched a magazine, *"Sangharsh Jaari Rahega,"* discussing taboo social issues.
- Approached by **Wasal Khan**, a bonded labourer in a Punjab brick kiln, pleading to rescue his daughter from being sold into a brothel — this rescue (achieved after many attempts) became the catalyst for his lifelong work.
- **1980** — launched **Bachpan Bachao Andolan (BBA)** ("Save the Childhood Movement") to rescue children/families from slavery and reintegrate them into society via India's legal framework. BBA has rescued **90,000+ children** from trafficking/exploitative labour in India over four decades (per KSCF site; other sources cite 138,000+ globally across the wider movement).
- Played a pivotal advocacy role in the passage of India's **Child Labour Act, 1986**.
- **1991** — established **Mukti Ashram** in Delhi, India's first short-term transit rehabilitation centre for rescued children.
- **1994** — established **GoodWeave** (formerly **Rugmark**) — the first-ever civil society–business coalition and certification/social-labelling mechanism for child-labour-free carpets in South Asia.
- **1998** — established **Bal Ashram**, a long-term rehabilitation centre at Virat Nagar, near Jaipur.
- **1998** — conceived and led the **Global March Against Child Labour**, traversing 103 countries and 80,000 km, demanding an international law on the worst forms of child labour. This led the **ILO to adopt Convention No. 182** (1999) — the fastest-ratified convention in ILO history.
- Architect of the **Global Campaign for Education (GCE)**; led the countrywide movement that made education a constitutional provision, paving the way for India's **Right to Education (RTE) Act, 2009**.
- **2004** — founded the **Kailash Satyarthi Children's Foundation (KSCF)** itself, to advocate for child-friendly policy creation/implementation globally.
- **2014** — awarded the **Nobel Peace Prize** (shared with Malala Yousafzai) "for the struggle against the suppression of children and young people, and for the right to all children to education" — the 8th Indian (some sources say 2nd since Mother Teresa, in terms of humanitarian/peace-focused laureates) to win a Nobel Prize. His own words: **"The Nobel Peace Prize is just a comma, not a full stop."**
- Instrumental in getting child-protection/welfare clauses included in the UN's **Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)** (September 2015).
- Founder of **Parliamentarians without Borders**, uniting legislators globally against violence against children.
- **2016** — launched the first **Laureates and Leaders Summit for Children** (New Delhi, at Rashtrapati Bhawan); a second edition was held in Jordan. Brings together Nobel Laureates and world leaders on children's issues.
- **2016** (December) — launched the **100 Million for 100 Million Campaign**, mobilising 100 million youth to shape a better future for 100 million children denied their rights.
- **2017** — led the **Bharat Yatra against Child Sexual Abuse**, a 12,000 km march from Kashmir to Kanyakumari with CSA survivors, civil society, politicians, bureaucrats, celebrities, and faith leaders.
- Has addressed the UN General Assembly, International Labour Conference, UN Human Rights Commission, UNESCO, and parliamentary hearings in the US, Germany, UK, Italy, Brazil, Panama, Nepal, Spain, and Chile.
- Has demanded a new legally binding UN convention against online child sexual abuse/pornography, with backing from world leaders including Angela Merkel and Pope Francis.
- Favourite reflection on childhood: *"Childhood means simplicity. Look at the world with the child's eye – it is very beautiful."*
