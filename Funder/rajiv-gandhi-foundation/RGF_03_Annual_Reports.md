## Detailed Highlights — FY 2020-21 Annual Report (58 pages)
*(read in full via `read_pdf`; this was the most recent/most detailed report and covers RGF's pandemic-year activities across all programme verticals)*

### Introduction
FY2020-21 (Apr 2020–Mar 2021) was dominated by COVID-19. RGF pivoted to relief work, digital education delivery, webinars on social/economic issues, and continued policy research, alongside RGICS "Policy Watch" publications and celebration of birth anniversaries of notable figures.

### Livelihoods
- **COVID relief (with NGO partners):** 20+ partner NGOs supported across 12 states/UTs (Assam, Bihar, Delhi, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Punjab, Puducherry, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal) — reaching 300,000+ people (migrants in transit, daily-wage earners, homeless people, beggars, low-income families) with food and essential supplies. Example partners: Purbanchal Maitri Development Society (Assam), Sarva Seva Samity Sanstha (Bihar), Khudai Khidmatgar & Centre for Education and Communication & Shakti Foundation (Delhi), Manjari Foundation (MP), Roti Bank Foundation (Mumbai), Aajeevika Bureau Trust & Dhara Sansthan (Rajasthan), Mahatma Gandhi Trust (Tamil Nadu), several Uttar Pradesh NGOs (VISION Society, PAHAL, Red Brigade Lucknow, Vigyan Foundation, Badlav Foundation, Navodaya Institute, Yeh EK Soch Foundation), and Rajarhat Prasari (West Bengal, Cyclone Amphan relief).
- **COVID relief by RGF team/volunteers** directly, in additional locations.
- **Rajiv Aajeevika Samvardhan Abhiyan (RASA)** activities.
- **Disaster recovery:** Reconstruction & livelihood restoration + skill development in Wayanad district, Kerala (post-landslide); flood relief & livelihood restoration in Jammu & Kashmir; water conservation structures built in Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh.

### Education
- **INTERACT:** Reached the target of 300 scholarships by March 2021 (enrolling final cohort of 6 children from Kashmir); 252 active scholars tracked across **14 states** at one point in the year (J&K 94, Manipur 85, Assam 32, Telangana 10, Haryana 6, Chhattisgarh 7, Jharkhand 4, Maharashtra 4, Puducherry 3, UP 2, Nagaland 2, Gujarat 1, Karnataka 1, Rajasthan 1). Continued despite COVID via audio/video calls and digital programming with the Wonderoom team.
  - Higher-education alumni fully/partially sponsored at Suresh Gyan Vihar University (Jaipur) and Renaissance University (Indore) — courses spanning B.Tech (Mechanical / CSE-AI/ML), BBA, B.Sc. Agriculture Hons., B.Sc. Biotechnology, B. Physiotherapy, BA Journalism & Mass Comm., MBA, BA Public Administration, BCA.
  - Nutrition/hygiene programme against Acute Encephalitic Syndrome (AES) in Muzaffarpur, Bihar, restarted with partner NGO Save a Mother Foundation.
  - "INTERACT Plus" peer/alumni Zoom sessions on COVID-19, mental health, and continuity of learning, across Assam, Manipur, and Kashmir.
- **Rajiv Gandhi Cambridge Scholarship:** Update from scholar Devarchan Banerjee — research work in Uganda with the University of Chicago's Booth School of Business on public-procurement reform (4,000-firm randomized intervention); later worked at Dalberg Global Development Advisors advising the Indian government and international agencies on COVID-19's impact on early childhood development (10,000-respondent panel survey across 10 states), and with the Jai Vakeel Foundation on a Monitoring & Evaluation strategy based on Amartya Sen's Capabilities Approach.
- **Rajiv Sadbhavana Media Lab (RSML):** Ran the **IDEAL** (Interactive Digital Education and Assessment of Learning) initiative via the **"Sadbhavana se Samadhaan" (S2S)** platform — ~500 children registered during the year. Hosted the "Sadbhavana in the Times of Corona" competition (ages 8–13, 14–28 April 2020) and a 76-day "Sadbhavana with Nature Festival" (5 June–20 Aug 2020, ~19 sessions, ~40–45 participants/session, simulcast on Zoom/Facebook).
- **Rajiv SKILLAB / STEP Centres**, **Antyodaya Shiksha Abhiyan**, and a **Law Scholars programme for Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya students** were also active during the year (per report table of contents).

### Sadbhavana — Heritage Outreach & Public Engagement
- **MARG (Memories and Archives of Rajiv Gandhi):** Curated the online exhibition *"Rajiv's World: The World of Nature"* (20–31 Aug 2020, marking his 76th birth anniversary). Digitised/catalogued archival photographs, videos and documents (adding searchable meta-tags); began migrating from the ageing "Sanchay" software to a new web-based platform. The physical MARG Gallery had to be partly dismantled after a Land & Development Office notice regarding the Jawahar Bhawan ceiling; restoration was planned for the following year with exhibition designer Vasundhra Sangwan.
- **Rajiv Sadbhavana Abhiyan:** A commemorative run (16–20 Aug 2020) from Jawahar Bhawan via Veer Bhumi, India Gate, 10 Janpath, and the Indira Gandhi Memorial, led by Guinness World Record peace-runner **Sufiya**; culminated in a Sadbhavana Pledge ceremony led by Trustee Suman Dubey. A follow-on "Sadbhavana se Samadhan" run (10–19 Nov 2020) covered Faridabad, Ghaziabad, Sonipat, Bahadurgarh, Noida, Gurgaon and Delhi over 8 days. RGF also sponsored Sufiya's **Golden Quadrilateral Run** (~6,000 km in 135 days), during which she became the first and fastest woman to run India Gate–Gateway of India (in 29 days, 1 hr, 11 min — she completed the full run in 125 days).
- **Skill Training on Digital Technology for Persons with Disabilities (PwDs):** Launched 20 Aug 2020 (Sadbhavana Divas) at Jawahar Bhawan, inaugurated by T.D. Dhariyal (Ex-Commissioner for PwDs, Govt. of NCT Delhi). Survey of 86 past "Access to Opportunity" (vehicle-grant) beneficiary students across 14 states identified 42 for digital-skills training; delivered in partnership with NGO Saral Services to 143 PwD candidates in two batches (3-month courses, 36 lectures covering 20 digital tools each) to build employability-focused digital literacy.

### Rajiv Gandhi Institute for Contemporary Studies (RGICS)
In 2020-21, RGICS activities were reorganised into five themes (Constitutional Values & Democratic Institutions; Growth with Employment; Governance and Development; Environment, Natural Resources & Sustainability; India's/"Place in the" World), engaging discourse partners including MPs, civil servants/regulators, service providers/users, media/academia, and citizen groups.

Notable outputs in FY2020-21 included:
- A **Position Paper on SiC-based Semiconductors** (with FICCI and Chhattisgarh IT Promotion Society/CHiPS) evaluating a potential Silicon-Carbide manufacturing hub in India, followed by an investor-interaction event organised by FICCI.
- Research studies on India's **National Electronics Policy** (2012 and 2019) and Chhattisgarh's electronics-sector potential, including discussions with Ernst & Young/CHiPS advisor Praveen Sambani and IIT Bhilai Director Prof. Rajat Moona.
- **Constitutional Values theme outputs:** case studies on the Jallikattu movement (Tamil Nadu); public health/education systems (Save a Mother Foundation); an RTI Act (2005) challenges study; papers on civil society's role, "Democracy and Resistance," street vendors' livelihood law, communal-conflict causes/solutions, biodiversity degradation & zoonotic disease risk, and the relevance of Bhoodan/Gramdan today; an IDR interview with CEO Vijay Mahajan; three "Policy Watch" issues (Sep 2020, Dec 2020 special issue, Feb 2021).
- **Growth with Employment theme outputs:** COVID-19/MSME policy papers (incl. a proposal to expand the MUDRA scheme), an India-China trade & investment study, a "Chinese Goods – Make in India and Create Jobs" working paper, the Chhattisgarh power-electronics hub paper, and three Policy Watch issues (Mar 2020, Aug 2020 COVID special, Dec 2020, Mar 2021).
- **Governance and Development theme outputs:** grassroots health-systems study (Uttar Pradesh), a school turnaround case study (Dhulagori Adarsha Vidyalaya), an MGNREGA Act implementation-status study, an RTE Act decade-later assessment, a food-security research report, a critique of the National Education Policy 2020's foreign-universities vision, a Tamil Nadu migrant-workers case study, and further Policy Watch issues (Jul 2020, Dec 2020, Jan 2021).
