# What We Do — Programmes & Initiatives (Detailed)

## 1. Livelihoods
Site: https://rgfindia.org/livelihoods

RGF works with small and marginal farmers — especially SC/ST households — to improve water security, land productivity, soil health, and sustainable agriculture; and helps youth build skills and pathways to dignified self-employment.

- **UMEAD** (Utilization of MGNREGA for Enhancing Agriculture Development) — also referred to in field materials as the **"Umeed"** programme: helps farmers use MGNREGA-permitted works (farm ponds, land levelling, bunding, plantation) to build water availability, raise crop productivity (enabling 2–3 irrigation cycles and multiple cropping cycles/year), and secure dependable farm incomes. Beneficiaries report annual income gains of roughly Rs 25,000–30,000 from a single farm pond.
- **Rajiv Ajeevika Samvardhan Abhiyaan (RASA)** — a micro-entrepreneurship initiative developed in 2021–22: youth receive entrepreneurship training and mentoring to convert vocational skills into stable, income-generating self-employment.
- **COVID-19 relief (2020–21, per Annual Report):** RGF supported 20+ partner NGOs across 12 states (Assam, Bihar, Delhi, MP, Maharashtra, Odisha, Punjab, Puducherry, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, UP, West Bengal), reaching 300,000+ people (migrant workers, slum dwellers, daily-wage earners, homeless people, beggars) with food/essential supplies during the lockdown. RGF's own Sadbhavana Associates also directly ran relief work in several states.
- **Disaster relief/reconstruction:** Reconstruction and livelihood restoration after the 2018/2019 Wayanad (Kerala) landslides/floods; flood relief and livelihood restoration in Jammu & Kashmir; water conservation structures built in Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh.
- **Natural Resource Management / Gram Gaurav** (per Wikipedia): Ongoing since 2001 in backward, environmentally degraded villages of Jaipur, Pali and Karauli districts (Rajasthan). Scaled up via **Gram Gaurav**, established 19 December 2011, now covering 74 villages in the Dang region of Karauli and Dholpur districts — augmenting water resources, conserving soil, and enhancing agricultural production.

## 2. Education
Site: https://rgfindia.org/education

RGF promotes inclusive learning through scholarships, creative learning centres, remedial education, and hands-on science & technology outreach.

- **INTERACT** (Initiative to Educate, Rehabilitate and Assist Child Victims of Terrorism): supports children who lost one or both parents to conflict/terrorism-related violence, at high risk of dropping out. Provides scholarships, emotional support, and regular academic follow-up.
  - FY2020-21: 300 scholarship target completed; 252 active school-going scholars across **14 states** (largest cohorts: J&K 94, Manipur 85, Assam 32, Telangana 10, Haryana 6). ~13 alumni fully sponsored through higher education (B.Tech, BBA, B.Sc. Agriculture/Biotechnology, Physiotherapy, MBA, Journalism, BCA, Public Administration) at Suresh Gyan Vihar University (Jaipur) and Renaissance University (Indore) with full/partial tuition waivers.
  - FY2024-25 (per website): 270 children across 13 states supported.
  - "INTERACT Plus" activities: peer support Zoom meetings, mental-health sessions (Covid-era), alumni check-ins, and a nutrition/hygiene programme against Acute Encephalitic Syndrome (AES) in Muzaffarpur, Bihar (with partner NGO Save a Mother Foundation).
- **Rajiv Gandhi Cambridge Scholarship** — with the Cambridge Commonwealth Trust: two scholarships/year for Indian students to pursue MPhil/LLM at the University of Cambridge. Notable alumni: Devarchan Banerjee (research at Booth School of Business/Univ. of Chicago on public procurement in Uganda; later Dalberg Global Development Advisors) and Rishi Rajpopat (Sanskrit grammar research, Harvard University Press book, Chomsky Global Connections Award).
- **Wonderoom**: a creative learning space for curiosity, expression, and joyful learning (reading, arts, theatre, science, digital literacy). Extension activities now run in 30+ locations across NCR Delhi and rural Rajasthan, Bihar, and Sikkim through **AShA Centres**.
- **AShA**: began during COVID-19 to support out-of-school/at-risk children; grew into a structured remedial and enrolment-support programme (daily sessions, family follow-ups, school coordination).
- **Rajiv SKILLAB** (a.k.a. R-SKILLAB, Jawahar Bhawan): focused on science education & vocational training. During COVID-19 evolved into **GeNext Science & Technology and Entrepreneurship (STEP) Centres** — hands-on experiments, mobile science demonstrations, vocational exposure, interactive technology sessions.
- **Antyodaya Shiksha Abhiyan**: an education-access initiative for underprivileged children (a homepage stat cites large cumulative reach of "Antyodaya Shiksha Abhiyan Children").
- **Law Scholars Programme** for Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya students (per FY2020-21 Annual Report).
- **Rajiv Sadbhavana Media Lab (RSML) — education angle:** runs the **IDEAL** (Interactive Digital Education and Assessment of Learning) initiative and the **"Sadbhavana se Samadhaan" (S2S)** online platform; ~500 children registered on the platform during the FY2020-21 reporting period; ran the "Sadbhavana in the Times of Corona" children's competition (age 8–13; April 2020) and a 76-day "Sadbhavana with Nature Festival" (5 June–20 Aug 2020) with ~19 online sessions, ~40–45 participants each.

## 3. Environment
Site: https://rgfindia.org/environment

RGF strengthens community participation in environmental protection through awareness, youth engagement, and action-based learning.

- **ECOLABS** (Environmental and Cultural Opportunities for Livelihood Advancement and Building Sustainability): builds environmental awareness in children/youth via informal gatherings, interactive sessions, and field-based learning.
- **Sadbhavana Tree Plantation Drive** (launched 2019): restores green cover and builds community stewardship, honouring Rajiv Gandhi's memory — nurseries begin 21 May (his punya tithi/death anniversary) and the drive concludes 20 August (his birth anniversary). Since 2019: 3,05,128+ saplings planted across 1,040 locations in 21 states.
- Studies, discussions, webinars, and expert interactions on water management, sustainable agriculture, pollution, and climate change, connecting grassroots experience with research/policy audiences.

## 4. Sadbhavana and HOPE
Site: https://rgfindia.org/sadbhavana-and-hope

Promotes mutuality and goodwill through **Humane Optimism and Public Engagement (HOPE)** — building empathy, awareness of constitutional values (especially fraternity), and advancing justice/equality/liberty via social empowerment and active citizenship.

- **RISE & ACT** (Rajiv Institute for Social Empowerment and Active Citizenship Training) — launched 20 August 2021 (Rajiv Gandhi's 77th birth anniversary): trains women, youth, and local groups on constitutional values, leadership, and community problem-solving through workshops and youth-led initiatives.
- **Rajiv Sadbhavana Media Lab (est. 2019):** documents/disseminates RGF's work via films, interviews, digital content; archive of 4,000+ videos including *Living Gandhians* and *Pioneers in Development* series; trains youth in filming/editing and puppet shows.
- **Cultural events** at Jawahar Bhawan (with the Jawahar Bhawan Trust): plays, musical performances, exhibitions, book releases, film screenings.
- **MARG** (Memories and Archives of Rajiv Gandhi) — curated exhibitions/archival displays on Rajiv Gandhi's life and India's tech/social advances (computers, telecom) that he championed.
- **Sadbhavana Digest**: periodic newsletter, 17 issues published to date (https://rgfindia.org/sadbhavana-digest/).
- **Skill Training Program on Digital Technology for Persons with Disabilities (PwDs):** part of the Sadbhavana/Heritage-Outreach portfolio per the FY2020-21 Annual Report.

## 5. Contemporary Studies — RGICS
Site: https://rgfindia.org/contemporary-studies/ | Related site: https://www.rgics.org

The **Rajiv Gandhi Institute for Contemporary Studies (RGICS)**, founded August 1991 as RGF's think tank, undertakes research connecting field experience with policy analysis across five themes:

1. **Constitutional Values and Democratic Institutions** — how justice, equality, liberty and fraternity shape India's social/political landscape (recent RGICS Policy Watch issues have covered the July 2026 youth protests under this theme).
2. **Governance and Development** — functioning of public systems, institutional accountability, and effectiveness of local governance.
3. **Growth with Employment** — how public policy can drive widespread, employment-generating growth (RGICS notes that while the 1985/1991 reforms placed India on a high-growth path, sustained high growth over decades is still needed to lift a still-poor population).
4. **Environment, Natural Resources and Sustainability** — climate change, water, forest and land issues, analysed from a people's-perspective/experiential lens (a current RGICS focus: the Himalayas warming faster than the rest of India, threatening the region's role as the world's largest freshwater source, the "Third Pole").
5. **India's Place in the World** — how India can engage globally on climate change, terrorism, trade, and human rights in an increasingly interdependent world.

RGICS publishes a recurring **"Policy Watch"** series (e.g., a recent issue examines the second Trump administration's first year and its global impact) and has hosted speakers including Robert McNamara, Nelson Mandela, Hillary Clinton, and Edward Said.
