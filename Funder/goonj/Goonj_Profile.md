# Goonj — Profile, Governance & Background

Official website: https://goonj.org

---

## 1. Mission & Vision
Source: https://goonj.org/knowing-goonj/

> Goonj aims to build an equitable relationship of strength, sustenance, and dignity between the cities and villages, using under-utilized material as a tool to trigger development with dignity.

Goonj envisions growing as an idea across regions, economies, and countries — using urban surplus material to address basic but neglected issues of the financially and materially poor, involving them in designing and implementing their own solutions, with their own effort, knowledge, and dignity — with material as a reward for participation.

**Homepage mission statement:** "We ignite the self-worth and dignity of common people across the villages and cities of India and mobilize them as equal stakeholders — inspiring them to leverage the value and potential of all they have — to solve their challenges."

## 2. Values & Guiding Principles
Source: https://goonj.org/values-guiding-principles/

- Recognise and value the potential of local resources and traditional wisdom of people.
- Focus on the **receiver's dignity instead of the donor's pride**.
- Promote a circular economy by ensuring maximum use of each material.
- Collaborate with partner organisations to increase effectiveness and scale.
- View every entity as an **equal stakeholder** in development, not a hierarchical relationship.
- "In the race of development we are all too focused on machines — i.e., the big, known issues — ignoring the needles: the most important basic needs." Goonj focuses on the "needles."
- Goonj describes itself as creating "a parallel economy which is not CASH based but TRASH based" — old, reusable material becomes a valuable development resource instead of everything needing to wait for money.

**Design & Strategy Principles:** build empathy/dignity/value connections between the resourceful and resource-scarce; leverage already-available resources/wisdom/skills/time; take a human-centred, community-driven approach to poverty and discard; equip both urban and rural end-users to thrive sustainably; listen deeply and with humility to rural communities; facilitate/nudge/empower rather than dictate; stay responsive and keep learning; evolve solutions *with*, not *for*, communities; root ideas in local context/culture/wisdom/needs; let guiding principles drive decisions amid complexity.

## 3. History
Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goonj_(NGO) ; https://goonj.org/knowing-goonj/

Goonj was founded in **1999** by **Anshu Gupta**, starting with just **67 clothes**; it now handles **over 3,500 tonnes of material every year**. It is registered under the Societies Act, with 80G and 12A tax exemptions, and is FCRA-registered for foreign contributions.

**Origin story:** Anshu Gupta met a Delhi rickshaw-puller named Habib, whose rickshaw bore the words *"Laawarish Lash Uthanewala"* ("person who picks up unclaimed dead bodies"). Habib was paid a nominal ₹20 and two metres of cloth per body carried to the crematorium. Habib's young daughter Bano told Gupta: *"When I feel cold, I hug a dead body to sleep. It does not turn around, it doesn't trouble me."* This encounter crystallised for Gupta that clothing — a basic human need — was entirely absent from the development agenda, and led him (with his wife and friends) to found Goonj.

**Key milestones:**
- **2004:** The Indian Ocean earthquake/tsunami prompted the "Not Just a Piece of Cloth" (NJPC) campaign — old but usable clothes flooding the Chennai relief centre were disinfected, washed, recycled into sanitary pads, and packed into "Dignity Kits." By 2014, 4 million pads had been distributed at a nominal ₹2 (~2.1¢ US) each.
- **2009:** Goonj joined the "Joy of Giving Week" (later renamed **Daan Utsav** / Donation Festival), an annual week-long donation drive starting Gandhi Jayanti (2–8 October).
- **2010:** "Ek Jodi Kapda" project launched with Whirlpool Corporation (clothing collection centres).
- **2011:** "Joy of Exchange" campaign with Pantaloons Fashion & Retail (donation drop-boxes at Pantaloons offices pan-India).
- **2012:** Anshu Gupta named India's Social Entrepreneur of the Year by the Schwab Foundation (World Economic Forum affiliate).
- **2013:** "Share the Language of Love" campaign with Johnson's Baby India (children's clothes/toys/books collection).
- **2013 Uttarakhand floods** — "Flood Overcome" relief programme.
- **2014 Kashmir floods** — "Rahat Floods" relief project.
- **2015:** Goonj shipped 70,000+ kg of reusable material/month; recycled 1,000 tonnes of used clothes/household goods/discards that year. Partnered with Oriflame India for material collection (woollens, toys, books) for remote communities. **Anshu Gupta awarded the Ramon Magsaysay Award** ("Asia's Nobel Prize").
- **2016:** "Look Good Do Good" project with Raymond — free custom tailoring on new trousers in exchange for old ones ("Trouser Exchange Program").
- **2018 Kerala floods** — relief-supply collection drive across India.
- **2018 (Dec):** Goonj turned 20 — reported triggering 500+ rural development works/month.
- **2018 (fellowships):** "Urban Fellowship" cohort launched (first fellowship programme).
- **2019:** "Grassroots Fellowship" launched.
- **2020:** COVID-19 relief work; Goonj and its rural partnership network "Sanjha" recognised among the World Economic Forum's Top 50 "Covid Last Mile Responders"; "Sanjha Fellowship" and "Next Generation Fellowship" launched.
- **As of March 2021** (per Wikipedia): Goonj had reached 25 states across India, transporting over 6 million kg of material/year, with "Green By Goonj" converting unusable cloth pieces into new products.
- **2021:** Received the Mackenzie Scott gift to continue ground operations. The animated short film *"Safar"* (by Kireet Khurana, based on words by Anshu Gupta) won Best Animation Film at the LAMPA International Film Festival, Moscow.
- **2022–23:** Goonj's 25th anniversary year (organisation); introduced/scaled **Cloth Based Employment (CBE)** and **GARRD** (Goonj Alliance for Rapid Response to Disasters).
- **GARRD → SARRD:** GARRD was later renamed **SARRD** (Societal Alliance for Resilience and Response to Disasters), broadening it into a cross-sectoral, multi-institution alliance.
- **2024:** Sat Paul Mittal National Award (Platinum, Institutional Category); Leader in Volunteer Engagement award (iVolunteer India).
- **2025:** Don Norman Design Award – Humanity-Centred Design (HCD+) Organization Laureate, for the "Not Just a Piece of Cloth" (NJPC) menstrual wellbeing initiative.

## 4. Founder & Governance

### Anshu Gupta — Founder
Sources: https://goonj.org/profile/anshu-gupta/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anshu_Gupta

Anshu Gupta, popularly called the **"Clothing Man,"** studied mass communication (Indian Institute of Mass Communication) and holds a master's in Economics. He worked as a freelance journalist before quitting a corporate career in **1998/1999** to found Goonj — "the world's first agency working on clothing as a development issue." Under his leadership, Goonj created a barter model between urban surplus material and rural community labour, pioneering a "parallel economy" that is "not cash-based but trash-based."

Anshu Gupta also founded **Gram Swabhimaan**, a related initiative.

### Meenakshi Gupta — Co-Founder
Source: https://goonj.org/governing-body/

Meenakshi Gupta handles communication, research, strategic conversations, and ecosystem-building at Goonj, and has been central to embedding Goonj's values across the organisation over two decades. She is a founding member of **Catalyst 2030** (a global network of social entrepreneurs), has served on juries for awards including the **Nari Shakti (Women Power) awards**, and is a Dance Movement Facilitator. Before Goonj, she had a 15-year corporate career, including 8 years as News Publicity Head with **BBC South Asia** (until 2005).

## 7. Privacy Policy & Donations
Source: https://goonj.org/privacy-policy/

- Goonj does not sell, exchange, or rent personal information to any organisation or individual, and only shares it as needed to process/fulfil donations (or as legally required).
- Personal data (name, address, email, phone) collected during donations/events/volunteering is used to process donations, send receipts/thank-you notes, respond to queries, and share project/event updates.
- Uses cookies for authentication/personalisation and anonymous aggregate analytics (e.g., IP-based usage tracking); aggregate (non-identifying) data may be shared with sponsors/partners.
- Donations use SSL encryption; card details are not stored except via the payment provider for authorised recurring donations.
- **All cash donations are tax-deductible under Section 80G**; Goonj is **FCRA-certified** to receive foreign-currency funds.
- Refunds are considered for unintended donations (contact required with original receipt).

## 8. Wikipedia Background (additional NGO context)
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goonj_(NGO)

Goonj undertakes disaster relief, humanitarian aid, and community development across parts of 23–25 Indian states, focusing on clothing as a basic but unaddressed development need. Notable flood-relief responses cited by Wikipedia: **2013 Uttarakhand floods** ("Flood Overcome"), **2014 Kashmir floods** ("Rahat Floods"), and **2018 Kerala floods** (nationwide relief-supply collection).

**Awards (organisational, per Wikipedia):** Changemakers' Innovation Award (2004, for School to School); World Bank Development Marketplace Award; NASA/Nike/USAID LAUNCH Innovation Challenge recognition (2012, "Beyond Waste" theme).
