## Detailed Highlights — FY 2022-23 Annual Report ("Humanity and Circularity for Climate Action")
*(25th-anniversary year; read in full via `read_pdf`)*

**Founder's note (Anshu Gupta):** Frames FY2022-23 as a milestone 25th year, highlighting two innovations at the forefront of that year's work: **Cloth Based Employment (CBE)** and the **Goonj Alliance for Rapid Response to Disasters (GARRD)**. Argues for moving from charity/aid toward dignity- and agency-centred development, and for rethinking disaster response as part of the ongoing development continuum rather than a series of one-off disruptions.

**Executive Summary / Highlights of the year:**
- Material channelised: **9.6 million kg** (of which 7.8 million kg recycled/upcycled/reused)
- Worked across **7,300 villages**, 250+ districts, 25 states & UTs
- **2.8 million lives touched**
- **20,900+ community development activities**
- **400,000+ person-days of livelihood** created under Cloth Based Employment
- **9,200+ agriculture/water-related community assets** created (ponds, canals, kitchen gardens, plantations) to address food security amid disasters/weather uncertainty
- School to School (S2S): **1,900+ school/educational set-ups** reached; **150,000+ School Kits** distributed
- GARRD-driven disaster relief reached **316,000+ individuals**

**Table of contents (structure):** Note from the Founder; Executive Summary; Who We Are (the idea, guiding principles, design/strategy principles, initiatives); How We Work (linking people, purpose, possibilities); What We Do (Access & Infrastructure, Water Management, Sanitation, Environment & Agriculture, Menstrual Health & Hygiene, Education, Responding to Climate Emergencies, GARRD, Missed Out Communities (MoCs), Green By Goonj, Strengthening Ecosystems, Partnerships — Urban volunteer-led collection camps, Corporates & institutes); Working In-Depth (Cloth Based Employment); Financials; Appendix.

## Detailed Highlights — FY 2021-22 Annual Report ("Reviving the Barter Economy – with Dignity and Circularity")
*(read in full via `read_pdf`)*

**Founder's note:** Reflects on the COVID-19 recovery phase, urging courage to rethink old assumptions and "go back to basics." Frames the report as a tribute not just to Goonj's own team but to the thousands of unpaid community members/volunteers whose daily actions ("not on our rolls, not paid a single penny") sustain the model.

**Key figures for FY2021-22:**
- Urban surplus material reused: **4.9 million kg**
- Total material circulated (at highest value): **14.20 million kg**
- **17,000+ community-led projects** facilitated (reviving water bodies/check dams, large-scale nutrition work through Kitchen Gardens, etc.)
- Goonj states it works directly on **8 of the UN's 17 Sustainable Development Goals**

**"How We Do This" — four operating pillars described in the report:**
1. **Reverse logistics of surplus material** for poverty, inequality, and climate change — treating urban waste cycles (production/consumption/disposal) as a resource loop rather than pure waste management.
2. **Material processing** via "pluralistic, manpower-intensive technologies" — material is sorted into Reuse (as-is), Refurbish (minor repairs), and Recycle (new products) at Processing Centres, deliberately kept low-tech/hand-crafted/energy-light to maximise livelihood creation for women with low formal literacy/education.
3. **Engaging urban and rural communities** to jointly recognise (a) the waste/environmental risk on the urban side and (b) rural communities' own untapped capability and dignity — positioning both as equal stakeholders.
4. **Addressing poverty, inequality, and climate crisis through rural development**, built on three pillars: (a) mobilising communities to identify their own priority challenges; (b) community-led design of localised, resource-appropriate solutions; (c) community-led action to create/repair/revive neglected local assets.

**Table of contents (structure):** Note from the Founder; Knowing Goonj; Community-led Rural Development; Responding to Disasters; Continuing COVID Response; Deep Dive into Kitchen Gardens; Strengthening Partnerships; Strengthening Ecosystem; Financials; Acknowledgement.

## Detailed Highlights — FY 2018-19 Annual Report (20th Anniversary Year)
*(read in full via `read_pdf`)*

**Founder's note:** Marks Goonj's 20th year (founded 1999). States that Goonj was by then triggering **500+ rural development activities every month**, with a footprint across **25 states/UTs**, handling over **5.5 million kg of urban surplus material** that year. Notes infrastructure-building, sanitation, and water as the top three community-prioritised issues that year. First-time initiatives launched in FY2018-19: a major photo exhibition on disasters ("Myths and Realities"), "Chaupal Meets" (open, no-agenda community conversation spaces), and a National Dialogue on Menstruation ("Missing Voices and Missing Issues").

## Detailed Highlights — FY 2016-17 Annual Report
Marks Goonj's **18th year** (Feb 2017). The founder's note reflects on the milestone Ramon Magsaysay Award (2015) as prompting a rethink of goals, while reaffirming the core value of working on basic needs, dignity, and voice for "the last person." Uses an infinity-loop cover motif as a metaphor for circularity and urban–rural interconnectedness. Notes Goonj's growth "from 67 personal clothes" at founding into today's dignity-centred model.
