## Quick Facts
| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Founded | 1999 |
| Founder | Anshu Gupta |
| Co-Founder | Meenakshi Gupta |
| Headquarters | Goonj, Near Sunder Public School, Gujjar Rajesh Pilot Marg, Behind G.D. Goenka School, Madanpur Khadar, Sarita Vihar, New Delhi – 110076 |
| Legal status | Registered society; 80G and 12A tax-exemption; FCRA-registered (foreign contributions) |
| Core idea | Using urban surplus material (especially cloth) as a currency/tool for rural development, disaster relief, and dignity-centred humanitarian aid |
| Presence | 25+ states/UTs across India (23–25 depending on year cited) |
| Scale | Handles 3,500+ tonnes (millions of kg) of material a year; 58 million kg handled cumulatively (Apr 2014–Mar 2025) |
| Key initiatives | Cloth for Work / Dignity for Work, Cloth Based Employment, Not Just a Piece of Cloth (MyPad), School to School, Rahat (disaster relief), Green by Goonj, SARRD (formerly GARRD), Goonj Fellowships |
| Notable recognition | Ramon Magsaysay Award (2015, to Anshu Gupta), Ashoka Fellowship (2004), World Bank Global Development Marketplace Award (2007/2009), Mother Teresa Memorial Award (2020), Don Norman Design Award (2025) — full timeline in `Goonj_Profile.md` |
| Contact | +91-11-41401216 (HQ) / 1800-202-9012 (toll-free, common to most regional offices) |
