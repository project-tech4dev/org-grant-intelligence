# Child Aid Network (CAN) — Official Information

**Source:** All information below is compiled exclusively from CAN's official website: **https://can-ss.org** (Home, About Us, Our Work, Contact & Partner, and Privacy Notice pages — accessed September 2026). No third-party sources were used.

---

## 1. Organisation Overview

- **Name:** Child Aid Network (CAN)
- **Tagline:** "Child rights, community action, and peace."
- **Country of operation:** South Sudan
- **Type:** Women-led South Sudanese national NGO
- **Registration:** Registered with the **Relief and Rehabilitation Commission** under **Registration No. 1,386**
- **Mission statement (site tagline):** "Child-focused support with dignity and care." CAN supports children, young people, and families through protection-aware, community-centred action in South Sudan.

## 4. About CAN (from the "About Us" page)

> "Child Aid Network (CAN) is presented through a simple portfolio-style website designed for partner visibility and responsible communication."

- The website explicitly avoids **public contact forms** and **identifiable child imagery** at launch — described as a deliberate choice to keep "the first version safer and easier to manage."
- **How the site frames itself:** "Professional, compact, and easy to update."
  - **Child-focused NGO** — "A public-facing point that helps partners quickly understand CAN."
  - **Safeguarding-aware communication** — same framing, emphasising child-safe visuals/content.
  - **Partnership-ready profile** — positioned as a fast, credible profile for donors/partners rather than a full institutional portal.

## 5. Focus Areas / Our Work (from the "Our Work" page)

CAN's site states plainly: *"CAN's work is framed around safe public communication. Specific projects, case stories, and images should only be added after consent and safeguarding checks."* — i.e., the current site intentionally withholds detailed project data, photos, and case stories pending future safeguarding/consent review.

The four stated focus areas are:

1. **Child Protection & Welfare** — "A careful focus on children's dignity, safety, and wellbeing."
2. **Education Support** — "Encouraging learning, access, and opportunity for young people."
3. **Family Resilience** — "Working with caregivers and communities around practical support needs."
4. **Partner Visibility** — "A clear online profile for donors, stakeholders, and collaborators."

**"Responsible Updates" note (Our Work page):** *"This first version keeps the site clean and credible. Photos, donor logos, project figures, and detailed case stories should only be added after the organization confirms consent and accuracy."*

## 7. Privacy Notice (full text, from the "Privacy Notice" page)

> "This website provides public information about Child Aid Network and its partnership priorities. At launch, the website uses direct email and phone contact instead of a public contact form.
>
> If you contact CAN, your name, organization, email address, phone number, and message may be used only to respond to your inquiry, coordinate partnership conversations, or provide requested information.
>
> Please do not send sensitive personal, protection, medical, survivor, child case, or security information through general email unless necessary and appropriate. Any future contact form, analytics, or tracking tools should be added only after review and approval."

This reflects a strong internal safeguarding/data-protection posture — explicitly cautioning against sending sensitive case/survivor/child protection information via general email.

## 9. Important Notes on Available Information

Based on a full review of all five pages of the official website, CAN's current site is explicitly presented as a **minimal, first-version "portfolio-style" launch site** — the organisation's own text repeatedly states that:
- No project-level data, photos, donor logos, or detailed case stories are published yet.
- No identifiable images of children are used, and none will be added without safeguarding clearance.
- Content (stories, evidence, figures) is intended to be added **later**, after internal review/consent processes.

As a result, **no additional operational details are currently available from the official source** regarding: founding year, leadership/board names, budget/financials, specific programme beneficiary numbers, donor list, or annual reports. These simply do not yet exist on the live official website as of this review. If/when CAN publishes an expanded site or annual report, that content would need to be pulled separately.
