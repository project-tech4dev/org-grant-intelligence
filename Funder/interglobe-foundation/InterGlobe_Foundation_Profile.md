## 2. Overview & Mission
Source: https://www.interglobe.com/interglobe-foundation

**Tagline:** "Driving a Cycle of Positive Change"

> "We believe that every day is an opportunity to be and do better. To demonstrate this value in spirit and action, we at InterGlobe Foundation continuously strive to take actions that ensure we are socially accountable, ecologically sustainable and economically prudent."

Since its formation, InterGlobe Foundation has focused on creating "meaningful and transformative impact" in three focus areas: **Culture and Heritage Preservation**, **Environment Protection**, and **Livelihood Promotion**. It works by "meeting the needs of the present without compromising on the future," reaching economically challenged communities through tailored programmes, and supporting their journey toward social, economic, and environmental progress — implemented by partnering with NGOs across India.

**Quote (Rohini Bhatia, Chairperson):** "InterGlobe Foundation is committed to investing in our nation and citizens. Our vision is to build pride in communities through providing sustainable livelihoods, by conserving the environment and promoting heritage and culture."

## 3. Focus Area: Culture & Heritage Preservation
Source: https://www.interglobe.com/foundation/culture-heritage

**Framing:** "Every heritage site is the physical manifestation of a nation's history and culture... Through restoration projects like Rahim's tomb in Delhi and Indra Kund stepwell in Rajasthan, we have impacted over 6,550 lives while successfully preserving the historical and cultural values that bind us as a nation."

The Foundation partners with organisations to carry out **structural restoration of heritage landmarks**, paired with revival of craft skills and cultural legacy — protecting heritage while creating local job opportunities. Work spans both tangible heritage (physical restoration) and intangible heritage (establishing sites' relevance/importance, raising public awareness of preservation).

**Named projects:**
- **Restoration of Abdur Rahim Khan-I-Khanan's Tomb ("Rahim's Tomb")**, New Delhi — restored with the Aga Khan Trust for Culture. Quote from **Ratish Nanda** (Aga Khan Trust for Culture): "The tomb's condition was precarious, to say the least, when the project began. There were deep cracks in the crypt, the first floor and the dome. This needed immediate attention and the support from InterGlobe Foundation came in the nick of time to restore the monument."
- **Restoration of the Indra Kund stepwell**, Delwara, Rajasthan.
- **Restoration of the tilework on the dome of Muhammad Qutb Shah's Mausoleum**, Hyderabad.
- **Cultural mapping and documentation of ten cities.**
- **"My City My Heritage" campaign** — city heritage walks organised jointly with **IndiGoReach** (IndiGo's CSR arm), held in numerous Indian cities including Kozhikode, Lucknow, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Ahmedabad, Puducherry, Gwalior, and Patiala (see §7, News).
- **"Tangible Conversations, Intangible Heritage" exhibition** — now in its third edition (per recent press release), alongside a new "Heritage Dialogue" series.

**Impact quote:** "I really appreciate the incredible attention to historical processes and conservation techniques." — Lynn Meskell.

## 4. Focus Area: Livelihood Promotion
Source: https://www.interglobe.com/foundation/livelihood

**Since 2015**, InterGlobe Foundation has supported livelihood opportunities for marginalised communities, with a special focus on **women and youth**. The approach combines strengthening traditional occupations (agriculture, tailoring) with introducing new occupations, via training, skill development, market linkages, and confidence-building. It also ensures "last-mile delivery" — access to credit, land investment, and support for the overall enterprise ecosystem — and that livelihood interventions are ecologically friendly.

**Reach:** Over **4,000 women** and **700 young workers** engaged across programmes.

**Quote (from the Foundation):** "We are committed to building a society that is just, equitable, inclusive and in favour of establishing sustainable livelihoods across communities, especially for women and youth."

**Named projects:**
- Skill-development programme training **200+ underprivileged youth**.
- **"Pragati"** — training 150 women in Assam to manage Areca-nut leaf cutlery production.
- **"GramJyothi"** — a women's livelihood project in Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh.

**Impact quotes:**
- "The additional income has been helpful in continuing the education of my children." — Nasimunisa.
- "I am very thankful to the GramJyothi project for giving me an opportunity to learn and engage in income enhancement activities. I make Rs. 10,000-12,000 per month by preparing khova at home and selling it in the local market. This additional income is helping me look after my family well." — Nirmala Devi, Tadkanpalle.

## 6. InterGlobe HERITAGE Fellowships
Source: https://www.interglobe.com/foundation/heritage-fellowships

Launched to bring together a cross-section of the public to **document and research India's cultural heritage** — both tangible and intangible — as the world loses such heritage faster than it can be recorded. The fellowships empower individuals to explore previously undocumented aspects of heritage and raise awareness of lesser-known cultural treasures.

- **Three editions completed successfully** as of the most recent full write-up.
- **Editions referenced on site:** InterGlobe HERITAGE Fellowships **2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025** (the 2025 edition was announced per a 24 July 2025 press release).

## 7a. Report Reviewed — IndiGo ESG Report FY 2024-25 (not a financial statement)
**Finding:** InterGlobe Foundation does not publish its own standalone Annual Report or CSR report PDF. However, its heritage-conservation work is carried out jointly with **IndiGoReach** (the CSR arm of InterGlobe Aviation Ltd./IndiGo), which *does* publish a detailed annual **ESG/Sustainability Report** — a narrative report, not a financial statement. This was read and analysed in full (73 pages) via this session's tools; the data extracted is captured below and in `InterGlobe_Foundation_Programs.md` (Part D):

- **Official source (verified, direct from goindigo.in):** https://www.goindigo.in/content/dam/indigov2/6e-website/header/information/indigo-green/2025/ESG_Report_FY_2025_high_res.pdf (linked from the "IndiGo Green – ESG report" section of goindigo.in)
- **Scope:** Covers April 1, 2024 – March 31, 2025; prepared per GRI Universal Standards 2021, aligned with SASB airline-sector standards, TCFD, and UN SDGs.
- *(Note: the PDF is not currently cached in the local downloads folder — re-download from the source URL above if the raw file is needed again.)*

**Relevant "Communities" section (pages 40-43) — IndiGoReach CSR strategy, FY2024-25:**
- **CSR focus areas:** (1) Heritage conservation, (2) Skill development, (3) Environmental sustainability
- **Heritage conservation:** 2 landmark restoration projects (Mausoleum of Abdur Rahim Khan-i-Khanan, Delhi; Gwalior Fort, Madhya Pradesh); developing sections of the Shivsrushti Historical theme park, Pune; training of masons/artisans/craftsmen using eco-friendly materials and sustainable construction methods
- **"My City My Heritage" campaign:** Since its launch in November 2022, heritage walks have been organised in Prayagraj, Bhubaneswar, Shillong, Nashik, Hyderabad, and — in FY2024-25 — Kozhikode, Lucknow, and Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar (the "sixth heritage walk" milestone). Explicitly notes: **"In February 2025, IndiGoReach and the InterGlobe Foundation..."** jointly conducted these activities (text continues onto the next page in the source, confirming the direct organisational link between IndiGoReach and InterGlobe Foundation)
- **Skill development:** 100,695 individuals trained for enhanced livelihoods (over 90% women beneficiaries); skill development for 1,000 transgender individuals; over 90% of trained individuals reported a 20–25% income increase; new TTLH (Travel, Transportation, Logistics, Hospitality) sector training in Dehradun, Gurgaon, Shillong, New Jalpaiguri, Hyderabad, and Jodhpur (with Pratham Education Foundation and the Tourism & Hospitality Skill Council) — targeting 3,900+ students trained over 3 years (1,025 trained in FY2024-25 alone)
- **"WATER" programme** (Women-led Action Towards Environment Rejuvenation): empowering 75,000 women across Jharkhand and Chhattisgarh via natural resource management, agriculture/animal husbandry training; 5 Farmer Producer Companies + 205 Producer Groups formed; 5,500+ Self-Help Groups across 674 villages; over 60% of women beneficiaries in Jharkhand now earn over ₹1 lakh/year ("Lakhpati Didis")
- **Environmental sustainability:** 180,000+ saplings planted in FY2024-25 (on top of 115,000 planted previously); 70,698 kg of waste recycled at the "Zero Waste" airport project, Indore (with Airports Authority of India & AAS Foundation) — a self-sustaining model using compost/recycling revenue to fund worker salaries; Himachal Pradesh project: 300 biogas plants, ~3,000 energy-efficient cookstoves, 200 solar lights distributed, 15,000 fruit trees planted (85% survival rate), benefiting 4,000+ families
- **Rural Maharashtra initiative:** targeting 20,000 women via sustainable/organic farming training, land & water resource development, and support for women-led micro-enterprises via microfinance
- **Employee volunteering:** "i-Serve Volunteering Platform" launched (accessible via the internal "6E Breez" platform) — the same "iServe" programme referenced on the InterGlobe Foundation site (§7)

*(Note: financial-statement PDFs — e.g., IndiGo/InterGlobe Aviation Ltd.'s statutory Annual Report with audited accounts — were intentionally **not** downloaded, per your instruction to fetch the report but skip financial statements.)*

**Governance clarification (from the ESG report's Governance section, pp. 44–48):** CSR oversight for IndiGoReach sits with **InterGlobe Aviation Limited's (IndiGo's) own Corporate Social Responsibility Committee** — a formal Board committee of the *publicly listed* airline entity, responsible for recommending annual CSR expenditure/projects, ensuring compliance with the Companies Act, 2013 CSR Rules, and monitoring project effectiveness. IndiGo's Board of Directors (as of the FY2024-25 report) includes Mr. Vikram Singh Mehta (Chairman), Mr. Rahul Bhatia (Managing Director), Mr. Michael G. Whitaker, ACM (Retd.) Birender Singh Dhanoa, Mr. Gregg Albert Saretsky, Mr. Anil Parashar, Ms. Pallavi Shardul Shroff, and Mr. Meleveetil Damodaran — **Rohini Bhatia is not a director of the listed IndiGo entity**; her Chairperson role is specific to InterGlobe Foundation, which sits under the privately-held InterGlobe Enterprises Pvt. Ltd. structure (see §1/§8), a distinct legal vehicle from IndiGo/InterGlobe Aviation Ltd. This explains why InterGlobe Foundation itself files no separate public Annual Report while IndiGo, as a listed company, has extensive statutory disclosure (Annual Report, BRSR, ESG Report).

## 11. Corporate Context — InterGlobe Enterprises (parent company)
Source: https://www.interglobe.com/about-us

InterGlobe Enterprises is a travel conglomerate founded in **1989**, beginning with an Air Transport business. It now spans **Aviation** (IndiGo), **Hospitality** (InterGlobe Hotels, incl. the Miiro international brand), **Logistics** (MOVIN), **AI & Technology** (AIONOS), **Airline Management** (InterGlobe Air Transport), **Advanced Pilot Training** (CAE Simulation Training), and **Aircraft Maintenance Engineering** (School for Aircraft Maintenance Engineering). It operates in **150+ cities across 40+ countries** with **72,000+ employees**.

**Corporate values:** Integrity, Customer Orientation, Future Mindedness.

**ESG initiative:** *"Globe360"* is InterGlobe's overarching ESG programme (Environmental: energy/water management, GHG emissions, green building certification; Social: DEI, health & safety, employee engagement; Governance: ethics/compliance, data privacy) — distinct from, but complementary to, InterGlobe Foundation's community-facing CSR work. Quote (Aditya Pande, Group CEO): "ESG isn't a separate initiative for us; it's the very foundation of how we operate."
